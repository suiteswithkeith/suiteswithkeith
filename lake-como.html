Plan my Lake Como stay

The first Lake Como decision isn’t which hotel. It’s which part of the lake.

Near the town of Como — Cernobbio, Moltrasio and Torno — you’re about an hour from Milan, with quieter villages and easy arrivals. In the middle of the lake — Tremezzo, Cadenabbia and Bellagio — you’re where the lake is at its most beautiful, with the villages and villas a short boat ride away, but the road in is slow.

I stayed at Il Sereno, Villa Serbelloni and the EDITION, and toured Grand Hotel Tremezzo, Passalacqua and Villa d’Este. Booking through me costs the same as booking direct, and my clients typically get a space-available upgrade, daily breakfast and a hotel credit on top — plus the cars and private boats that make Como easy.

Grand Hotel Tremezzo

Art Nouveau grand hotel

Just a fun hotel. Book a lake-view junior suite, spend your afternoons at the garden pool, and know you’ll be sharing it with a crowd.

Like stepping into a Wes Anderson film: tiered gardens with my favorite pool on the property, five restaurants, a clay court and lake-view junior suites facing Bellagio. It’s lively, and outside guests come in numbers.

Perfect for Families, first trips to the lake, couples who like a buzz

Know before you go

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

01
Arrive by boat for the middle of the lake. The road from the town of Como to Tremezzo, Cadenabbia or Bellagio is narrow and takes 45 minutes to an hour. I arrange a private car from Milan to the water in Como, then about 30 minutes by private boat to your hotel’s dock. For Passalacqua, Villa d’Este, the Mandarin Oriental and Il Sereno, a car from Milan, about an hour, is simpler.
02
See Bellagio, but think twice about sleeping there. It’s the lake’s most famous village and very crowded, even in October. Visit by boat and come home somewhere calmer.
03
Book boats through me. Private boats for dinners across the lake, a day on the water or the move between hotels are the best part of Como. I work with the lake’s best captains, at fair prices.
04
Mind the season. Most of the lake’s luxury hotels close for winter. Il Sereno is open mid-March to October 26; Passalacqua stays open through New Year’s. May, June and September are the sweet spot.
05
Floating pools rock. Several hotels have pools floating on the lake, which move with the water. If that bothers you, Il Sereno’s heated pool is on land, and Grand Hotel Tremezzo’s garden pool sits above the lake.
Make it one trip

Two hotels, one lake.

For a honeymoon, I’d split the stay: two or three nights at Passalacqua, then a ten-minute private boat across the lake to Il Sereno. Two completely different stays, and only one travel day.
