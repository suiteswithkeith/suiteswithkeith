A note from Keith
Over the past few months, I spent four weeks island hopping through the Cyclades, and it’s easy to understand why Greece has become one of the world’s most beloved destinations. Traveling solo across eight islands, I never once felt alone. I felt looked after.

After touring and staying at 38 remarkable hotels across the Cyclades, these are the ones I’d recommend to my clients without hesitation.
