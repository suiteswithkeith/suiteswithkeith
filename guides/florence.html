Where I’d stay

One for every kind of Florence trip. These are the hotels I send friends to.

Best overall

Four Seasons Firenze

A Renaissance palazzo inside a private garden that feels impossible this close to the Duomo. It’s the only hotel in Florence that gives you a resort and the city in the same stay, and the service is the most polished in town.

Ask me about this hotel →

Best Ponte Vecchio views

Portrait Firenze

Suites right on the Arno, so the Ponte Vecchio is out your window rather than down the street. The rooms are apartment-sized, which matters more than you’d think after a long museum day. Ask for a river view; that’s the whole point.

Ask me about this hotel →

Best location

The Place

A small townhouse on Piazza Santa Maria Novella, with nearly everything that matters on foot. It feels less like a hotel and more like staying with a friend who has excellent taste. Plan one evening around the rooftop at aperitivo hour.

Ask me about this hotel →

Best city views

Collegio alla Querce

A former school in the hills, about a 30-minute walk from the center, with the whole city laid out below almost every room. I’ve stayed here, so I can tell you exactly which rooms face the Duomo.

Ask me about this hotel →

Best newcomer

Borgo Pignano Florence

The city house from the family behind Borgo Pignano, one of my favorite estates in Tuscany. Same calm, same eye for detail, on a smaller and more personal scale. It pairs beautifully with a few nights at the estate afterward.

Ask me about this hotel →

Best splurge

Villa San Michele

A 15th-century former monastery on the Fiesole hillside, recently renovated, with the best views in Florence. Terraces step down the slope and a shuttle takes you into town. Go spring through fall, when the terraces are the point.

Before you go

Things I’d tell a friend

  • Skip the museums on Monday. The Uffizi and the Accademia are closed.
  • Book the Duomo climb early. Timed slots sell out.
  • Stay at least three nights.
  • See David after hours. I can arrange it.
  • Heading to Tuscany next? Ask me about a private driver to Chianti.
