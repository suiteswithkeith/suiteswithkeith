The Lungarno Collection

The Ferragamo Family’s Hotels · Florence · Rome · Milan

Small, beautifully designed hotels from the family behind Ferragamo, with service that makes you feel like a regular from the first night.

The Lungarno Collection is the Ferragamo family’s hotel group. Most of it sits along the Arno in Florence, the family’s home city, and the Portrait hotels carry the same idea to Rome and Milan: very few rooms, Italian design, and a team that remembers you.

I am working through the collection one hotel at a time, starting with the one I stayed in. Each review below is written from my own visit.

Start here

Portrait Firenze

Florence · I stayed September 2026

37 rooms and suites on the Arno, steps from the Ponte Vecchio. The day before I arrived I broke my camera’s SD card. The team went out and bought me a new one, and had it to me within the hour. That is Portrait.

Best for couples, fans of Italian design, and anyone who wants high-touch service that never feels invasive.
