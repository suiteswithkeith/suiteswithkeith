The question behind the question

Warmth and history, or a statement?

Borgo Santandrea is spectacular and brand new in feel. Santa Caterina is the opposite: it has been here for over a century and it knows exactly what it is doing.

At Santa Caterina, you feel like a guest in someone’s home. The family has run it since 1904, and so many of the team have been there 15 to 20 years or more.

If you want warmth and history over a statement, choose Santa Caterina. If you want something very modern, with a proper beach, choose Borgo Santandrea.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms

At Santa Caterina, every category I book faces the sea head-on: the Special Suites with a private pool, the Full Sea View Garden Suites, the front sea view Junior Suites and the upper-floor Front Sea View Doubles. I stayed in room 45, in a little building just to the left of the main hotel. So private and serene, truly a dream.

At Borgo Santandrea, the rooms are very modern. Think twice before booking one with a private pool: they are not that private, and you can look down on your neighbors, and they can look down on you.

Service

Santa Caterina is still run by the founding family, and so many of the team have been there 15 to 20 years or more. You dial 260 for the front desk, there is no WhatsApp, and reception keeps the calendar in real books with real pens. In a world of constant tech, that face-to-face connection really means something.

At Borgo Santandrea, service is refined but warm, and dinner at Alici is fantastic.

Beaches and the afternoon sun

At Santa Caterina, take the stairs down to the beach club at least once; the gardens on the way feel like a fairy tale. The elevator gives you the coolest view you have ever seen on the way to the beach.

Borgo Santandrea has a beautiful, massive, proper beach. At both, the sun leaves the water early, so in the fall your swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm.

Families and longer stays

For a multigenerational trip, Santa Caterina. Families come back for decades, and Casa Marchesa, about 300 meters away, has a three-bedroom villa that keeps everyone together, and a pool just for its guests. Stay at least five nights.

Borgo Santandrea is for travelers who want a modern, spectacular statement and a real beach. Come in May to June, or September.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Santa Caterina or Borgo Santandrea?

Borgo Santandrea is spectacular and brand new in feel. Santa Caterina is the opposite: it has been here for over a century and it knows exactly what it is doing. If you want warmth and history over a statement, choose Santa Caterina.

Which has the better beach?

Borgo Santandrea: beautiful, massive and a proper beach. Santa Caterina’s beach club is reached through the gardens or by elevator. At both, the water goes dark early in the fall.

Which is better for families?

Santa Caterina. Families come back for generations, and the three-bedroom villa at Casa Marchesa keeps everyone together.

Which room should I book at each?

At Santa Caterina, a front sea view: a Special Suite with its own pool, or room 45, where I stayed. At Borgo Santandrea, ask me first, and think twice about the private pool rooms, which are overlooked.

Can I add Positano?

Yes, and many of my clients do: slow days on the Amalfi side, then a few nights in Positano at Il San Pietro or Villa Treville. I can split the stay.
