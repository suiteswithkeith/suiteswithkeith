Villa FAQ

Villa Diane & St. Barths: the questions I get

What is the best villa in St. Barths?

If you want a private villa with a full hotel behind it, my pick is Villa Diane at Fouquet’s Saint-Barth. It sleeps 10 in five suites, has an infinity pool over Gustavia harbor, and you can walk into town. I’ve vetted the villa and know the team personally.

Where is Villa Diane?

At Fouquet’s Saint-Barth, on the hillside above Gustavia, the island’s harbor town. The harbor, the shops and the restaurants are a walk away, and the villa faces west for the sunset.

How many people does Villa Diane sleep?

Up to 10 guests in five double suites over three floors: a master suite on the upper level, two suites on the main level and two on the lower level. The two lower suites can be set up with twin beds, which is helpful for kids or friends sharing.

What’s included with Villa Diane?

A dedicated butler, 24/7 concierge, private roundtrip transfers from the airport or the port, complimentary sunbeds at the hotel’s Loulou beach restaurant, daily housekeeping with turndown, unpacking on arrival, fresh fruit daily and 24/7 access to the fitness centre. A private chef can be arranged.

Is Villa Diane a private villa rental or part of a hotel?

It’s a private villa within Fouquet’s Saint-Barth, the Barrière group’s hotel in Gustavia. You get your own house and infinity pool, with Beefbar, the Loulou beach restaurant, a Biologique Recherche spa and a full hotel team behind you. I’m very pro villas at hotels versus standalone villa rentals, especially in St. Barths, where staff, restaurant tables and beach sunbeds are hard to come by in high season.

How much does Villa Diane cost?

Rates are on request and depend on the season and the length of stay. Send me your dates and group size and I’ll come back with the exact total from the hotel, taxes included.

When is the best time to visit St. Barths?

December through April is high season, with the driest weather and the island at its liveliest; Christmas and New Year’s are the busiest weeks of the year. Spring is quieter and still beautiful. Tell me what kind of trip you want and I’ll suggest the right dates.

Can I book Villa Diane for Christmas or New Year’s Eve?

Yes, on request. Festive stays start from December 25 with an 8-night minimum, are paid in full at booking and are non-refundable. The villa looks over Gustavia harbor, so you can watch the New Year’s Eve fireworks from your own terrace. Festive availability changes quickly, so message me with your dates.

How do I get to St. Barths?

Most people fly into St. Maarten (SXM), then take a short connecting flight to St. Barths or the ferry to Gustavia. A private charter is the easiest option in high season. Private roundtrip transfers from the St. Barths airport or the port come with the villa, and I can arrange the connection and private jet charter too.

Does booking Villa Diane through you cost more?

No. Rates are identical to booking direct, and my clients receive Fora perks on eligible stays. I’ve vetted Villa Diane and know the Fouquet’s Saint-Barth team personally, so you also get me: dinner reservations, boat days, transfers and the rest of the trip planned before you land.
