The Shape Of It

The island, and where everything sits

Paros is roughly a circle with a mountain in the middle. Naousa and the best beaches sit around the north, Parikia and the ferries on the west, the hill villages climb the slopes inland, and Antiparos — with the clearest water in the Cyclades — hangs just off the southwest corner. Tap any pin for what it is.

A car, for the whole stay

The one island where I say rent one for every day you’re there. Roads are good, parking is easy outside the two towns, and the best beaches sit at the end of short dirt tracks.

Arriving by ferry

Everything docks at Parikia — about three hours from Piraeus direct, or forty minutes from Mykonos and Santorini. Naousa is twenty minutes across the island from the port.

Arriving by air

Forty minutes from Athens into a small, calm airport in the south. Flights sell out well before the ferries do in season, so this is the first thing I lock in.

Hopping to Antiparos

The little car ferry shuttles from Pounda every half hour and takes ten minutes. No booking, no drama — drive on, drive off, and you’re on a different island for lunch.

Bookable · One

On the water

The water on the Antiparos side of Paros is the clearest I know in the Cyclades, and the only way to reach the best of it is by boat. Both sails below run out of Piso Livadi on the east coast — a working fishing harbor twenty minutes from Naousa, with none of the marina scrum you get on the bigger islands.

Full day · 8 hours · Book ahead

Sail to Antiparos & Despotiko

The one I’d protect on Paros. Out of Piso Livadi under sail at 9:30, down into the Antiparos Strait, anchored in the Blue Lagoon, then right around South Antiparos with a full lunch cooked and served on deck. A last swim off South Paros on the way home. Antiparos is a genuinely unusual little island and the water on that side is the best on Paros.

Can be fully private for the two of you, or shared with up to ten — your call.

From $1,583 for the boat, privately

Evening · 3.5 hours · Book ahead

Evening sail and dinner on deck

The shorter version, and lovely on its own night. Sails up at 18:00 from Piso Livadi toward Naxos or the south coast, a drink in hand, a swim in a sheltered bay, then dinner served on deck as the sun drops. Back at the dock by dusk.

From $110 per person

Half day · No booking needed

Antiparos, the easy way

If the sail isn’t your day, take the ten-minute car ferry from Pounda instead. One long main street of galleries and good tavernas, the cave in the hills above, and Soros beach on the way back — a different island for the cost of a coffee.

Bookable · Two

Villages, beaches and slow days

This is why Paros over the bigger names: real villages with real life in them, and beaches you choose by mood rather than by whichever one your hotel happens to sit on. None of these need more than the car and a loose sense of when lunch should be.

Evening

Naousa harbor after dark

The heart of Paros once the sun goes down. Browse the boutiques, wander the little Venetian harbor, then a long dinner at Siparos with the waterfront coming alive around you.

Full day

Lefkes, Aliki, and a swim

Morning in the mountain village of Lefkes — marble lanes, no sea, no crowds — then south to the fishing village of Aliki for a seafood lunch at To Balconi tou Aki, and the clear water at Lageri in the afternoon.

Half day

Kolymbithres

Wind-carved granite coves and water you can see straight through. Unlike any other beach in the Cyclades, and a short hop across the bay from Naousa — go early, before the boats arrive.

Full day

A day at Crios Beach Club

The island’s most relaxed beachfront setting for swimming and a slow lunch. Nothing to do but stay put until the light changes — the day that asks nothing of you at all.

Evening · Book ahead

Dinner up in the hills

Santa Pacou back in Lefkes, or Parostia at Cosme — one of my favorite restaurants on the island and the right way to close out Paros. Both are tables I’d lock in before you land.

Afternoon

The north-coast beaches

Lageri for calm, shallow water and almost nobody on it; Santa Maria when you want loungers and a bar. Both face Naxos across the channel, and both are ten minutes from Naousa.

How I’d spend the days

Four nights means all three of these days fit, with room for a sailing day on top.

Day One · Arrive

Ease into Paros with an afternoon at your hotel before heading into Naousa as the sun begins to set. Browse the boutiques, wander the harbor, and settle in for a leisurely dinner at Siparos while the waterfront comes alive after dark.

Dinner: Siparos

Day Two · Discover

Start the morning exploring the mountain village of Lefkes, then continue south to Aliki for lunch at To Balconi tou Aki, one of my favorite seaside tavernas on the island. Spend the afternoon swimming in the crystal-clear waters of Lageri Beach before returning to Lefkes for dinner at Santa Pacou.

Don’t miss: sunset wandering the marble streets of Lefkes.

Day Three · Unwind

Ease into the morning before spending the day at Crios Beach Club, where you’ll find one of the island’s most relaxed beachfront settings for swimming, lunch, and an afternoon by the sea. End your stay with dinner at Parostia — it’s downstairs at Cosme, one of my favorite restaurants on the island, and the perfect way to close out Paros without going anywhere.

Dinner: Parostia, at Cosme

The Tables

Where I’d eat

Naousa · Best atmosphere

Sigi Ikthios

Tucked into the old harbor with the fishing boats almost at your table. The evening there is the point as much as the fish.

Santa Maria, outside Naousa · Best seafood

Siparos

On the water looking across at Naxos, and the seafood benchmark on the island. The long first-night dinner that sets the tone for the trip.

Naousa · Best food

Yemeni

The best cooking on Paros, plate for plate — modern Greek in the lanes behind the harbor, without a view and better for it.

Naousa · Best vibe

Stilvi

Where I’d send you when you want the night to keep going — good plates, better energy, and no rush to give the table back.

Lefkes · Most unique setting

Santa Pacou

Dinner up in the marble village, a world away from the harbor scene. Time it after the sunset walk through Lefkes and it’s the most memorable evening on the island.

Aliki · Best lunch

To Balconi tou Aki

A seaside taverna down on the south coast and one of my favorites on the island. The lunch I’d build the Lefkes day around — go after the village in the morning, before the beach in the afternoon.

At Cosme, Naousa · Best last night

Parostia

Downstairs at Cosme, and one of my favorite restaurants on Paros. The right way to close out the trip — and if you’re staying there, an evening with nowhere to drive afterward.
