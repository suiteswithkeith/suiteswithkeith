Questions

Asked often, answered straight

What types of trips do you specialize in?+

Europe first — plus honeymoons, family and multi-generational trips, milestone celebrations, and escapes to Napa Valley, Mexico and beyond.

Do you charge for hotel bookings?+

No. Recommendations and bookings are complimentary, and often carry preferred-partner amenities on top.

Why book through you instead of Amex Fine Hotels + Resorts or Chase?+

The perks are similar. The experience isn’t. The rate is the one you’d pay booking direct — sometimes better — and through my relationships with the hotels, your stay is personalized from before you arrive until you check out.

I have an Amex FHR or Chase credit — can I still use it with you?+

Yes — the best play is usually to book one night through Amex or Chase FHR and the remaining nights through me. You typically keep the benefits of both programs, plus my advocacy with the hotel, which the card programs don’t offer.

What if I already have travel booked?+

In many cases I can take over the reservation as your advisor, advocate on your behalf, and add any available preferred-partner amenities to a stay you already have.

How far in advance should I inquire?+

Three to six months before departure is ideal. Shorter works — if you’re traveling within the next 30 days, you’ll hear from me the same day.

Can you help me use my Amex, Chase, Citi or Bilt points?+

Yes — I consult on points so you get the best value from them, including booking international business and first-class award flights.

Do you handle transfers?+

Yes — door-to-door transfers are part of every itinerary I plan: private helicopter charters through my partnership with Hoper, private boats between islands like Sifnos, Paros and Milos, luxury airport transfers, and chauffeured cars across Greece, Italy, France and beyond.

Do you handle excursions?+

Absolutely. Private guides and drivers, chef’s tables, after-hours museum tours, wine tastings, a private boat day on Lake Como, a high-touch shopping day in Paris — if you can imagine it, I’ll build it into the trip.

Can you arrange private yacht charters or private jets?+

Yes, through trusted charter brokers: yacht charters from single-day boats to fully crewed multi-day itineraries in the Greek islands, the Amalfi Coast and the Caribbean, and private jet charter for domestic and international routes. Tell me the route, dates and group size and I’ll source vetted options.

Do you offer a yearly retainer?+

For the right clients, yes — a limited number of annual arrangements: one flat yearly fee for unlimited planning access, covering hotels, full itineraries, transfers, points strategy, and support while you’re traveling.

Do you book private homes and villas?+

Yes. Through my partner network I have access to a hand-selected list of private homes and villas, each vetted for quality and service. They come with added benefits and, in most cases, no end-of-stay cleaning fee.
