The best luxury beachfront hotels in Greece

Most of the famous hotels in Greece look at the sea from a cliff. These are the ones where you can walk into it.

Updated November 2026

Here is the thing nobody tells you about Greek luxury hotels: most of them are not on a beach.

Santorini’s caldera hotels hang off a cliff. Much of Mykonos is perched high above the sea. Those views are the point, and I love many of those hotels. But if what you actually want is sand, a sunbed and a swim before breakfast, the list gets much shorter.

These are the hotels on my site that sit on the water or have their own beach. I’ve said plainly which ones I’ve stayed at, which I’ve toured and which I know through the team and my clients.

The list

Where the sea is at your feet

01 · Mykonos · Agios Ioannis

Bill & Coo Coast

The best beach hotel in Greece

Most luxury hotels on Mykonos ask you to admire the sea from above. Bill & Coo Coast lets you live beside it. The adults-only suites sit directly on a protected, sunset-facing bay, so you get less of the meltemi wind than elsewhere on the island. There’s a private beach club, with Beefbar for lunch, and it’s #06 in my Greece Top 10. Given the choice, I’d always pick the Coast over Bill & Coo’s Mykonos Town property.

Right for
Couples, beach-club lovers. Adults only, 17 and up.
Room I’d book
The Coast Beachfront Suite with Plunge Pool.From about $900 a night.

Read the full review

Keith’s notes

Directly on the sand. Rooms sit right on the water, not above it.

Sunset-facing. Agios Ioannis looks west, and the bay is sheltered from the wind.

Families, look elsewhere. It’s adults only. I point families to Cali or the Four Seasons.

The verdict

If I had the choice, I’d always choose the Coast location. It’s the beach hotel I’d book first in Greece.

02 · Paros · Naousa

Cosme

Paros’ best beach stay, and I stayed

Cosme is beachfront, with a private beach club directly in front of the hotel: no shuttles, no fight for sunbeds. It’s laid-back luxury, and genuinely refreshing. Between the pool, the beach club and the dining, you could stay four nights without getting bored, which is rare on the islands. It’s #09 in my Greece Top 10.

Right for
Couples, families with teens, four-night stays
Room I’d book
A base room, honestly.They’re enormous. From about $950 a night. For more privacy, the Enastro.

Read the full review

Keith’s notes

Skip the plunge-pool rooms over the shared pool. You lose the privacy you’re paying for.

Go in May or September to October. Rates drop dramatically.

Paros is not “the new Mykonos.” If you’re chasing the Mykonos scene, this isn’t it.

The verdict

Paros’ best beach stay. The beach club out front is the reason to book it.

03 · Mykonos · Kalo Livadi

Four Seasons Hotel Mykonos

The family pick, with a private beach below

New for 2026, on about 15 acres of hillside above Kalo Livadi Bay on the south coast, with its own private beach below. To be precise, you walk down to the sand rather than step onto it. It has two infinity pools and a kids club, and it’s about 20 minutes from Mykonos Town. I know the team well and have sent them clients, and it’s already one of the hotels I recommend most on the island.

Right for
Families, couples, travelers who want resort calm
Room I’d book

Keith’s notes

Families, start here. The kids club and the beach make it the easiest Mykonos stay with children.

The rooms are the quiet win. About 500 square feet, sea-facing, and big enough for a family of three.

Away from town. Twenty minutes is the trade for a beach and quiet.

The verdict

Already one of the hotels I recommend most on Mykonos, and the one I’d book for a family that wants a beach.

04 · Athens Riviera · Vouliagmeni

Four Seasons Astir Palace

The beach resort with a city attached

On a private peninsula overlooking the Saronic Gulf, with multiple beaches and private coves. Astir Palace feels like two vacations in one: a real beach resort, and the Acropolis within reach. I’ve walked the peninsula myself. The private coves deserve more than a quick swim, and Pelagos is one of the best Michelin-starred meals in Greece.

Right for
Families, beach lovers, celebrations, longer stays
Room I’d book
A Sea View Bungalow Suite.Around $1,300 a night through me.

Read the full review

Keith’s notes

Start or end your Greece trip here. Few hotels balance city and beach this easily.

Book dinner at Pelagos. One of the best Michelin-starred meals in Greece.

Museums and rooftops first? Then stay in town at The Dolli instead.

The verdict

One of Europe’s finest urban resorts. The best way to turn Athens into a beach stay.

05 · Crete · Mirabello Bay

Minos Palace

The value pick, on its own peninsula

Minos Palace sits on its own small peninsula between Agios Nikolaos and Elounda, with water on three sides and a private sandy beach tucked into the coves. All 148 rooms face the sea. It’s a piece of geography no new hotel will ever get again. I haven’t been to Crete yet. It’s here on the strength of its setting.

Right for
Couples and quiet-seekers. Adults only.
Room I’d book
A sunset-side or private-pool suite.About $400 a night in September.

Keith’s notes

The best value here. Sea-facing rooms at a fraction of Mykonos prices.

Adults only. For families on this bay, look at its sister hotel, Minos Beach Art Hotel.

The verdict

Quiet, surrounded by water, and the best value on my Crete shortlist.

06 · Crete · Agios Nikolaos

Minos Beach Art Hotel

Bungalows twenty steps from the sea

You walk into the sea from your room. Its 125 bungalows, villas and suites are none of them more than about twenty steps from the water. It opened in 1963 and is older in the best sense. One honest caveat: swimming is off rocks and small coves. Its two beaches are private but modest, so if you need a long sandy run, this isn’t that coast.

Right for
Families, travelers who want to live on the water
Room I’d book
A waterfront or seafront bungalow.About $650 a night in September.

Read the full review

Keith’s notes

My family pick for eastern Crete. Bungalows, space and the sea at your door.

Rocks, not sand. If a long sandy beach is the point, look at its sister, Minos Palace, ten minutes away.

The verdict

Not a sandy beach, but as close to living on the water as any hotel in Greece.

Worth knowing

Close, but not quite

These come up often when people ask about beach hotels in Greece. They’re wonderful, but they’re not beachfront in the way the six above are, or I haven’t seen them yet.

One&Only Aesthesis, Athens Riviera. A true beach resort within striking distance of the Acropolis, and the beach-scene alternative to Astir Palace. Book cabanas ahead. I haven’t inspected it yet.

One&Only Kea Island. Sixty-three villas stepped down a cliff above Vroskopos Bay, with Bond Beach Club at the waterline. It’s a cliffside resort with a beach club, not a beach hotel, and it closes for winter.

The Rooster, Antiparos. Sixteen houses above Livadia beach, which sits just below the property. The most soulful stay in the Cyclades.

Rosewood Blue Palace, Crete. On the hillside at Elounda, with a private beach and three sea-view pools. It opens in spring 2027, and I plan to see it in May.

Santorini. You won’t find it on this list. The caldera hotels are built into the cliff, and that view is why you go. If you want both, pair Santorini with a beach island. My island pairings show how.

Before you ask

The questions I get

What is the best beachfront hotel in Greece?

Bill & Coo Coast on Mykonos, for couples. Its adults-only suites sit directly on Agios Ioannis Bay. For families, Four Seasons Mykonos, with its private beach and kids club.

Are there beachfront hotels in Santorini?

Not at the luxury level I recommend. Santorini’s best hotels are on the caldera cliffs, and the view is the reason to go. Pair it with a beach island such as Mykonos or Paros.

Which Greek beach hotel is best for families?

Four Seasons Mykonos, with a private beach, two infinity pools and a kids club. On the Athens Riviera, Astir Palace. On Crete, Minos Beach Art Hotel.

What is the best beach hotel on Paros?

Cosme in Naousa, with a private beach club directly in front of the hotel. I’ve stayed there.

Is there a beach hotel near Athens?

Yes. Four Seasons Astir Palace sits on a private peninsula on the Athens Riviera with multiple beaches, and One&Only Aesthesis is the beach-scene alternative.

When is the best time for a beach trip to Greece?

Shoulder season is good value. On Paros I like May and September to October, when rates drop dramatically. Note that some resorts, such as One&Only Kea, close for winter.
