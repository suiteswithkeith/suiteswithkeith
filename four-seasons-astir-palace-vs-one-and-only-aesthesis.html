Head-to-head · The Athens Riviera

Astir Palace or Aesthesis?

The Athens Riviera has become a luxury destination in its own right, thirty minutes from the Acropolis — and it now has two flags worth choosing between: the Four Seasons peninsula, or the One&Only beach scene. Here’s how I match clients to each.

Both on the Athens Riviera · Astir toured in person, from $1,300 a night through me · both bookable with my perks

Choose Astir Palace

If you want two vacations in one

A private peninsula overlooking the Saronic Gulf — multiple beaches, private coves, Astir Marina, and Pelagos, one of the best Michelin-starred meals in Greece, all thirty minutes from the Acropolis. The hotel I build day two of my Athens itinerary around.

Choose Aesthesis

If the beach day is the point

The Riviera coast south of the city, and home to the Mãnko Beach cabana day I write into my sample honeymoon itinerary — a One&Only beach afternoon within reach of the Acropolis.

Athens deserves two full days minimum — don’t treat the Riviera as a layover. These two aren’t mutually exclusive either: I book them together as a full Riviera stretch before the islands. At Astir, the Sea View Bungalow Suite is the room I’d book; rates start around $1,300 a night through me.

The question behind the question

Choose your Riviera flag deliberately.

Astir Palace is the peninsula resort I’ve walked myself, and few hotels balance city and beach this effortlessly — mornings by the sea, afternoons at the Acropolis, dinner over the Saronic Gulf. For families, celebrations, and longer stays, it’s the anchor.

Aesthesis is the beach-scene alternative — the reason it’s on my list is that exact Mãnko cabana day, booked as its own stay when the trip calls for it. Tell me your Athens chapter — city days, beach days, or both — and I’ll match you to the right flag, or split the stay and give you the whole Riviera.
