The question behind the question

Do you want the most luxurious hotel, or the best location?

Aman Rosa Alpina is the most luxurious hotel in the Dolomites, in a fantastic spot for skiing and hiking, and as good for families as it is for couples. The suites are the star. The views are more intimate than dramatic.

COMO Alpina’s huge selling point is location: you walk out of your room onto the trails in summer and the slopes in winter. It is not quite the hotel Aman is, but the price reflects that, and the spa and pool hold their own against all of them.

If the hotel itself matters most, pick Aman. If you want to ski from your door, or you want one of the best locations in the region for less, pick COMO Alpina. For a ski trip, it is my first choice in the Dolomites.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Skiing and hiking

COMO Alpina is the only true ski-in, ski-out hotel of the five I saw, the best on my list by a distance. In summer the trails start at your door, and it ties with Gardena Grödnerhof as my best summer location. The Alpe di Siusi is a protected area, so cars need a permit; the hotel handles it, and a private transfer avoids the question.

Aman Rosa Alpina is not ski-in, ski-out, but there is a ski room and the lifts are about two minutes away by shuttle, and the location is fantastic for hiking too. One thing to know: San Cassiano was a ghost town in October. Come in winter or summer, when it is buzzing.

Views and rooms

The suites at Aman Rosa Alpina are the reason to come, with beautiful views of the forest, the village and the mountains above. Those views are intimate; if a sweeping panorama is the point, Aman itself is not the place.

COMO Alpina has the big views: rolling meadows with mountains towering all around. The rooms are spacious, with beautiful decks, large bathrooms and tubs. Mind the chairlift right outside the Mountain View rooms, which can feel a little loud and not very private in the shoulder season. Tell me how you are traveling and I will steer you to the right room.

Spa and wellness

Aman Rosa Alpina has a lovely spa, and its gym ties with Gardena Grödnerhof’s for the best in the Dolomites.

COMO Alpina leans into wellness, like most COMO properties, and the spa and pool stack up very nicely against every hotel I saw. Expect German and Austrian sauna culture at both: the main saunas are mixed, and swimsuits come off.

Service and polish

Service in the lobby restaurant at Aman Rosa Alpina was fantastic. Two small things surprised me: some of the original walls of the former hotel show their age, and our arrival was a little awkward. Everything after was excellent. It is still my pick for the best luxury property in the region.

At COMO Alpina, dining was solid, and we had a very nice dinner in the formal restaurant, where our servers were great. Service elsewhere was good, if a little awkward at times. You trade some polish for one of the best locations in the Dolomites at a lower price.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Aman Rosa Alpina or COMO Alpina?

It depends on the trip. Aman Rosa Alpina is the most luxurious hotel in the Dolomites and my pick for the best luxury property. COMO Alpina has the better location if you want to ski or hike from your door, and it costs less.

Which is better for skiing?

COMO Alpina. It is the only true ski-in, ski-out hotel I saw, and my first choice for a ski trip. Aman Rosa Alpina has a ski room and and the lifts about two minutes away by shuttle.

Which is better for families?

Both. Aman Rosa Alpina is as good for families as it is for couples, and COMO Alpina is also one of my picks for families, alongside Gardena Grödnerhof.

Which has the better views?

COMO Alpina, with the meadows and the peaks all around. The views at Aman Rosa Alpina are beautiful but intimate: the forest, the village and the mountains above.

Which room should I book at each?

At Aman Rosa Alpina, a suite. They are the reason to stay. At COMO Alpina, tell me how you are traveling first; in the shoulder season I would avoid the Mountain View rooms, because of the chairlift right outside.

When should I go?

For Aman Rosa Alpina, winter or summer, when San Cassiano has life in it. The hotel told me there is a seven-night minimum in winter, but sample searches don’t show it, so I confirm the rules first. COMO Alpina is my first choice for winter and ties for summer.
