The question behind the question

Do you want the best base, or a stop with character?

Gardena Grödnerhof was a close second to Forestis for me. It sits right in Ortisei, a genuinely charming town with great restaurants, with lifts to two completely different hiking areas, an incredible spa and warm, polished service. It is the most well-rounded hotel I saw in the region.

Miramonti is a nice, unique property with an iconic pool, close to Merano. It isn’t at the level of the region’s top hotels as a luxury experience, but it is a great hotel for someone who values personality, the pool and the views over polish.

If you are coming to hike, eat out and see the famous peaks, pick Gardena. If the pool and the views are the point, pick Miramonti. Ortisei to Merano is about an hour by car, so many trips can have both.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Where you are

Gardena Grödnerhof sits right in Ortisei. Take the Seceda lift and you are in the jagged high country of Val Gardena; take the gondola to the Alpe di Siusi and you are in rolling meadows. Avoid July and August if you want quiet; spring, early summer, September and October are when it shines.

Miramonti is above Merano, a spa town with a gentler climate and big views, outside the main Dolomites valleys. For hiking among the famous peaks, I would base you in Ortisei, which is exactly where Gardena is.

Rooms

At Gardena Grödnerhof, everything from Superior Deluxe up has been beautifully redone, and the original building was restored so well you’d never guess its age. It is my pick for the best design in the Dolomites. The Superior and Comfort rooms keep the original charm, but the renovated rooms are what I book.

At Miramonti, every room is designed differently, and the whole place is a little quirky. Because no two are alike, the right room matters, and I match one to you.

Pools, spa and gym

Gardena Grödnerhof has an incredible spa, an indoor pool, an outdoor pool looking over the rooftops of Ortisei, and a gym that ties with Aman Rosa Alpina’s for the best in the Dolomites.

Miramonti’s infinity pool is the best outdoor pool of any hotel I saw in the region, and it is the reason people come. Expect German and Austrian sauna culture at both: the main saunas are mixed, and swimsuits come off.

Service, food and families

Service at Gardena Grödnerhof was warm, polished and consistent across the whole stay, and Ortisei is the best town in the region for dining off property. It works for couples and families alike, and in winter the skiing is all around you.

Miramonti is character over polish. It isn’t a top-tier luxury experience, and it is best suited to couples and design lovers who want the pool and the views.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Gardena Grödnerhof or Miramonti?

For me, Gardena Grödnerhof. It was a close second to Forestis on my trip, and it is the best base in the Dolomites. Miramonti is a great stay for the right traveler: someone who wants the pool, the views and a hotel with personality.

Which is better for hiking?

Gardena Grödnerhof. Lifts leave from Ortisei to Seceda and to the Alpe di Siusi, two completely different landscapes. Miramonti is above Merano, outside the main Dolomites valleys.

Which has the better pool?

Miramonti, for the outdoor pool; it is the best of any hotel I saw in the region. Gardena Grödnerhof has an incredible spa with indoor and outdoor pools.

Which is better for families?

Gardena Grödnerhof. It works for couples and families alike, and it made my list of the best Dolomites hotels for families. Miramonti is best suited to couples and design lovers.

Which room should I book at each?

At Gardena Grödnerhof, a Superior Deluxe or above; those are the renovated rooms. At Miramonti, every room is different, so I match one to you.

Can I do both?

Yes. Ortisei to Merano is about an hour by car, and Miramonti is an easy add-on at either end of a trip built around Gardena Grödnerhof. I arrange the drivers.
