Head-to-head · Oia’s marquee names

Katikies or Canaves Oia Suites?

Two of the biggest names in Oia — one holding the island’s most photographed real estate on classic interiors, the other completely renovated into the best hotel I saw for first-time visitors. Here’s where each one stands today.

#10 in my Santorini ranking vs #03 in my Greece Top 10 · both toured in person

Choose Katikies

If the legend is the draw

The storied real estate itself: three Caldera properties whose terraced white architecture made half of Santorini’s postcards, with the expansive Pelagos House spa — and enormous upside the day the renovation comes.

Choose Canaves Oia Suites

If you want the caldera, perfected

The best hotel I saw for first-time visitors to Santorini — completely renovated, privacy engineered into the site, and home to the River Pool Suite, the single best suite I saw across all 38 hotels in Greece.

Complete privacy doesn’t exist anywhere on the caldera — Canaves just does a particularly good job with it. And if the River Pool Suite is available for your dates, stop deliberating.

The question behind the question

Today, this one isn’t close — but it isn’t forever.

Right now, Canaves Oia Suites is the classic Santorini caldera experience, perfected — I’d be hard pressed to recommend any other hotel first to someone who wants exactly that. The renovation, the privacy, and the spa put it a clear tier above, and the rate gap between the two is roughly the $100–$200 a night I’d call the best-spent money on the island.

But Katikies holds locations that could compete with anything on Santorini, and the moment a refresh lands I’ll be first in line to re-review. Until then: loyalists and points-of-view aside, my booking advice follows the ranking. Tell me your dates and I’ll price both.
