The question behind the question

Do you want a hotel where you can be yourself, or one that does tradition properly?

La Minerva is the hotel where I felt I could be myself. I shared a great laugh with the woman who checked me in, and the restaurant upstairs, the bar downstairs and the lobby all had a real buzz.

The Hassler is a very traditional luxury hotel right at the top of the Spanish Steps, and that is the appeal. The Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof is a highlight, and the courtyard restaurant is a calmer place to eat than the streets outside.

If you are younger and want a real Roman neighborhood, pick La Minerva. If you love classic grand hotels and want to be at the heart of the action, pick the Hassler.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Views

At the Hassler, you are right above the Spanish Steps, and some of the bathrooms look over the rooftops to St. Peter’s. You cannot get much more central.

The views around La Minerva are more authentic Rome than pristine Rome: neighborhood rooftops and lived-in streets. It is a hotel in a real part of the city, and I appreciated that compared with a lot of its competitors.

Dinner upstairs

Both hotels save the best for the top floor. The Hassler has a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof, with the rooftops of Rome laid out below you.

La Minerva’s rooftop restaurant is where I would spend the evening, looking over the domes of the centro storico, before heading down to the bar.

Style

The Hassler is classic luxury for travelers who want Rome’s grand-hotel style: canopy beds, damask walls and crystal lamps.

La Minerva is an Orient Express palazzo by the Pantheon, and its personality lives in the shared spaces and the people. The best slippers of my whole trip were here. I kept them.

Neighborhood and crowds

The Steps draw crowds to the Hassler from morning to night. If you want quiet, the Eden is a few minutes away on a calm residential street.

La Minerva puts you by the Pantheon, in the city as Romans actually live it. La Dolce Vita journeys start in Rome, so it is where I put clients before the train.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, La Minerva or the Hassler?

It depends on what you want from Rome. La Minerva for warmth, personality and a real neighborhood. The Hassler for very traditional luxury right at the top of the Spanish Steps.

Which is better for younger travelers?

La Minerva, for the warmest shared spaces and the friendliest team I met in Rome.

Which has the better restaurant?

The Hassler has the Michelin star, on the roof, plus a beautiful courtyard restaurant. La Minerva’s rooftop restaurant is where I would spend a relaxed evening.

Does the Hassler have a spa?

Not yet. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027. Until then, look at the Bulgari or the Corinthia if a spa matters.

Where should I stay before La Dolce Vita?

La Minerva. La Dolce Vita journeys start in Rome, and I put clients there first.
