Head-to-head · Lake Como

Villa d’Este or Villa Serbelloni?

Two of Lake Como’s grand old hotels, and the decision is really about the setting. Villa d’Este is a self-contained Renaissance estate in Cernobbio. Grand Hotel Villa Serbelloni sits at the water’s edge in Bellagio, so you walk out the door into the most famous village on the lake.

Toured Villa d’Este · stayed at Villa Serbelloni · October 2026

Choose Villa d’Este

If you want an estate to yourself

A 16th-century villa in a 25-acre park, with the lake’s original floating pool and century-old clay courts. You may never leave the grounds, and it’s about an hour from Milan.

Choose Villa Serbelloni

If you want Bellagio on your doorstep

A family-run grand hotel at the tip of Bellagio, with frescoed salons, a lakefront pool and the village’s lanes, shops and ferries a few steps away. It’s the classic Bellagio stay.

The question behind the question

A park of your own, or the village outside your door?

Villa d’Este is for travelers who want the hotel to be the trip. The estate is huge and self-contained, with the park, the floating pool and the courts, and it’s the lake’s most famous address. It’s also the easier of the two to reach from Milan.

Villa Serbelloni is for travelers who want to be in the middle of things. Bellagio is the lake’s best-known village, and the hotel puts you right in it, with the ferries to Varenna and Tremezzo leaving a few minutes’ walk away.

One thing to know about Bellagio: in summer it fills with day-trippers. The hotel is your retreat from that, but if you want real peace and quiet, Villa d’Este’s park is the calmer setting.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location changes everything

Cernobbio sits at the southern tip of the lake, ten minutes from the town of Como. Bellagio sits at the point where the lake’s three branches meet, in the middle of everything. If you plan to explore by boat, Bellagio is the better base. If you want to arrive quickly and settle into one place, Cernobbio is.

Arriving in Bellagio by boat

The road to Bellagio is slow, 45 minutes to an hour from Como. I usually arrange a car from Milan to the water in Como and then a private boat of about 30 minutes to the hotel. It’s the better way to arrive on Lake Como.

Which is more formal?

Both are classic grand hotels. Villa d’Este is the more formal, and the dress code at dinner is part of the experience. Villa Serbelloni feels more relaxed because the village is right outside.

Doing both in one trip

They’re different enough to combine. A couple of nights at Villa d’Este after landing from Milan, then a boat up the lake to Bellagio, gives you both sides of Lake Como: the grand estate and the village.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Villa d'Este or Villa Serbelloni?

Villa d’Este is better if you want a self-contained estate and the lake’s most famous hotel, close to Milan. Villa Serbelloni is better if you want to stay in Bellagio and explore the lake by ferry and boat.

Is Villa Serbelloni in Bellagio?

Yes. Grand Hotel Villa Serbelloni is at the water’s edge in Bellagio, a short walk from the village and the ferry landing.

Which is closer to Milan?

Villa d’Este, in Cernobbio, about an hour by car. Bellagio is about an hour and a half by road, or a car to Como plus about 30 minutes by private boat.

Is Bellagio too busy in summer?

The village gets crowded with day-trippers in high season. Staying at the hotel means you have a retreat, and early mornings and evenings in Bellagio are lovely. If quiet matters most, consider Villa d’Este or a hotel on a quieter shore.

Does Villa d'Este have a floating pool?

Yes. Its pool floating on the lake is the original, and the most famous on Lake Como.
