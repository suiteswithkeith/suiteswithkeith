Bulgari or Corinthia?

Space is rare in central Rome, and these are the two exceptions I found. Both have a spa. The difference is the mood. Here is how I would choose.

Visited both · October 2026

Choose Bulgari

If you want energy and great dining

Spectacular. Base rooms the size of junior suites elsewhere, marble and mosaics, a lively, sophisticated scene, more bars and restaurants than I could count, and the biggest gym in Rome.

Choose Corinthia

The question behind the question

Both give you space. Do you want a buzz with it, or quiet?

The Bulgari is lively, almost like an EDITION but much more sophisticated, with more places to eat and drink than I could count. If you want quiet and calm, it is not the one.

The Corinthia is across the street from parliament, so the area is incredibly safe, and quiet while still being in the middle of everything. I was blown away. I think it is truly a game changer for Rome.

If you want to stay in for dinner, drinks and a workout, pick the Bulgari. If you want a calm base and a beautiful spa, pick the Corinthia.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms and space

The Bulgari’s base rooms are what other hotels would sell as a deluxe room or a junior suite, so the entry rate already buys real space. You often don’t need to upgrade.

The Corinthia’s rooms are large and tastefully appointed, and some bedrooms open onto terraces. It is new, so room categories and views vary. Book with someone who knows it.

Spa and gym

If you train on vacation, the Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome, massive and with serious equipment, plus a spa.

The Corinthia’s spa is beautiful, as are its public spaces, which are big enough that the hotel never feels crowded.

Eating and drinking

At the Bulgari, there is a beautiful rooftop bar and pizza restaurant, another bar on the floor below it, and more. Downstairs there’s a classic Roman trattoria with fair prices, like €18 pastas. I loved that.

At the Corinthia, the draw is the renovation and the calm. Being central and quiet means the city’s restaurants are a short walk away.

Design

The Corinthia’s renovation is a showstopper from the moment you walk in, down to the onyx and marble bathrooms.

The Bulgari is very modern, with lots of marble and incredible mosaics in the bathrooms. It sits right next to the Mausoleum of Augustus, which is almost done with its renovation.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Bulgari or the Corinthia?

Both give you space, which is rare in central Rome. The Bulgari for energy, great dining in the hotel and the biggest gym in Rome. The Corinthia for quiet, a beautiful spa and a showstopper renovation.

Which is quieter?

The Corinthia, across the street from parliament. The Bulgari is on the lively side, and you step out into busy streets.

Which has the bigger rooms?

Both are among the few central Rome hotels where space is the story. At the Bulgari, the entry rooms are the value. At the Corinthia, the rooms are large, and I’ll steer you to the right category.

Which has the better spa and gym?

For a gym, the Bulgari: the biggest I saw in Rome. For a beautiful spa in a calm hotel, the Corinthia.

Which is better for a couple wanting quiet?

The Corinthia. If you want quiet and calm, the Bulgari is not the one I’d pick. The Eden, my top pick in Rome, is the other quiet option.
