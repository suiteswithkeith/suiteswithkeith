The judgment

Cosme or Parīlio?

Paros’ two best hotels are ten minutes apart and nothing alike. No hotel will answer this question. I will — it takes about thirty seconds.

Choose Cosme

If you want to walk to dinner

A true beach out front, Naousa on foot, and entry rooms bigger than most hotels’ junior suites. You won’t think about a car once.

 Choose Parīlio

If you want to be alone

Architecture, quiet, and a private pool you’ll actually spend the afternoon in. For most honeymoons, this is the one.
