Head-to-head · The Andronis question

Andronis Boutique or Luxury Suites?

Same portfolio, same Oia cliffside, two very different trips. The flagship reads refined, old-school luxury; Boutique is the authentic cave-style Santorini people fly in for. I toured both back to back — here’s how I split them.

#02 and #04 on my Santorini ranking · same portfolio · both toured

Choose Boutique

If you want Santorini to look like Santorini

Real cave-style rooms, recently renovated, with a plunge pool or jacuzzi in every one. It skews toward younger honeymooners, and rates tend to land below the flagship — while giving up very little.

Choose Luxury Suites

If refined and looked-after is the point

The flagship: elegant, more exclusive on paper, all 39 suites and villas with a water feature, and home to Lycabettus — the island’s most famous cliffside table. Also the portfolio’s best choice when mobility is a factor.

The question behind the question

Boutique for the honeymoon; the flagship for the anniversary.

Luxury Suites lends itself perfectly to a 50+ clientele on anniversary trips — or couples returning to Santorini for the first time since their honeymoon. It’s equally beautiful, just more old-school luxury than authentically Santorini, and that’s exactly why it works for that trip. It’s also my pick in the portfolio when mobility is a concern.

For everyone else: Boutique’s cave-style rooms are the real thing, you give up very little versus the flagship, and you usually pay less doing it.
