The question behind the question

Do you want a grand hotel to retreat to, or a neighborhood to live in?

The Corinthia is a retreat in the middle of the city. It is across the street from parliament, so it is quiet and incredibly safe, and the rooms are large, which is rare in central Rome.

Casa Monti is about the neighborhood. There are authentic trattorias all around, and the hotel itself is hip, young and fun, for anyone who wants something other than the classic luxury hotels.

If you want space, quiet and a beautiful spa, pick the Corinthia. If you are a younger couple who wants personality and a real Roman neighborhood, pick Casa Monti.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Neighborhood

Casa Monti is on Via Panisperna in Monti, and the neighborhood is the real thing. When I visited on a Sunday, real Romans were having lunch at the trattorias around it. Inside, there is a lively restaurant downstairs and a beautiful one on the roof.

The Corinthia is on Piazza del Parlamento. It is quiet and walkable to everything, which is the draw.

Rooms

Space is the story at the Corinthia: large, tastefully appointed rooms, onyx and marble bathrooms, and bedrooms that open onto terraces. It is new, so room categories and views vary. I’ll steer you to the right one.

At Casa Monti, the rooms are all personality: super cool patterns, details everywhere and curtains that hide the TV.

Two spas

At Casa Monti, couples can book the spa just for the two of them, which makes it great for couples. I arrange it before you arrive.

The Corinthia’s spa is beautiful, as are the public spaces, which are big enough that the hotel never feels crowded.

Design

The Corinthia’s renovation is a showstopper from the moment you walk in. I was blown away.

Casa Monti is from the same company as Le Fantaisie in Paris, and it shows: pattern on pattern, and a yellow vintage Fiat 500 parked out front.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Corinthia or Casa Monti?

They suit different travelers. The Corinthia for large rooms, a beautiful spa and a quiet, central base. Casa Monti for younger couples who want something hip, young and fun in a real Roman neighborhood.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

For younger honeymooners who want something fun, Casa Monti, with the spa booked just for the two of you. For space and quiet, the Corinthia.

Which has the better spa?

The Corinthia’s spa is beautiful. Casa Monti’s can be booked privately, which makes it great for couples.

Which has the bigger rooms?

The Corinthia. Its rooms are large, which is rare in central Rome.

Which is quieter?

The Corinthia, across the street from parliament. Casa Monti has a lively restaurant downstairs and trattorias all around, which is the point.
