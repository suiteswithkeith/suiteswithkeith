Is Airelles Palladio worth the premium?

It is my number one hotel in Italy, and it is not cheap. Here is what the rate actually buys you, how it stacks up against the other big names in Venice, and who should book something else.

Toured in person · October 2026

Short version: yes, for most people who are already looking at Venice’s top hotels. But it depends on what you want from Venice.

I’m a travel advisor. In October 2026 I toured four of Venice’s top hotels in one day: the Danieli, Aman Venice, Orient Express and Airelles Palladio. It was not a press trip. I walked the rooms, the spa, the gardens and the Suite del Doge, and I came away calling Airelles the most spectacular hotel I toured in Venice.

“Worth it” is a different question from “best,” though. So this page is about value: what you get for the money, what you give up, and when I would steer you elsewhere.

The short answer

Is it worth it?

Yes, if
You want a resort in Venice, with pools, a real spa, gardens and four great restaurants, five minutes by boat from St. Mark’s.
Yes, if
You are traveling with family. A kids club, an 8-meter kids’ pool and Villa Mason, a three-bedroom suite with its own pool. Nothing else in Venice comes close.
Think twice if
You want to walk out into the streets. Airelles is on Giudecca, deliberately. For a palazzo on the Grand Canal, look at Aman.
Think twice if
You are staying one night. You pay for an estate you won’t have time to use. I’d plan four nights here.
Where the money goes

What the rate actually buys

The “premium” question usually comes from comparing a nightly rate against another hotel’s nightly rate. That comparison misses most of what Airelles is doing. One of my favorite things about it is how much is simply included. You stop signing for things.

01Things you would pay for elsewhere

  • Daily breakfast.
  • The full minibar: wine, bottled cocktails, snacks and a candy jar, restocked daily and at turndown.
  • Poolside hot and non-alcoholic drinks.
  • Laundry for up to 10 pieces per room per day, plus packing and unpacking.
  • The hotel boat to San Marco, frequent and on demand, about three to five minutes each way.
  • The spa, pools, gardens and kids club.

On a four-night family trip, the boat alone changes how you use the city. You go in for the morning, come back for the pool and lunch, then go back in for dinner. Paying for water taxis back and forth adds up quickly.

02A resort, not a palazzo

Airelles has three historic buildings, a church and nearly a hectare of walled gardens at the eastern end of Giudecca, looking across the water at San Marco. It has a 25-meter outdoor pool in the gardens, an 8-meter kids’ pool and an indoor pool in the 1,700-square-meter Spa Airelles by Guerlain, which runs over three levels with a hammam and a yoga studio.

Aman Venice, Orient Express and the Danieli have no pool. In Venice in July, that is not a small thing.

03Four chefs under one roof

Norbert Niederkofler’s three-Michelin-star cooking in Villa Frollo, the first Matsuhisa in Venice, Jean-Georges’ first abc kitchens in Italy, and Cédric Grolet’s pastries throughout. Then the bars, the library, and now Elton John’s late-night club, which opened in November 2026, after my visit. That is a lot of firepower for 45 rooms, and it is a big part of why the estate never feels isolated.

04What booking through me adds

Rates are identical to booking direct. As a Virtuoso booking, you also get an upgrade on arrival when available, a $100 food and beverage credit, a one-way water taxi from Marco Polo Airport on stays of two nights or more, and early check-in and late check-out when available. Suite bookings add an extra amenity and a round-trip airport water taxi. Breakfast is already in the Airelles rate.

The honest part

What you give up

01You are not on the Grand Canal

I would argue that is the selling point: real peace and quiet in a very touristy city. But if your idea of Venice is stepping out your front door into the calli, Giudecca is the wrong side of the water. Look at Aman or the Danieli instead.

02It is further from the train

Santa Lucia station is about 20 minutes by boat. If you are arriving from Florence or Milan and want to be right there, Orient Express in Cannaregio is the easier choice.

03The top suite books fast

The four-bedroom Suite del Doge is a single key, so lock in dates first. Book early for July and for Biennale years.

04My one fear did not come true

I expected Giudecca to feel isolated. It does not, at all. In October it was lively, but never loud.

If not Airelles

When I’d book something else

Aman Venice if you want a small, very private palazzo on the Grand Canal, or you are pairing Venice with Aman Rosa Alpina in the Dolomites. Aman wins at being Aman. For couples, either one is a wonderful choice.

Hotel Cipriani if you want the biggest pool in the city, a clay tennis court and a quieter, more grown-up mood. It is seasonal, so outside its season Airelles is the Giudecca alternative. I haven’t stayed at the Cipriani myself yet.

The Danieli if being at San Marco is the point. It is a bigger hotel, which means better odds of an upgrade.

Orient Express if you care most about interiors, or are arriving by train. Three nights is the sweet spot there.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Is Airelles Palladio worth the price?

For most travelers already looking at Venice’s top hotels, yes. Breakfast, the minibar, laundry, the hotel boat, the spa and the pools are all included, and no other top Venice hotel gives you pools, a three-level spa and four restaurants together. It is less good value if you want to be on the Grand Canal or are only staying one night.

How much does Airelles Palladio cost?

Rates vary a lot by season and room. As a guide, Villa Mason, the three-bedroom suite with a pool, runs approximately $15,000 to $25,000 per night, and the Suite del Doge starts from approximately $50,000 per night. Send me your dates and I’ll confirm exact rates for the room you want.

Is it cheaper to book Airelles Palladio direct?

No. Rates through me are identical to booking direct, and you also get Virtuoso perks: an upgrade on arrival when available, a $100 food and beverage credit, an airport water taxi on stays of two nights or more, and early check-in and late check-out when available.

Have you stayed at Airelles Palladio?

I toured it in person in October 2026, including the Suite del Doge and Villa Mason, as part of a day touring four of Venice’s top hotels. It was not a press trip.

Is Airelles Palladio better than Aman Venice?

For what you get, yes: Aman has no pool, a smaller spa and fewer places to eat and drink. Aman is smaller, more private and on the Grand Canal. My Aman vs Airelles comparison goes deeper.

How many nights should I stay at Airelles Palladio?

Four. You want time to use the estate as well as the city.
