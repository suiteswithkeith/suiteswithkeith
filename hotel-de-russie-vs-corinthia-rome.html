Hotel de Russie or Corinthia?

Two of the few central Rome hotels with a real spa. One is a Rocco Forte I loved, with a courtyard at its heart; the other is a brand-new showstopper on a quiet square across from parliament. Here is how I would choose.

Visited both · October 2026

Choose Hotel de Russie

If you want the courtyard and a pool

I loved it. The courtyard is the star, the renovated rooms are playful, and the spa has an indoor pool. You are between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps.

The question behind the question

Is a courtyard enough calm, or do you want a quiet street as well?

At Hotel de Russie, the calm is inside. The beautiful, large courtyard feels like an escape from the city around it, but step out the front door and you are in the crowds between Popolo and the Steps.

The Corinthia is across the street from parliament, so the area is incredibly safe and quiet while still being in the middle of everything. Add large rooms and a beautiful spa and it is in the conversation with the city’s classics.

If you want a classic with a garden at its heart and a pool downstairs, pick the de Russie. If you want quiet outside as well as in, and more room, pick the Corinthia.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location and quiet

The Corinthia is on Piazza del Parlamento. It is central, walkable to everything, and quiet, which is rare in Rome.

Hotel de Russie is between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps, the golden triangle of Rome on foot. That also means a busy street out front. The courtyard is where you escape it.

Rooms

Space is the story at the Corinthia. The rooms are large and tastefully appointed, with onyx and marble bathrooms. It is new, so categories and views vary. Book with someone who knows it, and I’ll steer you to the right one.

Hotel de Russie’s renovated rooms have the cutest ceilings, each one lemon or orange themed. I also saw a stunning suite with a terrace and fantastic views; ask me which one.

The spas

Spas and pools are few in central Rome, and these are two of them. Hotel de Russie has a nice spa with an indoor pool, a real draw after a day on foot.

The Corinthia’s spa and public spaces are beautiful. If a spa is high on your list, both belong on it.

The feel

Hotel de Russie is a garden retreat. Everyone gathers in the courtyard, and the rooms are playful and light.

The Corinthia is a grand new opening, and the renovation is a showstopper from the moment you walk in. I was blown away.
