Festive FAQ

New Year’s in St. Barths: the questions I get

Where should I stay in St. Barths for New Year’s Eve?

If you want a private villa with a full hotel behind it, my pick for New Year’s Eve 2026 is Villa Diane at Fouquet’s Saint-Barth. It sleeps 10 in five suites, has an infinity pool facing the sunset and the New Year’s Eve fireworks, and you can walk into Gustavia, so you’re close to the harbor on the biggest night of the year without being in the middle of it.

Can you see the New Year’s Eve fireworks from Villa Diane?

Yes. The villa looks over Gustavia and its harbor, and the New Year’s Eve fireworks are one of the views the hotel calls out specifically. You can watch them from your own terrace and infinity pool, then walk into town afterwards if you want to join the party.

How much does a luxury villa in St. Barths cost over New Year’s?

Festive is the most expensive week of the year in St. Barths, and top villas are quoted for the full stay rather than by the night. For Villa Diane, pricing is on request: send me your dates and group size and I’ll come back with the exact total from the hotel. The festive rate includes a dedicated butler, daily breakfast, private airport or port transfers and beach sunbeds.

What dates and minimum stay apply over Christmas and New Year’s?

Festive stays start from December 25, 2026, with an 8-night minimum, so Christmas Day through New Year’s and beyond.

How many people does Villa Diane sleep?

Up to 10 guests in five double suites spread over three floors: a master suite on the upper level, two suites on the main level and two on the lower level. The two lower suites can be set up with twin beds, which is helpful for kids or friends sharing.

What’s included with Villa Diane over the festive season?

A dedicated butler, a private chef for lunch and dinner daily on request, daily breakfast in the villa or at the restaurant, private airport or port roundtrip transfers, complimentary sunbeds and priority booking at Loulou beach restaurant, 24/7 concierge and fitness centre access, daily housekeeping with turndown, unpacking on arrival and complimentary soft drinks in the minibar.

Is Villa Diane walking distance to Gustavia?

Yes. Fouquet’s Saint-Barth sits above Gustavia, so the harbor, the shops and the restaurants are a walk away. Over New Year’s, when roads and parking around the harbor are at their busiest, that matters.

Is Villa Diane a private villa rental or part of a hotel?

It’s a private villa within Fouquet’s Saint-Barth, the Barrière group’s hotel in Gustavia. You get your own house and infinity pool, with Beefbar, the Loulou beach restaurant, a Biologique Recherche spa, a fitness centre and a full hotel team behind you. I’m very pro villas at hotels versus standalone villa rentals, especially over the holidays, when finding staff and restaurant tables on the island is hardest.

Can we have a private chef for New Year’s Eve dinner?

Yes. A private chef for lunch and dinner is available daily on request as part of the festive package, so New Year’s Eve dinner can happen on your own terrace with the fireworks in front of you. I’ll set up the menu with the hotel before you arrive.

How do I get to St. Barths for New Year’s?

Most people fly into St. Maarten (SXM), then take a short connecting flight to St. Barths or the ferry to Gustavia. A private charter is the easiest option over the holidays. Private roundtrip transfers from the St. Barths airport or the port are included with the villa, and I can arrange the connection and private jet charter too.
