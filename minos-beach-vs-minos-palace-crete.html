Head-to-head · Mirabello Bay, Crete

Minos Beach or Minos Palace?

Same founding family, same bay, about ten minutes apart — the 1963 original with waterfront bungalows and sculpture gardens, or its adults-only sister on a private peninsula. The Mamidakis family runs both; who’s traveling decides which is yours.

Sister properties · both on Mirabello Bay · both on my Greek Island Matrix

Choose Minos Beach

If the trip includes kids — or art

Arguably Crete’s first true luxury hotel, and it’s aged the way the best things do: 125 whitewashed bungalows in waterfront gardens, none more than about twenty steps from the sea, threaded with more than 55 site-specific artworks.

Choose Minos Palace

If you want water on three sides

The adults-only sister on its own small peninsula — an island within an island, where all 148 rooms face the sea because there’s nowhere else to face, and a swim before breakfast is a ten-step commute.

The question behind the question

The family books the art hotel; the couple books the peninsula.

Minos Beach is older in the best sense — established, shaded, unhurried, and unlike anywhere else on the island. Agios Nikolaos is under a mile away on foot, a genuine rarity for a resort of this kind, and worth using for the harbourfront tavernas.

Minos Palace occupies a piece of geography no new hotel will ever get again — and it’s the best value on my Crete shortlist. Quiet, surrounded by water, the definition of an easy recommendation.
