Head-to-head · Rome

Bulgari or Casa Monti?

Neither is a hushed grand hotel. Both have energy, a rooftop and a Roman trattoria close at hand. One is big, polished and marble; the other is small, playful and in Monti. Here is how I would choose.

Visited both · October 2026

Choose Bulgari

If you want polish and space

Spectacular. Base rooms the size of junior suites elsewhere, marble and mosaics, the biggest gym I saw in Rome, and a lively scene that is much more sophisticated than an EDITION.

Choose Casa Monti

The question behind the question

You want energy. Do you want it polished, or playful?

The Bulgari is lively, almost like an EDITION but much more sophisticated. It is big and modern, with so many places to eat and drink that I’m not even sure how many there are.

Casa Monti is a boutique hotel for younger couples who want something hip, young and fun. The fun spills out the door into Monti, where real Romans eat at the trattorias.

If you want space, a big gym and everything under one roof, pick the Bulgari. If you are a younger couple who wants a small, playful hotel in a real neighborhood, pick Casa Monti.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Trattorias

Casa Monti is surrounded by authentic Roman trattorias, and when I visited on a Sunday, real Romans were having lunch at them. Inside, there is a beautiful rooftop restaurant and a lively one downstairs.

The Bulgari brings the trattoria inside: a classic Roman one downstairs with dishes that are actually affordable, like €18 pastas. I loved that.

Rooms

The Bulgari’s base rooms are what other hotels would sell as a deluxe room or a junior suite. You often don’t need to upgrade to get space.

At Casa Monti, the rooms are about personality: super cool patterns, details everywhere and curtains that hide the TV.

Spa and gym

At Casa Monti, couples can book the spa just for the two of them. I arrange it before you arrive.

The Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome, massive and with serious equipment, plus a spa.

Rooftops

The Bulgari has a beautiful rooftop bar and pizza restaurant, and another bar on the floor below it.

Casa Monti’s rooftop restaurant is beautiful too, with terraces over the rooftops of Monti.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Bulgari or Casa Monti?

It depends on who is traveling. The Bulgari for space, polish and great dining without leaving the hotel. Casa Monti for younger couples who want something hip, young and fun in a real Roman neighborhood.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

For younger honeymooners, Casa Monti, and book the spa just for the two of you. The Bulgari suits travelers who want a big room and a lively scene.

Which has the bigger rooms?

The Bulgari. Its base rooms are what other hotels would call deluxe rooms or even junior suites.

Which is better for food?

Both are good for eating Roman. The Bulgari has a classic trattoria downstairs with €18 pastas, plus a rooftop bar and pizza restaurant. Casa Monti has a rooftop restaurant, a lively one downstairs, and authentic trattorias all around.

Is either one quiet?

Neither is the pick for quiet. For calm in the middle of Rome, I send people to the Eden or the Corinthia.
