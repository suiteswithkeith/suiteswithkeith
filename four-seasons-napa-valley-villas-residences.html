Four Seasons Napa Valley Villas & Residences

Private space for families and groups of friends, with a Four Seasons resort and a working vineyard around you, in the heart of Calistoga.

Ask about the villas Read my Four Seasons review

I’ve toured the villas and residences, and they’re a fantastic option for families and groups of friends in the heart of Calistoga.

I’ve stayed at Four Seasons Napa Valley four or five times now, and it holds the #02 spot in my Napa & Sonoma ranking. Architecturally it’s beautiful, with a mix of dark, moody buildings and bright white farmhouse styles set within the vineyards. The pools are gorgeous, the Michelin-starred restaurant is fantastic, and I love the pool bar.

What separates it is service at genuine Four Seasons standard, applied to a resort that fits families, couples, girls’ trips and multi-couple groups equally well. The villas and residences take that one step further: everyone stays together, with room to spread out, and the whole resort is still a short walk away.

Before you ask

The questions I get

What is the best place to stay in Napa for a family or group?

For me, the villas and residences at Four Seasons Napa Valley. I’ve toured them, and they’re perfect for families and groups of friends: your own space, with a Four Seasons resort, a working vineyard and a Michelin-starred restaurant around you, in the heart of Calistoga.

Does Four Seasons Napa Valley have villas?

Yes. Alongside the rooms and suites, the resort has private villas and residences set among the vineyards, with more space for families and groups who want to stay together.

Is Four Seasons Napa Valley good for kids?

Yes. It’s one of the few wine country resorts that fits families as well as it fits couples, and service is at genuine Four Seasons standard, which makes a big difference when you’re traveling with kids.

How many people do the villas and residences sleep?

It depends on the layout. Tell me who’s coming and I’ll send the floorplans and match you to the right one.

How much do the villas and residences cost?

Four Seasons prices dynamically, so rates depend on your dates and availability. Ask me and I’ll check, including any third- or fourth-night-free offers.

What do I get booking through you?

Four Seasons Napa Valley is a Fora and Virtuoso preferred partner, so booking through me can include perks like breakfast and upgrade priority, and sometimes a third or fourth night free. Rates are identical to booking direct.
