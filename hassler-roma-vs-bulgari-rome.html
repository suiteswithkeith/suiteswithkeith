The question behind the question

Do you want Rome’s grand-hotel style, or marble, mosaics and a buzz?

The Hassler is a very traditional hotel, and that’s the appeal. You are right at the top of the Spanish Steps, and you eat on the roof at a Michelin-starred restaurant with the rooftops of Rome below you.

The Bulgari is the opposite mood: very modern, lots of marble, and lively. The base rooms are very large, and there are more places to eat and drink than I could count.

If you love classic luxury and want the Steps outside your door, pick the Hassler. If you want room to spread out, a gym and a spa now, and a hotel with energy, pick the Bulgari.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Dining on the roof

The dining is a highlight at the Hassler: a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof, and a beautiful restaurant in the courtyard that is a calmer place to eat than the streets outside.

The Bulgari has a beautiful rooftop bar and pizza restaurant, another bar on the floor below it, and more. Downstairs there’s a classic Roman trattoria with dishes that are actually affordable, like €18 pastas.

Rooms

The Bulgari’s base rooms are what other hotels would sell as a deluxe room or a junior suite, so the entry rate already buys real space. I’ll tell you when a higher category is worth it.

The Hassler’s rooms are very traditional: canopy beds, damask walls, crystal lamps. Some bathrooms have windows over the rooftops to St. Peter’s.

Spa and gym

This is the clearest difference today. The Hassler is building a large new wellness and gym area that opens in 2027. Until then, if a spa and gym matter, I point people to the Bulgari or the Corinthia.

The Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome, massive and with serious equipment, plus a spa.

Location

The Hassler is right above the Spanish Steps. You can’t get much more iconic, or more central, but the Steps draw crowds from morning to night.

The Bulgari is right next to the Mausoleum of Augustus, which is almost done with its renovation. It is busy outside too. For quiet, the Eden is the one I would pick.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Hassler or the Bulgari?

It depends on your style. The Hassler for very traditional luxury at the top of the Spanish Steps and Michelin-starred dining on the roof. The Bulgari for very large base rooms, modern design, a lively scene and the biggest gym in Rome.

Which has a spa and gym?

The Bulgari, with the biggest gym I saw in Rome plus a spa. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027.

Which has the bigger rooms?

The Bulgari. Its base rooms are what other hotels would call deluxe rooms or even junior suites. The Hassler’s rooms are classic and very traditional.

Which has the better restaurants?

For a Michelin star, the Hassler’s Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof. For variety, the Bulgari: a rooftop bar and pizza restaurant, more bars, and a trattoria with €18 pastas.

Is either one quiet?

No. At both you step out into busy streets, and the Hassler is right at the crowded Steps. For quiet in the middle of Rome, I pick the Eden or the Corinthia.
