How to read the numbers
Real May 2027 rates, held once we book.

Every figure below is the room and breakfast for the two of you for the whole stay — not per night — taken from each hotel’s published mid-May 2027 pricing. They are accurate today but not locked: rates move as the season fills, and the best suites go early. Flights are separate, roughly $1,700–2,200 each from the US. Add transfers, a boat day and dinners out and most of these land inside the $11–13k you were aiming at, with headroom to move up a suite category if that is where you would rather spend it.

Acro Suites

Agia Pelagia · adults only · 25 min from Heraklion

Built into the cliff face above the water, and adults only, which is exactly what you want for the first days of a honeymoon. You land, you are checked in within half an hour, and then you genuinely do not have to think about anything again. It is the most complete decompression on this list.

Pools & spa

Infinity pools stepped down the cliff and a spa carved into the rock — couples’ treatment room, hammam, candlelit indoor pool. Most suites have their own pool or hot tub on the terrace. There is a swimming platform at the foot of the cliff rather than a beach, which is why the second half of this itinerary matters.

Phaea Blue

Elounda · villas above the gulf · 75 min drive

The beach half, and a proper one. Low stone villas stepping down a green hillside, each with its own pool, and the water below with sand, loungers and towel service. Boats leave the bay for Spinalonga island, and Plaka village and the town of Agios Nikolaos are close by for dinners and wandering when you feel like leaving.

Pools & spa

A private pool with almost every villa, a large main pool, and the beach below that. The spa runs a full menu — massage, facials, steam — and the couples’ treatment is worth booking for your first afternoon before the week fills up.

Tella Thera

Crete · small, design-led, very private

A handful of suites rather than a resort, built with real care and set in the landscape rather than on a strip. The appeal is total privacy: your own pool, your own terrace, and days that dissolve. You will want a car for a couple of the days, which on Crete is easy and pleasant.

Bill & Coo Coast

Agios Ioannis · adults only · on its own beach

The quieter, grown-up way to do Mykonos, and adults only. A small hotel directly on its own stretch of sand — ten minutes from town when you want it, completely removed from it when you don’t. Breakfast on the beach, and one of the best sunsets on the island without queueing for it.

Pools & beach

A sea-facing infinity pool a few steps above the sand, private plunge pools in the upper suites, and the beach itself — sheltered, swimmable, served from the hotel. Treatments are done in-suite.

Gundari

Folegandros · adults only · cliffs and nothing else

Low stone and concrete buildings on an empty clifftop, an outstanding restaurant, and an island of roughly one village. There is very little to do here, deliberately — which is the best argument on this list for taking the ten nights, because it takes a couple of days to slow down enough for it.

Pools & spa

A long clifftop main pool, private pools with most suites, and a small spa built into the rock — massage, sauna, cold plunge. Getting into the sea means a boat or a short drive, so the pools do the work.

Santorini to finish

Same four hotels and pricing as option four

Ending on the caldera rather than starting there has a real advantage: you arrive already unwound and brown, and the last evening of the honeymoon is that view at sunset. The one thing to watch is the boat — Milos to Santorini runs three or four times a week in May, so the ferry day sets the dates rather than the other way round.

Worth knowing

Milos has almost nothing directly on the sand at this level — the good hotels sit above the water rather than on it. If waking up and walking straight into the sea is the priority, options one and three are the honest answers.

Paros — Parilio or Cosme

Sister hotels · inland calm or on the sand

Two sisters, one decision: Parilio is the serene inland one built around a beautiful cross-shaped pool; Cosme sits right on the water at Naoussa, a short walk from the prettiest harbour town in the Cyclades. Cosme costs more — that is the beachfront premium at work.

Pools & spa

Both have proper pools and full spas; many suites at each take a private plunge pool. Cosme adds the sea at your feet and Naoussa’s restaurants a stroll away.

Erema to finish

Milos · same hotel and pricing as option five

Everything I said about Milos in option five holds here — the boat day, the beaches, the easy dinners — you simply reach it from Paros instead of ending on Santorini. Paros and Milos are connected by a direct ferry of about two hours.

The routing

In: US to Athens, then a 35-minute hop to Paros — an easy same-day connection. Out: a 40-minute flight from Milos back through Athens. The connection sounds fussier than it is; both hops run several times a day in May.
