How I’d choose

Which villa, for which trip

  • Young kids: Canaves Epitome on Santorini, or Four Seasons Mykonos with its kids club and private beach.
  • Two or three families together: Fouquet’s Paradiso or Delos, the Andronis Eden Villa, or Jupiter on Paros.
  • A milestone celebration: the Suite del Doge at Airelles Palladio in Venice, the Forestis Villa in the Dolomites, or Gundari on the cliffs of Folegandros.
  • A slow Italian summer: Borgo Pignano in Tuscany.
  • Wine country with friends: Four Seasons Napa Valley in Calistoga.
Before you ask

The questions I get

How do you choose the villas on this page?

Every villa here sits at a hotel I’ve stayed at, toured or work with closely, and I’ve sent clients to them. If I wouldn’t book it for my own family, it isn’t here.

Why villas at hotels instead of private villa rentals?

I’m very pro private villas at hotels versus standalone villas. You get your own house and your own pool, with a full hotel team, restaurants, a spa and often a kids club behind you, instead of arranging staff and groceries yourself.

What’s the best villa for a family?

For Santorini, the Canaves Epitome villas. For Mykonos, Four Seasons for younger kids and Fouquet’s for bigger groups. In Italy, Borgo Pignano in Tuscany. Tell me the kids’ ages and I’ll narrow it down.

Does booking a villa through you cost more?

No. Rates are identical to booking direct, and my clients receive Fora perks on eligible stays.
