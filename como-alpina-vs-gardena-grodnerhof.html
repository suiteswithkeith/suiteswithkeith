The question behind the question

Do you want a town, or the meadows outside your door?

Gardena Grödnerhof was a close second to Forestis for me, and the reason starts with location. Ortisei is a genuinely charming town with great restaurants, and lifts leave from town to Seceda and to the Alpe di Siusi. Add a beautiful renovation, an incredible spa and great service, and it is the best base in the Dolomites.

COMO Alpina’s huge selling point is location too. You are right on the Alpe di Siusi, and you walk out of your room onto the trails in summer and the slopes in winter. It is not quite the hotel Gardena is, but the price reflects that, and the spa and pool hold their own.

For a ski trip, pick COMO Alpina; it is my first choice in winter. In summer the two tie for location, so pick Gardena for the town and the restaurants, and COMO Alpina for hiking straight from your door. Or do both: it is one of my favorite pairings.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Town or meadows

At Gardena Grödnerhof, you walk out into Ortisei. Take the Seceda lift and you are in the jagged high country of Val Gardena; take the gondola to the Alpe di Siusi and you are in rolling meadows. The one caveat is July and August, when Ortisei is busy and the crowds follow you onto the trails.

COMO Alpina is up on those meadows, with mountains towering all around. The area is protected, so the road up is restricted to permit holders. The hotel handles the permit, and a private transfer avoids the question entirely.

Skiing and hiking

COMO Alpina is the only true ski-in, ski-out hotel of the five I saw, and in summer the trails start at your door. For skiing, it is my first choice in the Dolomites.

At Gardena Grödnerhof, the skiing all around Ortisei is fantastic, and you get to the lifts by foot or shuttle. For hiking, the Seceda lift needs a time-slotted ticket bought in advance; the Col Raiser lift from Santa Cristina, about 10 minutes away, reaches the same area. A private guide is better still, and I arrange one.

Rooms

At Gardena Grödnerhof, everything from Superior Deluxe up has been beautifully redone, and the original building was restored so well you’d never guess its age. The Superior and Comfort rooms keep the original charm, but the renovated rooms are what I book.

Rooms at COMO Alpina are very spacious, with big decks, large bathrooms and tubs. Mind the chairlift right outside the Mountain View rooms: in the shoulder season it can feel a little loud and not very private. In winter I don’t think it would bother you much.

Spa, food and service

Gardena Grödnerhof has an incredible spa and a gym that ties with Aman Rosa Alpina’s for the best in the Dolomites. Service was warm, polished and consistent, and Ortisei is the best town in the region for dining off property.

At COMO Alpina, the spa and pool stack up very nicely against every hotel I saw, and like most COMO properties the whole place leans into wellness. We had a very nice dinner in the formal restaurant; service elsewhere was good, if a little awkward at times. Expect German and Austrian sauna culture at both: the main saunas are mixed, and swimsuits come off.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, COMO Alpina or Gardena Grödnerhof?

For me, Gardena Grödnerhof. It was a close second to Forestis and it is the best base in the Dolomites. Pick COMO Alpina for a ski trip, or to hike straight from your door for less.

Which is better for skiing?

COMO Alpina. It is the only true ski-in, ski-out hotel I saw, and my first choice for a ski trip. At Gardena Grödnerhof, the skiing around Ortisei is fantastic, but you walk or shuttle to the lifts.

Which is better for summer hiking?

They tie for location. Gardena Grödnerhof has lifts from town to two completely different landscapes, Seceda and the Alpe di Siusi. At COMO Alpina, the trails start at your door. If you want quiet, avoid Ortisei in July and August.

Which is better for families?

Both made my list of the best Dolomites hotels for families. Gardena Grödnerhof is the more well-rounded hotel, with a town and restaurants on the doorstep; COMO Alpina puts the meadows right outside.

Which room should I book at each?

At Gardena Grödnerhof, a Superior Deluxe or above; those are the renovated rooms. At COMO Alpina, tell me how you are traveling first. In the shoulder season I would avoid the Mountain View rooms, because of the chairlift right outside.

Can I do both?

Yes, and it is one of my favorite pairings: two or three nights on the Alpe di Siusi at COMO Alpina and two or three in Ortisei at Gardena Grödnerhof. You get the meadows, then the peaks of Val Gardena, and the transfer between them is short.
