Head-to-head · Wine country honeymoons

Auberge du Soleil or Montage Healdsburg?

I keep a list of my 15 favorite honeymoon hotels in the world, called The Fifteen. Only two in American wine country made it: Auberge du Soleil in Napa and Montage Healdsburg in Sonoma. Here’s how I choose between them.

Both on my list of the world’s 15 best honeymoon hotels · both Fora / Virtuoso partners

Choose Auberge du Soleil

If dinner with a view is the highlight

Napa’s original luxury hotel, set on a hillside in Rutherford with views across the whole valley. It’s known for three things: the restaurant terrace, the view and the wine list. For a romantic dinner in Napa, there’s nowhere better.

Choose Montage

If you want to live among the vines

Bungalows set right in the vineyards, and a slower pace. Every vineyard room has its own fire pit, and after more than twenty stays, waking up next to the vines still hasn’t gotten old. The restaurant has the best view of any on my list.

Two valleys, two different trips

Auberge du Soleil is in Rutherford, right in the middle of Napa Valley. Montage is just outside Healdsburg, in northern Sonoma County. The Mayacamas Mountains sit between the two valleys, so these really are two different wine-country trips: different towns, different wineries and a different pace.

The verdict

So, which would I book?

Both are on my list of the world’s 15 best honeymoon hotels, so you can’t go wrong. It comes down to the trip you want.

Choose Auberge du Soleil

For a first trip to Napa

Book Auberge if it’s your first time in Napa and the trip is about romance. Dinner on the terrace as the sun goes down over the valley, a great bottle from the wine list, and the Napa most people picture before they arrive. For the classic version, this is the one.

Choose Montage Healdsburg

For the slower pace of Sonoma

Book Montage if you’d rather have a contemporary resort, your own bungalow in the vineyards and the slower pace of Healdsburg. It’s the hotel I’ve gone back to more than any other in the world, more than twenty stays, and the fire pit on your own terrace is a big part of why.
