Head-to-head · Rome

Hotel de Russie or Bulgari?

One is built around a big, calm courtyard between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps. The other is all marble, mosaics and energy, next to the Mausoleum of Augustus. Here is how I would choose.

Visited both · October 2026

Choose Hotel de Russie

If you want a garden escape and a pool

A beautiful, large courtyard that feels like an escape from the city, renovated rooms with lemon and orange ceilings, and a spa with an indoor pool.

The question behind the question

When you get back from a day on foot, do you want calm or a buzz?

Hotel de Russie is built around its courtyard. It is where everyone gathers, and it feels like an escape from the city around it. Add the spa with an indoor pool and you have somewhere to unwind after the crowds.

The Bulgari is lively, almost like an EDITION but much more sophisticated. The base rooms are very large, and there are so many bars and restaurants that you barely need to leave.

Both put you on busy streets the moment you step out. If you want to come back to a garden and a swim, pick the de Russie. If you want room to spread out and a scene upstairs, pick the Bulgari.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms and space

Space is rare in central Rome, and the Bulgari is one of the exceptions. The base rooms are what other hotels would sell as a deluxe room or a junior suite, so the entry rate already buys real space. I’ll tell you when a higher category is worth it.

At Hotel de Russie, the rooms have been renovated and they are charming, each with a lemon or orange themed ceiling. I also saw a stunning suite with a terrace and fantastic views. Ask me and I’ll tell you which one.

Pool or gym

Hotel de Russie has a nice spa with an indoor pool. That is rare in the centro storico, and a real draw after a day walking the city.

The Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome: massive, with serious equipment, plus a spa. If you train on vacation, this is the one.

Eating and drinking

At the Bulgari, there is a beautiful rooftop bar and pizza restaurant, another bar on the floor below it, and more. Honestly, I’m not even sure how many there are. Downstairs there’s a classic Roman trattoria with fair prices, like €18 pastas. I loved that.

At Hotel de Russie, life happens in the courtyard, with its bar, and in the restaurant with hand-painted walls.

Location and noise

Hotel de Russie sits between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps, the golden triangle of Rome on foot. The street out front is busy, so the courtyard is your calm.

The Bulgari is right next to the Mausoleum of Augustus, which is almost done with its renovation. It is on the lively side. If quiet is the priority for either, I would send you to the Eden.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Hotel de Russie or the Bulgari?

It depends on what you want to come home to. Hotel de Russie for the courtyard, the playful renovated rooms and a spa with an indoor pool. The Bulgari for very large base rooms, a lively, sophisticated scene and great dining without leaving the hotel.

Which has the bigger rooms?

The Bulgari. Its base rooms are what other hotels would call deluxe rooms or even junior suites. At Hotel de Russie, ask me about the suite with the views.

Which has the better spa?

For a swim, Hotel de Russie: its spa has an indoor pool, rare in the centro storico. For a workout, the Bulgari, with the biggest gym I saw in Rome plus a spa.

Which is better for a first trip to Rome?

Hotel de Russie is one I recommend to Rome first-timers: you are between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps, and you come back to the courtyard.

Is either one quiet?

Not outside the door. At both you step out into busy streets, and the Bulgari is lively inside too. For quiet in the middle of Rome, I pick the Eden or the Corinthia.
