The question behind the question

The new estate, or the legend?

If you want a pool, gardens and space in Venice, your choice comes down to these two neighbors on Giudecca. Both sit across the water from St. Mark’s, and both have their own boats to get you there in about five minutes.

I toured Airelles Palladio in October 2026, and it is my number one hotel in Italy. It gives you more: a bigger spa, more pools, more restaurants and more included in the rate. For families it is the clear answer.

I have not stayed at the Cipriani yet, so I write about it as a Belmond partner. It is the choice for travelers who want the classic Venice resort: the biggest pool in the city, the clay court, Oro, and a quieter, more grown-up mood, now refreshed by Peter Marino.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location and mood

Both hotels are on Giudecca, which I would argue is a selling point: you get real peace and quiet in a very touristy city, and St. Mark’s is five minutes away by hotel boat. The Cipriani sits on the tip of the island looking straight at the Piazza, with the lagoon on three sides.

The difference is the mood. Airelles is private and secure, yet lively, social and fun. In October it was lively but never loud. The Cipriani is classic late-1950s Venice, Murano glass and terrazzo, and its pool scene is calm and leans adult.

Pools, spa and tennis

The Cipriani has the bigger pool: Olympic-sized, heated and saltwater, the largest in Venice. It also has a clay tennis court framed by hedges and the lagoon, number two in my hotel tennis ranking. The new Dior Spa replaces what used to be the hotel’s weak spot.

Airelles has more of everything else: a 25-meter pool hidden in the gardens, an 8-meter pool just for children, and the Domus indoor pool inside Spa Airelles by Guerlain, 1,700 square meters over three levels. As a spa hotel, it wins.

Dining and what is included

Airelles has four chefs and five bars for 45 rooms: Norbert Niederkofler, Matsuhisa, Jean-Georges and Cédric Grolet. You could stay a week without repeating an evening. The minibar, breakfast, daily laundry, the spa and the pools are all included, so you stop signing for things.

The Cipriani has Oro, small and Michelin-starred, now open for lunch too, and Cip’s Club on the water, the table to book at sunset. Booked through me with Belmond’s Bellini Club, breakfast for two, an upgrade when available and a hotel credit come with your stay.

Rooms and families

At Airelles, the rooms are incredibly spacious. For a family I would book Villa Mason, the three-bedroom suite with its own pool and garden. For the big occasion, the four-bedroom Suite del Doge looks straight at St. Mark’s. Add the Kids Club and the kids’ pool and it is the best family hotel in Venice.

At the Cipriani, not every room has been renovated yet. Peter Marino’s first phase redid a set of rooms and suites, including garden suites with balconies over the Casanova Gardens and the two signature suites, Laguna and Serenissima, laid out like Venetian apartments facing the lagoon. Many of the other rooms still have the older decor, so which room you book matters more here than at almost any hotel in Venice. Tell me your dates and I can tell you exactly which one to book.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Hotel Cipriani or Airelles Palladio?

For most travelers, Airelles Palladio. It has the bigger spa, more pools, four chefs and more included in the rate, and it is my number one hotel in Italy. Choose Hotel Cipriani if you want the classic: the largest pool in Venice, a clay tennis court, Oro and a quieter, more grown-up mood.

Are Hotel Cipriani and Airelles Palladio near each other?

Yes. Both are on Giudecca, across the water from St. Mark’s. The Cipriani is on the tip of the island and Airelles is at the eastern end beside Le Zitelle. Each runs its own boat to St. Mark’s, about five minutes.

Which has the better pool?

The Cipriani has the biggest single pool: Olympic-sized, heated and saltwater, the largest in Venice. Airelles has three: a 25-meter pool in the gardens, a kids’ pool and an indoor pool in the spa.

Which is better for families?

Airelles Palladio: a Kids Club, a pool just for children, gardens and Villa Mason, a three-bedroom suite with its own pool. The Cipriani has a children’s club and suites for four, but its pool scene is quiet and leans adult.

Are all the rooms at the Hotel Cipriani renovated?

No. Peter Marino’s renovation is happening in phases: the reception, a first set of rooms and suites, and the signature Laguna and Serenissima suites are done, while many other rooms still have the older decor. Every room at Airelles Palladio is new, since it opened in 2026. If you are set on the Cipriani, I can tell you which room to book so you get a renovated one.

Which has tennis?

Hotel Cipriani, with a clay court framed by hedges and the lagoon. It is number two in my hotel tennis ranking.

Is Hotel Cipriani open year-round?

No. The Cipriani is seasonal and closes for the winter; Belmond currently lists the reopening as 1 June 2027. If you are traveling outside its season, Airelles Palladio is the Giudecca alternative.
