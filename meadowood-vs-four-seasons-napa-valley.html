Head-to-head · Napa

Meadowood or Four Seasons?

#01 and #02 on my Napa & Sonoma ranking, and the most common Napa splurge decision I’m asked to make. The estate that sets the service benchmark for all of wine country — or the vineyard resort that fits almost everyone.

Years of repeat stays at both · #01 and #02 on my ranking

Choose Meadowood

If the trip is a celebration

Around 40 private cottages on a wooded estate, service at Aman standards, and the intimacy-plus-polish combination that keeps it at #01 after years of repeat stays. The wine-country honeymoon and anniversary answer.

Choose Four Seasons

If the trip is a crowd

Set inside its own working vineyard in Calistoga, with a one-star Michelin restaurant, gorgeous pools, and the flexibility to host families, girls trips, and multi-couple groups as comfortably as couples.

The question behind the question

The benchmark or the all-rounder.

For a honeymoon, an anniversary, or any trip where the hotel is the celebration: Meadowood. Every time I go, I feel well taken care of — the service, the cottages, and the sense of place are why it’s the property I measure every other Napa stay against.

For everything else — the family trip, the girls trip, the four-couple harvest weekend — Four Seasons is the rare wine-country hotel that genuinely fits all of them, and the Michelin star and pool scene don’t hurt. Both are at the top of my list for a reason; the group decides this one, not the hotels.
