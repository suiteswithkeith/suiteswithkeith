Head-to-head · Sifnos

NOS or Stamna?

My two picks on Sifnos, a short drive apart and built on the same idea: quiet luxury, nothing in excess. The difference is how you want to spend the day. Sunrise over the Aegean and a walk to the Faros tavernas, or the best hotel dinner on the island and Apollonia on foot.

Stayed at Stamna, visited NOS · both June 2026 · both in my Sifnos guide

Choose NOS

If you want the sea and the sunrise

Minimalist Cycladic architecture above the fishing village of Faros, east-facing views that make early mornings the best part of the day, and some of Sifnos’s best waterfront tavernas a walk away. Calm, quiet, grown-up.

Choose Stamna

If dinner is the point

A small, design-forward perch in the hills above Platis Gialos, with Plio, a restaurant that holds its own on Greece’s great foodie island, and Apollonia’s wine bars and boutiques on foot. Earthy, warm, exactly what you want Sifnos to be.

The question behind the question

For a first trip to Sifnos: Stamna. For pure quiet: NOS.

Stamna is the one on my Greece top 10, and the one I stayed at. After ten nights between Mykonos and Paros it was exactly the respite I needed: the design, the perch over the Cyclades, and a dinner at Plio that lives up to the island’s reputation. Sifnos is a food island first, and Stamna puts you closest to it, with Apollonia a walk away for the evening.

But NOS is one of my favorite stays in the Cyclades, and it’s the better fit if you’re coming for stillness rather than the table. Early mornings with the sun coming up over the Aegean, an afternoon of doing nothing by your suite, then a walk into Faros for dinner on the water. If that’s the trip you’re picturing, it settles the question.

And Sifnos rewards slow travel, so on a longer stay, splitting the nights between the two is an easy yes. You get both sides of the island.
