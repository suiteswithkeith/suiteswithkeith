## The ask

Design a set of text overlays for an Instagram carousel of short videos shot at Grand Hotel Miramare in Santa Margherita Ligure. Each slide is a video clip with a typographic overlay on top. The overlays should feel like they belong to the hotel's own world (cream, Riviera red, a romantic script, wide engraved caps, sun-badge energy) while clearly being **@suiteswithkeith** content.

## Positioning (read this first)

- **The Miramare stands on its own.** Present it as an incredible hotel in its own right: a restored 1904 Belle Époque grand hotel, family-run, on the sea in a real Italian town.
- **Don't knock any other hotel.** No "better than", "vs.", "half the price of", and don't name the Splendido or Portofino hotels. The most we say is that Portofino is 15 minutes away for lunch.
- **The hook is the pairing.** The Miramare is the coastal leg that ties northern Italy together: Lake Como → the Riviera → Tuscany & Florence. About three hours by road in each direction.
- **The close is me.** I build the whole route and handle every move between hotels: private transfers, or a supercar for the drive with a follow car carrying your luggage.

## Format

- **Size:** 1080 × 1350 (4:5). This is the carousel feed size; 9:16 clips get cropped to it in the feed.
- **Safe area:** keep all text inside the middle ~1080 × 1150; leave ~100 px clear top and bottom. Keep the bottom-left corner free of key text (the IG UI and the "1/10" dots sit there).
- **Overlay treatment:** text sits directly on the video. Use a soft gradient scrim (top or bottom 35%, black at 0→35% opacity) only where needed for legibility. Cream or red cards (solid fill) are allowed on the text-led slides (1, 6, 9, 10).
- **Motion (optional):** gentle fades and a slow 2–4% scale on the type. No bouncing, no typewriter effects.

| Body type | Clean, simple sans, sentence case, centered |

| Layout | Centered, airy, editorial. Lots of breathing room. Never more than ~20 words on a video slide. |

**Don't use** the hotel's logo, wordmark or their actual badges. Evoke the style; keep the marks ours.

Handle on every slide: small `@suiteswithkeith` in wide caps, cream or red, top center or bottom right.
