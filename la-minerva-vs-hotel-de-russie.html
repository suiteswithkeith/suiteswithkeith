The question behind the question

Do you want to be in the neighborhood, or escape from it?

La Minerva puts you in the neighborhood. You step out by the Pantheon into the city as Romans actually live it, and the hotel’s warmth comes from its people and its shared spaces.

The Hotel de Russie gives you an escape. The street out front is busy, between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps, but inside, the large courtyard is the star of the property, and there is a spa with an indoor pool.

If you are younger and want energy and personality, pick La Minerva. If it is your first time in Rome and you want a courtyard and a pool to come back to, pick the de Russie.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Where you spend the evening

At La Minerva, the rooftop restaurant is where I would spend the evening, looking over the domes and rooftops of the centro storico. Then down to the bar and the lobby, which have a real buzz.

At the Hotel de Russie, the evening happens in the courtyard. It is beautiful and large, and it is where everyone gathers, with a bar of arched blue shelves at one side.

Spa and downtime

The Hotel de Russie has a nice spa with an indoor pool, rare in the centro storico and a real draw after a day on foot.

Downtime at La Minerva is social: the lobby and the bar are shared spaces you actually want to sit in. Book a Signature Suite through me and it can include a 50-minute massage for two.

Views

I saw a stunning suite at the Hotel de Russie with fantastic views, its terrace looking over the obelisk in Piazza del Popolo to the hills. Ask me and I will tell you which one.

The views around La Minerva are more authentic Rome than pristine Rome. I love that. If the postcard view matters most, ask me about the Eden.

Rooms and the little things

The Hotel de Russie rooms have been renovated, and they have the cutest ceilings, each one either lemon or orange themed, with marble bathrooms and a mosaic of birds set into some showers.

What I remember most at La Minerva is a great laugh at check-in and the best slippers of my whole trip. I kept them.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, La Minerva or Hotel de Russie?

It depends on the trip. La Minerva for younger travelers who want energy and a real neighborhood. The Hotel de Russie for a courtyard, a spa with an indoor pool and a classic first trip to Rome. I loved both.

Which is better for a first trip to Rome?

The Hotel de Russie. It is between Piazza del Popolo and the Spanish Steps, the golden triangle of Rome on foot. La Minerva suits travelers who want to live like a Roman by the Pantheon.

Which has a pool?

The Hotel de Russie, with an indoor pool in its spa.

Which is better before La Dolce Vita?

La Minerva. La Dolce Vita journeys start in Rome, so I put clients at La Minerva first.
