About

Meet Keith

This spring I spent four weeks checking into 38 hotels across eight Greek islands — because “which room should we book?” deserves a better answer than a screenshot of someone else’s stay.

I’m a Fora travel advisor specializing in Europe, honeymoons and hotels worth the airfare. I tour the properties, sleep in the rooms where it matters, keep the notes — and when you book through me, the hotel hears from me before you arrive.

Fora advisor · 123 hotels on file · 38 toured & stayed across the Cyclades

How it works

Three steps, no homework

01Tell me where and when. One sentence is enough — “Santorini in June.” Traveling inside 30 days? You’ll hear from me the same day.
02Get a recommendation with reasons. Three or four hotels, the room worth booking at each, and what each one is wrong for. Not a list — a verdict.
03I book it and stay on it. Same rate as booking direct — sometimes better — plus the perks, and my call to the hotel before you land.

The question everyone asks

“Why not just use my Amex?”

The perks are similar. The experience isn’t.

Amex and Chase can match the breakfast and the credit. What they can’t do is tell you which of two hotels is right for you, name the room worth the difference, or call the property before you arrive. Have FHR credits? Use both: book one night through Amex, the rest through me, and you usually keep the benefits of each.

Already booked?
In many cases I can take over the reservation as your advisor and add the partner benefits to a stay you already have.
Points?
I consult on Amex, Chase, Citi and Bilt points — including business and first-class award flights.
How far ahead?
Three to six months is ideal. Shorter works — a Saint-Tropez peak-season save once took 24 hours.
