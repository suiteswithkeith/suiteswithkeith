Head-to-head · Next-door neighbors, outside Oia

Andronis Arcadia or Canaves Epitome?

They share a property line just outside Oia and the same golden-hour orientation — a real sunset into the open Aegean, something the Caldera hotels physically cannot offer. And they could not be aiming at more different trips. One is a true resort with a scene; the other is my favorite hotel in Greece.

Literally next door · both sunset-facing · my #01 in Greece lives here

Choose Arcadia

If you want options and energy

A true resort — 114 suites and villas, every one with a private pool, including some of the island’s largest. The ARCADemy kids club gives families real infrastructure, and Pacman’s live music and Beefbar Santorini bring a scene the rest of this list doesn’t have.

Choose Epitome

If you want the private world

Fifty-three rooms that still feel boutique, landscaped gardens behind volcanic-rock walls, two-bedroom suites with pools and outdoor dining decks — and Omnia, the best hotel restaurant I ate at across the entire trip.

One practical note: Arcadia is massive and spread out — in summer heat, with no meltemi wind blowing, you’ll want a golf cart to get around. Epitome is more spread out than the Caldera hotels too, but its 53 rooms still feel boutique.

The question behind the question

Epitome — unless the scene is the point.

If you’re asking me for the single best hotel I experienced across the Greek islands, it’s Canaves Epitome — perhaps one of the top three stays of my entire life. The gardens turn the common areas into a private world minutes from Oia’s crowds, the two-bedroom suites are the star, and the sunset is the best in Greece.

Arcadia is the right Santorini hotel for travelers who want activity and options rather than seclusion — and for families, the infrastructure is real; they’ll tell you the same. Great if you like a bit of a scene; not the move if you want exclusivity and quiet. Planning a wedding at either — room blocks at Arcadia, a buyout at Epitome — is exactly what I do.
