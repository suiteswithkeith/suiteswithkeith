Head-to-head · The Cyclades’ two headliners

Santorini or Mykonos?

The two islands with real international airports, and the two carrying the biggest reputations in Greece. I worked both on my Cyclades trip — here’s which one your trip actually wants, and the hotel decisions waiting inside whichever you pick.

From my 38-hotel Cyclades trip · the two islands you can fly into from outside Greece

Choose Santorini

If the caldera is the reason you’re coming

The island that introduced the world to Cycladic luxury. It earns its reputation for two or three nights in a caldera suite — the cave rooms, the plunge pools, the sunset — and it’s the crescendo of any route: finish here, don’t start.

Choose Mykonos

If you want range — the loud island and the quiet one

I expected a party island and found some of the best hotels, meals and service of my entire trip through the Cyclades. It’s genuinely two islands — and which one you get depends almost entirely on what was booked before you landed.

You don’t actually have to choose: Mykonos 2 → Paros 3 → Santorini 3 is the full-arc route on my island pairings page, and the crossings between neighbours run under two hours. What I’d push back on is starting on Santorini — it’s the bar the rest of the trip gets measured against, and arriving day one ties you to whichever caldera hotel has space that night.

The question behind the question

First time, planning around dinner and the view: Santorini — as the finale.

Santorini earns its reputation, and for a first trip built around the classic picture it’s the answer — two or three nights in a caldera suite, booked well ahead, placed at the end of the route so it’s the last thing you see rather than the measuring stick.

But Mykonos was the island that surprised me most — substance where I expected only scene. If your trip is as much about the beach day, the boat day and the table as the view, it’s the stronger base — provided the bookings are made before you land. And the honest answer for most ten-night trips is both, with Paros in between.
