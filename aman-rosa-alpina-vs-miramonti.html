The question behind the question

Do you want polish, or personality?

Aman Rosa Alpina is the most luxurious hotel in the Dolomites, in a fantastic spot for skiing and hiking, and as good for families as it is for couples. The suites are the star, and the views are intimate.

Miramonti is a nice, unique property with an iconic pool, close to Merano. It isn’t at the level of the region’s top hotels as a luxury experience, but it is a great hotel for someone who values personality, the pool and the views over polish.

If service and finish matter most, pick Aman. If the pool and the views are the point, pick Miramonti. It is an easy first or last stop, especially if you are coming from or heading toward Lake Como or Switzerland.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Setting and views

Aman Rosa Alpina sits in San Cassiano, with views of the forest, the village and the mountains above. They are beautiful and intimate; you won’t get the dramatic panoramas of Forestis, COMO Alpina or Miramonti.

Miramonti looks out over the valley and Merano. Merano isn’t really in the Dolomites the way the other hotels I saw are: it is a spa town with a gentler climate and big views.

Rooms

Book a suite at Aman Rosa Alpina. They are the best rooms in the hotel and the reason to stay, in classic Aman wood and marble.

At Miramonti, every room is designed differently, and the whole place is a little quirky. Because no two are alike, the right room matters, and I match one to you.

Pools, spa and gym

Aman Rosa Alpina has a lovely spa with an indoor pool, and its gym ties with Gardena Grödnerhof’s for the best in the Dolomites.

Miramonti’s infinity pool is the best outdoor pool of any hotel I saw in the region, and it is the reason people come. Expect German and Austrian sauna culture at both: the main saunas are mixed, and swimsuits come off.

Service and the active days

Service in the lobby restaurant at Aman Rosa Alpina was fantastic. Two small things surprised me: some of the original walls show their age, and our arrival was a little awkward. It is not ski-in, ski-out, but there is a ski room and the lifts are about two minutes away by shuttle. Come in winter or summer; San Cassiano was a ghost town in October.

Miramonti is character over polish. For hiking among the famous peaks, base yourself in Ortisei or on the Alpe di Siusi, and treat Miramonti as the stay for the pool and the views.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Aman Rosa Alpina or Miramonti?

It depends on what you want. Aman Rosa Alpina is the most luxurious hotel in the Dolomites and my pick for the best luxury property. Miramonti is a great stay for the right traveler: someone who wants the pool, the views and a hotel with personality.

Which is better for skiing and hiking?

Aman Rosa Alpina. It is in a fantastic spot for both, with a ski room and the lifts about two minutes away by shuttle. Miramonti is above Merano, outside the main Dolomites valleys.

Which has the better pool?

Miramonti. Its infinity pool is the best outdoor pool of any hotel I saw in the region. Aman Rosa Alpina has a lovely spa with an indoor pool.

Which is better for families?

Aman Rosa Alpina. It is as good for families as it is for couples. Miramonti is best suited to couples and design lovers.

Which room should I book at each?

At Aman Rosa Alpina, a suite. They are the reason to stay. At Miramonti, every room is different, so tell me what matters to you and I will match one.

Is Miramonti really in the Dolomites?

Not really. Miramonti is above Merano, which is outside the main Dolomites valleys. It works best as a first or last stop on a Dolomites trip.
