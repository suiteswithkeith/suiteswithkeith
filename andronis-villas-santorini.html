The Andronis Villas

Thirteen private pool villas across four Andronis hotels in Oia and Imerovigli, from caldera hideaways for two to the twelve-guest Eden Villa.

Ask about the villas Canaves vs. Andronis

I’ve toured every Andronis hotel on Santorini, and each one does villas a little differently.

Every Andronis villa comes with its own pool, and the four hotels cover very different trips. Arcadia is the resort, with the biggest villas and real family infrastructure. Concept is the wellness retreat near Imerovigli, with the widest range of villas and the best spa on the island. Luxury Suites and Boutique are the classic Oia caldera addresses, where the villas are made for couples and small families.

Tell me who’s coming and what kind of trip you want, and I’ll tell you which hotel, and which villa, fits.

How I’d choose

Which Andronis villa, for which trip

  • A big family or two families: the Eden Villa or the Four Bedroom Villa at Arcadia, with the kids club down the path.
  • A wellness trip or a longer stay: one of the three-bedroom villas at Concept, close to the Kallos Spa.
  • A honeymoon or anniversary: the Sunset Villas at Luxury Suites, with dinner at Lycabettus.
  • First time in Santorini, small family: the Two Bedroom Villa at Boutique or Luxury Suites, right in Oia.

Before you ask

The questions I get

What is the largest villa at Andronis?

The Eden Villa at Andronis Arcadia: three storeys, 600 square meters (6,458 sq ft), an infinity pool and room for up to 12 guests, in a secluded corner of the resort facing the sunset.

Which Andronis villa is best for families?

I’d start at Andronis Arcadia. Every room has a private pool, there’s a real kids club, and the Eden Villa (up to 12), Four Bedroom Villa (up to 8) and Three Bedroom Villa (up to 6) cover most family sizes. At Concept, the Three Bedroom Villa with Garden View sleeps up to 7.

Which Andronis villas have caldera views?

The villas at Andronis Luxury Suites and Andronis Boutique in Oia sit on the caldera, and most of the Concept villas face the caldera and the sunset. The Arcadia villas face the sunset over the open sea.

Do all the Andronis villas have private pools?

Yes. Every villa in the collection comes with a private pool, an infinity pool, or a pool and hot tub.

Which Andronis villa is best for a honeymoon or anniversary?

The Sunset Villas, Aristotelis and Socrates, at Andronis Luxury Suites: infinity pools, caldera views and Lycabettus a few steps away. White House is the other one I’d look at for two.

How much do the Andronis villas cost?

Rates depend on the villa, the season and the length of stay, so ask me for your dates and group size and I’ll come back with the right options across all four hotels.

What do I get booking through you?

I have a preferred partnership with Andronis, so my clients receive Fora perks on eligible stays, like daily breakfast, a space-available upgrade and a hotel credit, and rates are identical to booking direct.
