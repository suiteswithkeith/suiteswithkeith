Why Paros works for a villa

A private base on the island I keep thinking about

  • Naousa for dinner. The harbor comes alive after sunset, with the boutiques and a long dinner on the waterfront.
  • Beaches and coves. Kolymbithres’ granite coves and lunch at a beach club are easy days out from the house.
  • Villages. A sunset stroll through Lefkes, the island’s hill village.
  • Boat days. Seeing the Cyclades from the water is the upgrade every villa group thanks me for later.
  • Easy island-hopping. Paros and Santorini is one of my favorite pairings, and Paros also connects easily to Milos and the rest of the Cyclades.

Before you ask

The questions I get

What is the best villa on Paros?

Jupiter. When I put together the awards edit for my Greece Collection, it took the Paros category outright and I named it Best Paros Villa. I’ve toured it in person.

How many bedrooms does Jupiter have?

Five, which makes it the right size for a multi-family trip, a multigenerational group or a celebration with friends.

Is a villa better than a hotel on Paros?

For a family or group, often yes. A five-bedroom villa gives you privacy and a shared home base that a row of hotel suites can’t, and Paros is built for the villa format: Naousa’s restaurants, the beaches and boat days all work beautifully from a private house.

Why Paros for a villa trip?

Paros is my pick for the best island in Greece for return visitors, and the island I keep thinking about. It has great beaches, Naousa for dinner, villages like Lefkes for a sunset walk, and easy ferry connections to the rest of the Cyclades.

How far ahead should we book?

Villas book differently than hotels. Peak weeks go early and minimum stays apply, so talk to me before you lock flights.

What do you arrange for a villa stay?

The parts a concierge desk would normally handle: staffing, provisioning, boat days, dinner reservations, transfers and car rental, all planned around the house.

What if we’d rather stay at a hotel?

My hotel picks on Paros are Vione and Andronis Minois, and my Cosme vs. Parīlio piece covers the design-hotel route.
