Hassler or Hotel de la Ville?

Next-door neighbors at the top of the Spanish Steps, with the same crowds outside. The difference is style, the rooftop, and whether you want a spa this year. Here is how I would choose.

Visited both · October 2026

Choose Hassler

If you want traditional grandeur and a Michelin star

A very nice, very traditional luxury hotel right at the top of the Steps, with a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof and a beautiful one in the courtyard.

The question behind the question

Same Steps, same crowds. Do you want tradition or color?

The Hassler is very traditional, and that’s the appeal. The dining is the highlight, with a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof.

Hotel de la Ville is next door, with colorful, very Roman interiors and Cielo, a rooftop bar and restaurant with views all over the city. It also has a spa now, while the Hassler’s opens in 2027.

If you love grand-hotel style and a serious dinner, pick the Hassler. If you want a more colorful palazzo, a rooftop bar for sunset and a spa this trip, pick the de la Ville. And if you are deciding between the Rocco Forte hotels, I prefer the de la Ville’s sister, the de Russie.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Two rooftops

The Hassler has a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof, with the rooftops of Rome laid out below you.

At Hotel de la Ville, the highlight is Cielo, the rooftop bar and restaurant across the top two floors, with views across Rome’s skyline. Come for sunset drinks.

Style and rooms

Hotel de la Ville is an 18th-century palazzo with interiors by Tommaso Ziffer: yellow walls, engravings, plaster reliefs and black and white floors. There are 104 rooms and suites, some with terraces and city views.

The Hassler is classic luxury for travelers who want Rome’s grand-hotel style: canopy beds, damask walls and crystal lamps, and bathrooms that open to the trees.

Spa

Hotel de la Ville has a Sicilian-inspired spa.

The Hassler is building a large new wellness and gym area that opens in 2027. Until then, if a spa and gym matter a lot, I point people to the Bulgari or the Corinthia.

The crowds

At both, you step out the door into the crowds at the top of the Steps, from morning to night. That is the trade for an address you can’t beat for being central.

If quiet matters more than the Steps, the Eden is a few minutes away on a calm residential street. At the Hassler, the courtyard restaurant is a calmer place to eat than the streets outside.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Hassler or Hotel de la Ville?

It depends on your style. The Hassler for very traditional luxury and Michelin-starred dining on the roof. Hotel de la Ville for colorful Roman interiors, the Cielo rooftop bar and a spa you can use now.

Are they really next to each other?

Yes. Both are at the top of the Spanish Steps, and the Hassler is right next door to Hotel de la Ville.

Which has the better rooftop?

For dinner, the Hassler’s Michelin-starred restaurant. For sunset drinks, Cielo at Hotel de la Ville, across the top two floors with views all over the city.

Which has a spa?

Hotel de la Ville, with a Sicilian-inspired spa. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027.

Is there a better Rocco Forte option?

For me, yes: Hotel de Russie, the de la Ville’s sister a few minutes away. Its large courtyard, renovated rooms and spa with an indoor pool made it the stronger stay.
