Head-to-head · Napa

Meadowood or Stanly Ranch?

My #01 in wine country against the valley’s boldest design statement. One is the benchmark I measure every other Napa stay against; the other is the prettiest argument for choosing architecture over vineyards. Years of repeat stays inform both takes.

#01 and #06 on my Napa & Sonoma ranking · repeat stays at both

Choose Meadowood

If you want to be taken care of

Service at Aman standards — the single biggest separator in wine country. Around 40 spacious, private cottages tucked into a wooded estate above St. Helena, and there really isn’t a bad room to be had.

Choose Stanly Ranch

If design and wellness are the trip

Possibly the most architecturally striking hotel in wine country, with arguably the prettiest spa in the valley, a large fire-pit terrace on every room, and Bear — one of the more interesting restaurants in Napa right now.

Two practical notes: about 90% of Meadowood’s rooms aren’t fully accessible — buggies are available for mobility concerns — and booking it through a Virtuoso partner adds a credit, often $500, that meaningfully changes the math on a multi-night stay. At Stanly Ranch, terrace privacy varies by room — I’ll steer you to the best-sited ones.

The question behind the question

The benchmark, most of the time.

There’s something about Meadowood that’s hard to put your finger on — Aman-quality service, a real sense of place, thoughtful turndown treats each evening. Every time I go, I feel well taken care of, and I can’t say that about every property on my list. That’s why it holds #01 after years of repeat stays across the valley.

Stanly Ranch is genuinely beautiful — but the location doesn’t quite deliver the classic wine-country feeling, and I don’t find myself wanting to go back as often as the hotels above it. If the vineyard postcard is the point of the trip, that’s Meadowood. If the spa and the architecture are the point, Stanly makes its case.
