Head-to-head · Oia, Santorini

Andronis Boutique or Canaves Oia Suites?

These are #01 and #02 on my Santorini ranking, minutes apart in Oia, and the comparison I’m asked to settle most on the island. I toured both on the same trip. The decision comes down to which room you’ve been picturing.

Toured same trip · #01 and #02 on my Santorini ranking · both preferred partners

Choose Andronis Boutique

If you’re dreaming of the cave room

Authentic cave-style suites, recently renovated — marble, wooden arches, surprisingly bright interiors — with a plunge pool or jacuzzi in every single room. These are the rooms people actually picture when they book Santorini.

Choose Canaves Oia Suites

If you want the caldera, perfected

Completely renovated, and it blew me away everywhere I looked — the finishes, the water features, the landscaping. It sits higher on the Caldera than most, which buys something genuinely rare on Santorini: privacy.

The question behind the question

If the River Pool Suite is available for your dates, stop deliberating.

Canaves Oia Suites was the best hotel I saw for first-time visitors to Santorini, and I’d be hard pressed to recommend anything else first for the classic caldera-luxury experience. Complete privacy doesn’t exist anywhere on the Caldera, but Canaves does a particularly good job of engineering it — set higher than most, with coverings above every room’s outdoor space.

But Andronis Boutique’s rooms are the real thing — genuine cave architecture with a water feature in every room, at rates that usually land a few hundred a night lower. Between the top Oia properties you really can’t go wrong; this one is about the picture in your head. And if you land on Boutique, book dinner at Lycabettus the moment you confirm the room.
