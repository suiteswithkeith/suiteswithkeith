Head-to-head · Mykonos

Kalesma or Cali?

#07 and #08 on my Greece top 10, and the definitive Mykonos style question: laid-back Cycladic design above Ornos Bay, or independent polish on the quiet east side. Pick your flavor of Mykonos.

#07 and #08 on my Greece top 10 · both toured in person · both preferred partners

Choose Kalesma

If the mood is the point

Cycladic minimalism in dark wood and marble that just works, set above Ornos Bay and central to everything — without any of the Mykonos Town chaos. The fire pit overlooking Delos at sunset is the memory that outlasts the trip.

Choose Cali

If space, privacy and the gym win

An independent stunner on the island’s quieter eastern side, with one of the largest infinity pools I saw in Greece, plunge pools that actually feel private — and the best hotel gym I have ever seen, anywhere.

Both are preferred partners — same rate as booking direct, with the extras layered on. Traveling with kids on Mykonos? Cali on the quieter east side is the pick I give families. And whichever you book, Pere Ubu at Kalesma is one of the island’s best sunset tables — reserve it the moment your trip is confirmed.

The question behind the question

Cali is flashier on paper — Kalesma won me over.

Kalesma’s laid-back beauty is what stayed with me: a glass of wine in the jacuzzi at golden hour with the bay below, the fire pit at sunset before dinner, and a design language unlike anything else on the island. Dining is pleasant rather than the headline — and Mykonos’s remarkable restaurant scene is minutes away.

But Cali is a genuinely remarkable property I’d happily return to — refined, personal service, staff who greet you by name, and I’d stay again just for that gym. For fitness-minded travelers and privacy seekers, it settles the question by itself.
