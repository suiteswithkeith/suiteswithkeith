The Villas at Four Seasons Mykonos

Two private pool villas above a private beach, with Four Seasons service behind them. One of the best ways to bring a family to Mykonos.

Ask about the villas Read about the hotel

I have a close relationship with the Four Seasons Mykonos team and have sent them clients, and the villas are what I point families to first.

Four Seasons Hotel Mykonos opened in 2026 on a hillside above Kalo Livadi Bay, built like a Mykonian village with its own private beach below and two infinity pools facing the Aegean. Most Mykonos villa rentals leave you on your own for service, food and logistics. Here you get a private house and a full Four Seasons around it.

There are just two villas, both with living and dining rooms and a private pool. The Syros Three-Bedroom Villa takes up to six adults, or four adults and two children, and it’s built for entertaining: an infinity pool with a private cabana, sunbeds and an outdoor shower, an outdoor living room with a dining table, bar and fireplace, an indoor living room with its own dining table, and three full bathrooms plus a guest powder room. The Two-Bedroom Sea-View Villa takes up to four, with two full bathrooms (each with double sinks, a separate shower and an oval tub by the window), a guest powder room, floor-to-ceiling glass doors and a terrace with a daybed. With only two villas, they go early for peak summer weeks.

Why it works for families

A private house, a private beach

  • A private beach. Beach days on Kalo Livadi Bay without the beach-club scramble.
  • Your own pool. Both villas have a private pool, and Syros has an infinity pool with its own cabana.
  • A Four Seasons kids club. The kids have their own program, and the parents get the beach and the spa.
  • Built for families. The Syros Villa sleeps four adults and two kids, and cribs are available in both villas.
  • Four Seasons service. Still rare on Mykonos, and it matters most with kids or a group.
  • Room to grow. Even the entry-level Deluxe Sea-View Rooms are around 500 square feet, so extended family can stay nearby.

Before you ask

The questions I get

What is the best villa in Mykonos for families?

The villas at Four Seasons Hotel Mykonos are where I’d start a family. You get a private house with your own pool, a private beach on Kalo Livadi Bay, and Four Seasons service behind you, which is still rare on Mykonos.

How many villas does Four Seasons Mykonos have?

Two: the Syros Three-Bedroom Villa (177 m², up to 6 adults, or 4 adults and 2 children) and the Two-Bedroom Sea-View Villa (120 m², up to 4 adults, or 2 adults and 2 children). Both have a private pool.

Does Four Seasons Mykonos have a kids club?

Yes. There’s a Four Seasons kids club on property, which is a big part of why I send families here: the kids have their own program while the parents enjoy the private beach and the pools.

Does Four Seasons Mykonos have a private beach?

Yes. The resort sits on a hillside above Kalo Livadi Bay on the south coast, with its own private beach below and two infinity pools facing the Aegean.

What if the villas are booked?

Even the entry-level Deluxe Sea-View Rooms are around 500 square feet, large enough for a family of three. For a bigger family I’ll look at suites and connecting options, or at the Fouquet’s Mykonos villas.

How far is it from Mykonos Town?

About 20 minutes by car. I arrange drivers for dinners in Chora and private transfers from the airport or port.

How much do the villas cost?

Four Seasons prices dynamically, so rates depend on your dates and availability. With only two villas, peak weeks go early, so ask me as soon as you know your dates.
