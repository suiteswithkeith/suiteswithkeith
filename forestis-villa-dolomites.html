The Forestis Villa

The ultimate private villa in the Dolomites: five bedrooms, a team of 14, a private pool and spa, and the Odle on the horizon. Children welcome.

Ask about the villa Read my Forestis review

I toured the villa during my stay at Forestis, and it was truly stunning.

Set at the entrance to Forestis, high above Bressanone, the villa is a private world within one of the finest wellness retreats in the Alps. It sleeps ten across five bedrooms, with a team of 14 staff dedicated just to the villa, so it feels like having your own hotel within the hotel.

You have your own pool, your own spa with a sauna, steam room and cold plunge, and a fully equipped gym, and it’s incredibly private all around. When you want more, the hotel’s restaurants, its spa and the best view of the Odle in the Dolomites are steps away.

One detail matters a lot for families: the Forestis hotel is for guests aged 14 and over, but children are welcome in the villa. For a milestone birthday, a family celebration or a group of friends, there’s nothing like it in the Dolomites.

Before you ask

The questions I get

How many people does the Forestis Villa sleep?

Up to ten guests, across five bedrooms.

How much does the Forestis Villa cost?

It starts at about €25,000 a night. Rates vary by season, so ask me for your dates.

Are children allowed at the Forestis Villa?

Yes. The Forestis hotel itself is for guests aged 14 and over, but children are welcome in the villa, which makes it the way to bring a family with younger kids.

What’s included at the villa?

A dedicated team of 14 staff, a private pool, a private spa with a sauna, steam room and cold plunge, and a fully equipped gym. Guests can also enjoy the hotel’s restaurants and spa.

How do you get to Forestis?

From Venice, about three and a half hours by train via Verona to Bressanone, then 25 minutes by car. There’s also a direct train from Munich, Innsbruck is the closest major airport, and a helicopter is an option. I arrange all of it for the whole group.
