The question behind the question

A villa for two, or the grand estate?

Passalacqua is unlike anywhere else in the world: a 1787 villa that’s meant to feel like your own home, with 24 rooms and no outside guests. It was recently named the world’s best hotel, it holds Three Michelin Keys, and it’s the best honeymoon hotel on Lake Como. Worth every euro if the moment calls for it.

Villa d’Este is the institution Passalacqua gets measured against. The gardens and the villa on the lakefront are beautiful, lunch was fantastic and the service is polished and warm. It has eight clay courts, private villas on the estate for groups, and a town at the gate.

Passalacqua sits above Villa d’Este in my ranking, and for a honeymoon or a milestone it’s the one. For a tennis trip with friends, a multigenerational group, or anyone who wants a town to walk into, choose Villa d’Este.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Two kinds of history

Passalacqua feels like being a guest at an 18th-century estate. It’s whimsical, with antiques the family collected over time and lots of wicker, and the staff remember your name. I loved it, though it isn’t my personal style; with two restaurants and a quiet village, I’d want a livelier base after a couple of nights.

Villa d’Este is the lake’s original grand hotel, a renaissance estate whose gardens, mosaics and floating pool have defined Como luxury for generations. It’s my pick on the lake for travelers who want history with their luxury, and it’s livelier than its reputation.

For tennis: one iconic court, or eight

Passalacqua’s sunken clay court sits in the terraced gardens above the lake, framed by cypress and the Alps. It’s one of the most iconic courts in the world and #04 on my hotel tennis ranking.

Villa d’Este has eight century-old clay courts along the water, #06 on the same list. Paired with one of the estate’s private villas, it’s the best hotel on the lake for a tennis trip with friends.

Rooms and views

At Passalacqua, the Junior Suite Al Lago is the one; it opens right toward the water. Rooms in the Palazzo and the lakeside rooms are generously sized, while rooms elsewhere can be small, and because this is a terraced garden property, most rooms see the lake in the distance. With so few rooms, it’s the single hardest confirmation on the lake.

At Villa d’Este, recent client stays have been very positive. I’m told they plan to reduce the number of rooms and modernize, which will put it in the same tier as Passalacqua, Il Sereno and Grand Hotel Tremezzo.

Dining, the spa and the village

Passalacqua has two restaurants, and only guests can use them or the spa. The spa is Moroccan-inspired, with vaulted ceilings, old stone columns and peacock wicker chairs, and a tunnel runs down to the lower gardens. Moltrasio is quiet, so plan a few dinners across the lake by boat; I set them up. One small note: clients who stayed recently found the pool service slow.

Villa d’Este’s dining was fantastic. Cernobbio is on the town side of the lake, so restaurants and shops are within walking distance, with easy access to the town of Como and the villa circuit by boat.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Passalacqua or Villa d'Este?

For a honeymoon or milestone, Passalacqua; it’s the best honeymoon hotel on Lake Como and sits above Villa d’Este in my ranking. Villa d’Este is better for tennis trips, multigenerational groups and anyone who wants a town at the door.

Can I visit Passalacqua without staying there?

Generally, no. Passalacqua’s restaurants and spa are for hotel guests only. If you’re a client of mine staying nearby, I can sometimes arrange a lunch or a tour.

Which is better for tennis?

For a group, Villa d’Este, with eight clay courts. For one unforgettable court, Passalacqua’s sunken clay court above the lake is #04 on my tennis ranking.

Which is better for families?

Villa d’Este suits multigenerational groups. Passalacqua is best for honeymoons, milestones and two couples traveling together. For a family trip, I’d also look at Grand Hotel Tremezzo.

How far are they from Milan?

Both are about an hour by private car. Passalacqua is in Moltrasio and Villa d’Este in Cernobbio, both near the town of Como. I arrange the car, and private boats once you’re there.

Should I pair Passalacqua with another hotel?

Yes, for a honeymoon. Two nights at Passalacqua, then a ten-minute boat across the lake to Il Sereno, is the trip I’d book. Here’s the pairing.
