The question behind the question

Do you want quiet, or the scene?

Le Sirenuse is a really cool, lively property in the middle of Positano. That is either a selling point or a sore point, depending on who you are and what you are looking for.

If you want privacy, go for Il San Pietro. If you want to be able to walk into town, which is busy, pick Le Sirenuse.

It is very easy to get into town from Il San Pietro, so being outside it is not really a minus. It is more of a plus: you get more privacy and quiet.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms: pick your view

At Il San Pietro, every room has a sea view and is impeccably kept: nautical, bright, blues and whites and tiles. Some rooms face the sunrise, others the sunset. Sunsets are hard to find on the Amalfi Coast, so tell me which you want.

At Le Sirenuse, I would only stay in a true sea-view room or suite. The rest either have courtyard views, or the pool view is right on top of the pool, and it is loud.

Beach clubs and the afternoon sun

Il San Pietro’s beach club is on the sunset side. In the fall, the best time to visit, swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm at most hotels on the coast. Not here.

Le Sirenuse has a new beach club, with complimentary boats there at 10:30 and 12:30. The hotel also runs a sunset cruise, but book it in advance. I learned that the hard way.

Service and atmosphere

Service at Le Sirenuse is very formal, and you come to see and be seen. The artwork around the property is stunning, the lookout over the dome is truly stunning, and the breakfast is out of this world.

At Il San Pietro, no one can look down on you, and the whole property is very private, so you will only see other guests.

Getting there

A boat transfer from Naples or Salerno is practical and enjoyable at Il San Pietro and Villa Treville, and I arrange the private transfer to Le Sirenuse. The nearest helipad is in Scala, close to Ravello. Reach out and I will handle all of it.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Il San Pietro or Le Sirenuse?

For me, Il San Pietro, if privacy and quiet matter to you. Pick Le Sirenuse if you want to walk into town. Getting into Positano from Il San Pietro is very easy on the free boat and car shuttles, so being outside town is not really a minus. It is more of a plus.

Which has the better beach club?

Il San Pietro’s is on the sunset side, so you keep the sun on the water late into the afternoon. Le Sirenuse has a new beach club, with complimentary boats there at 10:30 and 12:30.

Which room should I book at each?

At Il San Pietro, every room has a sea view; tell me whether you want the sunrise or the sunset side, and go Signature or Prestige for more space. At Le Sirenuse, only a true sea-view room or suite. The others face the courtyard or sit right on top of the pool, which is loud.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

Il San Pietro. It is #06 in The Fifteen, my honeymoon shortlist, and the privacy is the point.

Which is better for being in town?

Le Sirenuse. It is right in the middle of Positano, and you come to Le Sirenuse to see and be seen.
