The Villas at Fouquet’s Mykonos

Four private villas on the south coast, from two bedrooms to seven, and just another level compared to the rest of Mykonos.

Ask about the villas Read my Fouquet’s review

I toured the villas in May 2026. They’re modern, private and absolutely stunning, honestly another level compared to other Mykonos offerings.

Fouquet’s is my pick for Best New Resort in Greece. It brings the energy of Mykonos’ south coast together with the comfort of a true luxury resort, and I had the chance to tour it while it was still under construction, so returning after it opened was especially rewarding.

The villas are where it really separates itself. Most Mykonos villa rentals leave you on your own for food, service and logistics. Here you have a private villa with ZUMA for dinner, an underground sports court, polished service and Paraga Beach and Scorpios a walk away.

How I’d choose

Which villa, for which trip

  • A milestone birthday or a big group of friends: Paradiso, the largest, built for celebrations, with a gated private entrance, a big pool over the sea, an outdoor kitchen and poolside BBQs or a DJ set on request.
  • Two or three families together: Delos, the signature villa, with a private elevator between its two levels and every bedroom opening onto the pool terrace or a shaded patio by the sea.
  • Privacy above everything: Rock, at the far end of the estate with the sea on both sides and a path down to the water. It’s being upgraded from three bedrooms to five for 2027.
  • A couple, two couples, or a small family: Cyclades, the two-bedroom at the hotel’s furthest point, with a pool running the length of the villa.

Before you ask

The questions I get

What is the best villa in Mykonos?

For me, the villas at Fouquet’s Mykonos. I toured them in May 2026, and they’re modern, private and absolutely stunning, honestly another level compared to other Mykonos offerings. You get a private villa with a full luxury resort around it.

How many villas does Fouquet’s Mykonos have?

Four for 2027: Paradiso and Delos (seven bedrooms each, up to 14 guests), Rock (five bedrooms, new for 2027) and Cyclades (two bedrooms, up to 4 guests).

What is the largest villa at Fouquet’s Mykonos?

The Paradiso Villa: seven bedrooms and 12,356 square feet across two levels, for up to 14 guests. Every bedroom faces the sea for the sunrise over Paradise Bay, the master has its own jacuzzi, and there’s a massage room, a gated private driveway, a large pool over the sea and an outdoor kitchen. Poolside BBQs and DJ sets can be arranged on request.

Is Fouquet’s Mykonos good for a group of friends or a family?

Yes. The seven-bedroom villas sleep up to 14, which works for a milestone birthday, a multi-family trip or a group of friends, and you have ZUMA, an underground sports court and Paraga Beach on your doorstep.

What is the best villa at Fouquet’s Mykonos?

The Delos Villa is the hotel’s signature: seven sea-view bedrooms for up to 14 guests across two levels with a private elevator, and a long private pool suspended over the open sea. A 24/7 butler and dedicated concierge are included, and a private chef or a sunset reception can be arranged on request.

Which Fouquet’s villa is the most private?

The Rock Villa. It sits at the far end of the estate, bordered by the sea on both sides with views over Paraga and Paradiso bays, and a small path through the rocks leads straight down to the water. It comes with a 24/7 butler, a dedicated concierge and access to ROKA Beach Club, and a chef or a private sunset reception can be arranged on request.

Where is Fouquet’s Mykonos?

On the south coast at Paraga, within walking distance of Paraga Beach and Scorpios, so you get the energy of the south coast with the comfort of a true luxury resort.

How much do the villas cost?

Rates depend on the villa, the season and the length of stay, so ask me for your dates and group size and I’ll come back with the right villa.
