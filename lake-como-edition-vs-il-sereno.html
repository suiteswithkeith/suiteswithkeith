Head-to-head · Lake Como

The EDITION or Il Sereno?

Lake Como’s two modern hotels, for travelers who would rather skip the frescoes. The EDITION is the newer, livelier one in the middle of the lake and the best value at this level. Il Sereno is the quieter, more private one near Como, where every room sees the lake.

Stayed at both · October 2026

Choose the EDITION

If you want energy and value

The lake’s newest big opening, in Cadenabbia next to Tremezzo. It’s contemporary, social and strong value for the address, and the Bellagio ferry leaves a few minutes away.

Choose Il Sereno

If you want privacy

A small, quiet hotel in Torno by Patricia Urquiola, with a heated pool, a boathouse hot tub, its own Riva boats and almost no day visitors. It costs more, and it feels like it.

The question behind the question

A scene, or a hideaway?

The EDITION is the smart modern choice. It’s fresh and social, it sits in the middle of the lake where everything is close, and it costs noticeably less than the lake’s marquee names. That leaves more of your budget for the boats, villas and restaurants that make a Como trip.

Il Sereno is the romantic modern choice. I stayed this month and couldn’t find a single fault: the design, the heated pool, the private hot tub in the old boathouse, the sheets. Because Torno is tiny and the hotel is reachable only by its guests or by boat, it never feels busy.

If you want to be out exploring every day, choose the EDITION. If you want the hotel to be the reason you came, choose Il Sereno.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Two kinds of modern

The EDITION is contemporary and social, the way the brand is everywhere: a lively bar, a pool with a scene, and rooms that feel fresh rather than precious.

Il Sereno is modern in a quieter way. The feel is Japanese-minimalist and warm-industrial, with glass, walnut and stone, and there’s even a listening suite. It’s designed for couples who want calm.

Mid-lake or near Como?

The EDITION is in Cadenabbia, a couple of minutes from Tremezzo and the car ferry to Bellagio, which runs about every half hour and takes ten minutes. If you want to explore the villages, it’s the better base.

Il Sereno is in Torno, near the southern end of the lake and about an hour from Milan. It’s quicker to reach, and the hotel’s own boats make dinners across the lake easy.

Which room to book

At the EDITION, the decision is view versus price: the garden-facing Superior rooms are the value play, and the direct lake-view rooms are worth pricing against them. There’s a suite tier above.

At Il Sereno, every room sees the lake, but entry rooms get a partial view, some through the trees. The Grand Suite is where it gets special.

Arriving at the EDITION

The lake road from Como is slow. I usually arrange a car from Milan to the water in Como and then about 30 minutes by private boat to Cadenabbia. It’s a far better way to arrive.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Lake Como EDITION or Il Sereno?

The EDITION is better value and better located for exploring, with a livelier atmosphere. Il Sereno is more private, quieter and more romantic, with lake views from every room. Choose by whether you want a scene or a hideaway.

Where is the Lake Como EDITION?

In Cadenabbia, in the middle of the lake on the west shore, a couple of minutes from Tremezzo and the car ferry to Bellagio.

Is the EDITION cheaper than Il Sereno?

Generally, yes. The EDITION is the best value on the lake at this level, and Il Sereno’s better rooms are priced close to the lake’s top hotels. Ask me for your dates and I’ll price both.

Which is quieter?

Il Sereno. Torno is a tiny village, and the hotel can only be reached by guests or by boat, so there are almost no day visitors.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

Il Sereno, for most couples. For an even bigger honeymoon, pair it with Passalacqua across the water. See my Passalacqua + Il Sereno pairing.
