Head-to-head · Lake Como

Grand Hotel Tremezzo or Passalacqua?

Same family, two very different hotels. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is the lively Art Nouveau grand hotel in the middle of the lake. Passalacqua is the 24-room villa they opened in 2022, which was named the world’s best hotel. One is a resort; the other is a private home.

Toured both · October 2026

Choose Grand Hotel Tremezzo

If you want energy and the view

A 1910 Art Nouveau landmark in Tremezzo facing Bellagio, with several pools including one floating on the lake. It’s livelier, it welcomes families, and the mid-lake villages are minutes away by boat.

Choose Passalacqua

If you want the pinnacle

Only 24 rooms in an 18th-century villa in Moltrasio, with frescoes, silk walls and terraced gardens to the water. It’s quieter, more intimate and more expensive, and it’s the one I save for the trips that call for it.

The question behind the question

A grand hotel, or a villa of your own?

These two share an owner and a standard of service, but they answer different questions. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is a proper grand hotel: busy in a good way, with pools, restaurants and the best view on the lake, straight across to Bellagio. It’s the easy choice for families and for anyone who wants to explore the lake by boat.

Passalacqua is a villa with 24 rooms. It feels like you’ve been invited to stay at someone’s estate, and it’s the most celebrated hotel in Europe right now. For a honeymoon or a milestone, it’s the one.

They’re also about half an hour apart by boat, so you can stay at one and have lunch at the other.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Why the same family runs both

The De Santis family has run Grand Hotel Tremezzo for decades and opened Passalacqua in 2022. You can feel the shared standard in the service, but they built Passalacqua as the opposite of a grand hotel: small, private and run like a home.

Which one for a honeymoon?

Passalacqua, if the budget allows. It’s more intimate and more romantic, and the 24 rooms mean it never feels busy. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is a great honeymoon choice too if you want more going on, more pools and a lower rate, and its location makes day trips to Bellagio and Varenna effortless.

For a longer honeymoon, you could also pair Passalacqua with Il Sereno across the lake. Here’s that Passalacqua + Il Sereno pairing.

Location: mid-lake or near Como

Tremezzo is in the central section of the lake, so Bellagio, Varenna and Villa del Balbianello are short boat rides away. Moltrasio is near the southern end, about ten minutes from Como, which makes Passalacqua quicker to reach from Milan but further from the mid-lake villages.

For Grand Hotel Tremezzo, I usually arrange a car to Como and then about 30 minutes by private boat, rather than 45 minutes to an hour on the lake road.

Which is better with children?

Grand Hotel Tremezzo. It’s built for families in a way Passalacqua, with its 24 rooms and private-home feel, simply isn’t.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Is Grand Hotel Tremezzo owned by the same family as Passalacqua?

Yes. The De Santis family runs Grand Hotel Tremezzo and opened Passalacqua in 2022.

Which is better, Grand Hotel Tremezzo or Passalacqua?

Passalacqua is the more exclusive, intimate and expensive of the two, and the stronger honeymoon choice. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is better for families, for exploring the lake and for travelers who want a livelier hotel.

How far apart are Grand Hotel Tremezzo and Passalacqua?

Both are on the west shore. By private boat it’s about half an hour; by road it’s longer on the narrow lake road.

Is Grand Hotel Tremezzo cheaper than Passalacqua?

Generally, yes. Passalacqua is at the very top of the lake’s price range. Ask me for your dates and I’ll price both.

Which is better with kids?

Grand Hotel Tremezzo, with several pools and a family-friendly atmosphere.
