The Villas at Cali Mykonos

Four private villas with heated seawater pools on the quiet east side, from two bedrooms up to the four-bedroom Villa Calliope.

Ask about the villas Read my Cali review

I really loved Cali. It’s #08 in my Greece Top 10, and the Mykonos hotel I send families to.

Cali is one of Greece’s incredible independent hotels, on the island’s quieter east side, with refined service, one of the largest infinity pools I saw in Greece and the best hotel gym I’ve ever seen. The private pools here actually feel private, which is a rarity in Greece.

The villas take that further: your own heated seawater pools, terraces and outdoor kitchen, with a beach club, two private boats and a 2-to-1 staff-to-guest ratio behind you. It’s far from the party scene on purpose, and Cali’s boats get you to Scorpios and Nammos when you want it.

How I’d choose

Which villa, for which trip

  • Two families, or several generations together: the Four-Bedroom Calliope. The separate one-bedroom residence, with its own pool, is perfect for grandparents or a couple who want their own space.
  • A family of four or five, or three couples: the Three-Bedroom Calliope, with two pools and an outdoor kitchen for dinners in.
  • Two couples who want room to spread out: the Grand Two-Bedroom, with a bathroom each and then some.
  • A couple of couples, or a small family: the Two-Bedroom Villa, with its own sea-view pool.

Before you ask

The questions I get

What is the best villa at Cali Mykonos?

Villa Calliope, the resort’s premier residence. The four-bedroom version adds a separate one-bedroom residence, for 3,767 square feet inside, three heated seawater pools and four terraces. The three-bedroom version has two seawater pools, three terraces and an outdoor kitchen.

How many villas does Cali Mykonos have?

Four villa types: the Two-Bedroom Villa, the Grand Two-Bedroom Villa, and Villa Calliope, which books as either three or four bedrooms. Every one has a private heated seawater pool and a sea view.

Is Cali Mykonos good for families?

Yes. It’s the Mykonos hotel I send families to. It’s on the quieter east side, children get amenities on arrival, babysitting is available, and the villas give you real space and private pools.

Where is Cali Mykonos?

On the quieter east side of the island, far from the party scene by design. Cali runs scheduled shuttles to Mykonos Old Town, and its own boats get you to Scorpios, Nammos and the other beach clubs quickly.

What’s included with a Cali villa?

Private airport or port transfers, à la carte breakfast, scheduled shuttles to Old Town, 24/7 WhatsApp service, Clefs d’Or concierge and pre-arrival preferences, down to your pool temperature. A butler, in-villa dining, a private chef, a personal trainer and babysitting are available on request.

Are the villa pools heated?

Yes. Every Cali villa pool is a heated seawater pool, and you can set your preferred temperature before you arrive.

What is there to do at Cali?

The Missoni Resort Club for beach days, two Axopar boats for transfers and excursions to Delos and the other islands, the Terra Athletic Club with reformer Pilates, padel and pickleball, and the Terra Spa. The gym is the best hotel gym I’ve ever seen.

How much do the Cali villas cost?

Rates depend on the villa, the season and the length of stay. Send me your dates and group size and I’ll come back with the right villa and the exact rate.

Does booking a Cali villa through you cost more?

No. Cali is a preferred partner, so you get the same rate as booking direct, with Fora perks on eligible stays layered on, and me planning the boat days, dinners and the rest of Mykonos around it.
