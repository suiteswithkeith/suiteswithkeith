Head-to-head · Rome

Bulgari or Hotel de la Ville?

Both have a rooftop bar and both put you on busy streets. One gives you a junior suite’s worth of room at the entry rate; the other gives you the top of the Spanish Steps. Here is how I would choose.

Visited both · October 2026

Choose Bulgari

If you want space and a scene

Spectacular. Base rooms the size of junior suites elsewhere, marble and mosaics, the biggest gym I saw in Rome, and more bars and restaurants than I could count.

The question behind the question

Is it the room that matters most, or the address?

At the Bulgari, the room is the story. The base rooms are very large, so you know you’re getting a solid room size at these rates. In central Rome, that matters.

At Hotel de la Ville, the address is the story: right at the top of the Spanish Steps, with Cielo on the roof and views all over the city. It was nice, though I don’t have a ton to say about it.

If you want space, a gym and a hotel full of places to eat and drink, pick the Bulgari. If waking up at the top of the Steps is the point, pick the de la Ville, or look at its sister, the de Russie, which I prefer.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms

The Bulgari’s base rooms are what other hotels would sell as a deluxe room or a junior suite, with lots of marble and incredible mosaics in the bathrooms. You often don’t need to upgrade to get space.

Hotel de la Ville has 104 rooms and suites in an 18th-century palazzo, some with terraces and city views, and some looking out to the umbrella pines.

Rooftops and dining

Both have a rooftop. The Bulgari’s is a beautiful bar and pizza restaurant, with another bar on the floor below it and more besides. Downstairs, a classic Roman trattoria serves €18 pastas.

At Hotel de la Ville, Cielo spans the top two floors, with views across Rome’s skyline. Mosaico, the main restaurant, opens onto the courtyard, and the Julep cocktail bar is there for an evening drink.

Style

Hotel de la Ville’s interiors, by Tommaso Ziffer, are colorful and very Roman: yellow walls, engravings, plaster reliefs and black and white floors.

The Bulgari is very modern and lively, almost like an EDITION, but much more sophisticated.

Spa and gym

The Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome, massive and with serious equipment, plus a spa.

Hotel de la Ville has a Sicilian-inspired spa. For a pool, its sister, the de Russie, has one indoors.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Bulgari or Hotel de la Ville?

It depends on what you value. The Bulgari for very large base rooms, a lively scene and the biggest gym in Rome. Hotel de la Ville for the top of the Spanish Steps and the Cielo rooftop.

Which has the bigger rooms?

The Bulgari. Its base rooms are what other hotels would call deluxe rooms or even junior suites. Hotel de la Ville has some rooms with terraces and city views.

Which has the better rooftop?

They are different. Hotel de la Ville’s Cielo is a bar and restaurant across the top two floors with views all over the city. The Bulgari’s rooftop is a bar and pizza restaurant, with another bar just below.

Is either one quiet?

No. At both you step out into busy streets, and the Bulgari is lively inside too. For quiet, the Eden is a short walk from Hotel de la Ville on a calm residential street.

Should I look at the de Russie instead of the de la Ville?

If you like the idea of a Rocco Forte, yes. Hotel de Russie is the de la Ville’s sister, a few minutes away, and its courtyard, renovated rooms and spa with an indoor pool made it the stronger stay for me.
