Head-to-head · The One&Only Flags In Greece

Kea Island or Aesthesis?

One&Only planted two flags in Greece within a year of each other, and they answer very different questions: Kea Island is the all-villa resort on the quiet island an hour from Athens; Aesthesis is the Riviera beach scene with the Acropolis still in reach. Here’s how I match clients to each.

The two One&Only flags in Greece · both on my booking list · both bookable with my perks

Choose Kea Island

If the island is the trip

Sixty-three cliffside villas above Vroskopos Bay, every one with a private infinity pool, on an island the ferry crowds skip — plus real resort depth: Bond Beach Club, a three-floor spa, Club One’s courts, clubs for kids and teens. You arrive by speedboat, you stay put, and the world stays behind.

Choose Aesthesis

If the city stays in play

The Riviera beach coast south of Athens — home of the Mãnko Beach cabana day I write into my sample honeymoon itinerary — with the Acropolis still a drive away, not a boat away. Beach mornings, city evenings, no crossing required.

A useful tiebreaker: if the itinerary already has Cycladic islands in it, Aesthesis covers the Athens chapter without adding another crossing — but if you want one island done properly instead of three done quickly, Kea is the better week. Full transparency, as always: I haven’t inspected either one yet — both are on my booking list, and my clients get my vetting call with the on-property team before arrival.

The question behind the question

Same flag, two different trips.

Kea Island is the deeper commitment and the bigger payoff — a true all-villa resort on an island most itineraries skip, with enough program depth that you never need to leave. It replaces the island-hop for people who want one perfect base where everything is on property and the pool is yours.

Aesthesis is the easy yes — Riviera beach days with the city still in play, and the Mãnko cabana afternoon that anchors my sample honeymoon itinerary. Tell me the shape of the trip — one deep island week, or Athens with a beach — and I’ll match you to the right flag, or route you through both.
