Four Seasons Firenze or Collegio alla Querce?

A historic palazzo in the heart of the city, or a bright retreat in the hills above it. Here is how I help clients choose, and how to book either one with perks you won’t get booking direct.

The short answer

Both are exceptional. The choice is really about how you want Florence to feel.

Book Four Seasons if…

You want to walk out the door and be in Florence. It’s a strong pick for a first visit, for families, and for anyone who loves history, grand gardens and polished, formal service.

Book Collegio if…

You want quiet, light and a view, with the city a scenic walk away. It’s ideal for return visitors, couples and families who want a countryside feel without giving up Florence.

In the city

Four Seasons Firenze

The classic Florence stay. A historic palazzo close to the city center, set in sprawling private gardens that feel impossible this near the Duomo. The design is old-world and the service is refined and formal, the kind of place where every detail is handled before you think to ask.

Above the city

Collegio alla Querce

A brighter, more modern take on Florence, in the hills about a 30-minute walk from the center. It’s quiet and green, with an impressive art collection and sweeping views from almost every room. The mood is a little more romantic and it draws a slightly younger crowd, while still being wonderful for families.
