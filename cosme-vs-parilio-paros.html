Head-to-head · Paros, Greece

Cosme or Parīlio?

Ten minutes apart, nothing alike, and the pair I’m asked to choose between more than any other in Greece. I toured both on the same trip, back to back. Once you know which problem is yours, the decision takes thirty seconds.

Toured back-to-back · same trip · both Fora partners

Choose Cosme

If you want to walk to dinner

A true beach during the day, Naousa’s restaurants on foot at night, and no car keys all week. Warm, colorful, social.

Choose Parīlio

If you want to be alone

Contemporary Cycladic architecture, calm interiors, and a private pool you’ll actually spend the afternoon in. Calm, architectural, private.

Both are the same group, both Fora preferred partners — upgrade on arrival where available, daily breakfast, €100 hotel credit — and Parīlio guests share Cosme’s beach club, which quietly means you never fully have to choose.

The question behind the question

For a honeymoon: Parīlio, most of the time.

Specifically a Sun Suite with a private pool — the privacy is the point, and the outdoor space means an entire afternoon without seeing anyone. But if the version of the trip you’re picturing involves walking to dinner and staying out afterward, Cosme is the better fit, and you’ll use the location every single evening.

And if you can’t choose at all, the better question is whether you should be doing two islands. The same group opened Erēma on Milos — Paros then Milos is the trip I’d actually book.
