The Italy Matrix

Fourteen places, placed honestly. Fifty places to stay, and I’d book every one of them.

By Keith Pence · Travel Advisor

If you commented ITALIA, this is the full list. Every hotel I’d put a client in across Italy, grouped by place, with a link to my review of each one.

The matrix comes first, because the right hotel starts with the right place. Lake Como and the Dolomites are both beautiful and they are completely different trips. Then the hotels: forty-nine of them, plus one that moves.

How to read it

Rustic to polished is about what the place expects of you. Rustic means a trattoria and a rental car. Polished means a concierge, a dress code, and a driver waiting outside.

Undiscovered to well-trodden is about the crowds you’ll actually meet in season, not about whether anyone has written about it.

A filled dot means I have hotels there I’d stake my name on. Those are the ten places below.
A hollow ring means I know and love the place, and I’d send you for the place itself. Ask me where I’d stay.
