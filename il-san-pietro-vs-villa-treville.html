The question behind the question

Classic, or one of a kind?

There is no wrong answer here. Il San Pietro is the classic: consistent, bright, nautical rooms, and you choose sunrise or sunset.

Villa Treville is the one-of-a-kind: every room is very different, not just a little different, and the art is so cool and eclectic, but it really works. It is maybe slightly better value.

If you know what you like, tell me, and I will tell you which one and which room.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms: consistent, or one of a kind

At Il San Pietro, every room is impeccably kept and has a sea view. Choose the sunrise or the sunset side. Base rooms are fantastic; Signature and Prestige give you more space.

At Villa Treville, every room is completely different, so the match matters. The suite with the outdoor tub and shower is a standout, with one of the coolest showers I have ever seen.

Beach clubs and boats

Il San Pietro’s beach club is on the sunset side, so in the fall you keep swimming after most of the coast loses the sun around 4:30 or 5pm. Guests also get a complimentary 2-hour yacht cruise.

Villa Treville has a beach club on the rocks, right on the water, and three stunning boats with free shuttles into Positano.

Beyond the room

Il San Pietro has the iconic tennis court, #1 in my hotel tennis court ranking, and Michelin-starred dining.

Villa Treville has a spa in an iron-and-glass garden pavilion that opens to the sea, and a gym that looks straight out over the water.

Getting there

Both are outside town, so both work beautifully by boat. A transfer from Naples or Salerno is practical and enjoyable, and the nearest helipad is in Scala, close to Ravello. Reach out and I will handle it.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Il San Pietro or Villa Treville?

For me they are the two best options in Positano, and it comes down to style. Il San Pietro is the classic, with the sunset beach club and the iconic tennis court. Villa Treville feels like a private estate, is more eclectic, and is maybe slightly better value.

Which is more private?

Both are very private. Villa Treville has probably the most private rooms in Positano. At Il San Pietro, no one can look down on you, and you will only see other guests.

Which room should I book at each?

At Il San Pietro, choose the sunrise or the sunset side; the base rooms are fantastic, and Signature or Prestige give you more space. At Villa Treville, every room is completely different, so tell me your style and I will match you. The suite with the outdoor tub and shower is a standout.

Are both outside Positano?

Yes. Both sit on the cliffs just outside town, with free shuttles in. That is where the privacy comes from.

Can I arrive by boat?

Yes, at both. A boat transfer from Naples or Salerno is practical and enjoyable. The nearest helipad is in Scala, close to Ravello. I arrange it.
