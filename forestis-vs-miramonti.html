The question behind the question

Do you want the top luxury stay, or a hotel with character?

Forestis is my favorite hotel in the Dolomites. It has the best view of the Odle, and it won my categories for wellness, dining and service. Come for the spa, the scenery, the food and the quiet.

Miramonti is a nice, unique property with an iconic pool, close to Merano. It isn’t at the level of the region’s top hotels as a luxury experience, but it is a great hotel for someone who values personality, the pool and the views over polish.

If service and finish matter most, pick Forestis. If the pool and the personality are the point, pick Miramonti, and it is an easy add-on at either end of a Dolomites trip.

The detail

What to know before you choose

The view

At Forestis, the view of the Odle is unmatched among the Dolomites’ luxury hotels, from your room, the dining room, the pools and the spa. The best of it is from the fourth or fifth floor and above, in the tower closest to the historic lodge and in the middle tower. Only a request secures that, and it is exactly what I push for.

At Miramonti, the views are over the valley and Merano, and they are beautiful. Merano isn’t really in the Dolomites the way the other hotels I saw are, so you are looking out over a gentler landscape. Forestis won my views category.

Pools and spa

Forestis has the best indoor pool in the region, joined to the outdoor pool by a glass door and heated to around 32°C (90°F), plus a blacked-out sensory room, a swimsuit sauna, and a separate area with three saunas, a steam room, a cold plunge and a heated plunge pool. My 90-minute deep-tissue massage was excellent.

Miramonti’s infinity pool is the best outdoor pool of any hotel I saw in the region, and it is the reason people come. Expect German and Austrian sauna culture at both: the main saunas are mixed, and swimsuits come off.

Rooms and character

The Forestis tower suites are Scandinavian in feel, with daybed balconies, large bathrooms with tubs and walk-in showers, and heated floors. The lodge suites in the historic building are spacious and well priced.

At Miramonti, every room is designed differently, and the whole place is a little quirky. Because no two are alike, the right room matters, and I match one to you.

Service, food and getting there

Service at Forestis was some of the best I have experienced anywhere. Half board covers breakfast and dinner in either restaurant, and the seven-course tasting menu was a highlight. By train, get off at Bressanone and it is 25 minutes up the mountain by car.

Miramonti is character over polish; if service and finish matter most, Forestis is the one. For Merano, get off the train at Bolzano and finish by private car. It is an easy first or last stop, especially if you are coming from or heading toward Lake Como or Switzerland.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Forestis or Miramonti?

For me, Forestis. It is my favorite hotel in the Dolomites, with the best view, wellness, dining and service. Miramonti is a great stay for the right traveler: someone who wants the pool, the views and a hotel with personality.

Which has the better pool?

For an outdoor pool, Miramonti; it is the best of any hotel I saw in the region. For an indoor pool, Forestis, where the indoor and outdoor pools are joined by a glass door and both heated to around 32°C.

Is Miramonti really in the Dolomites?

Not really. Miramonti is above Merano, which is outside the main Dolomites valleys. For hiking among the famous peaks, base yourself in Ortisei or on the Alpe di Siusi.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

Forestis. It is my honeymoon pick in the Dolomites: the views, the spa, the food and the quiet.

Which room should I book at each?

At Forestis, a tower suite on the fourth or fifth floor or above, in the tower closest to the lodge; I request it for you. At Miramonti, every room is different, so tell me what matters to you and I will match one.

Can I do both?

Yes. Miramonti is an easy add-on at either end of a Dolomites trip, especially if you are coming from or heading toward Lake Como or Switzerland. I arrange the trains and private drivers, with Forestis reached from Bressanone.
