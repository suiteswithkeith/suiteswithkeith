The question behind the question

Two of my favorites, for different travelers

Both wowed me. I toured them on the same day in October 2026, and I would happily send clients to either.

Pick Aman if you want calm, the Grand Canal and Aman’s personal service, or if you are continuing to Aman Rosa Alpina. Pick Orient Express if design is the reason you travel: I have not seen that much marble since the bathrooms at Villa San Michele.

In either one, ask for a room on the piano nobile, where the ceilings are highest.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Two ways to restore a palace

Aman kept the original frescoes, silk walls and stucco and placed calm, modern furniture beneath them.

Orient Express spent eight years restoring ceilings and chandeliers and layering dark wood with around seventy kinds of marble.

Location

Aman is on the Grand Canal, a short walk from the Rialto.

Orient Express sits on a quiet canal corner in Cannaregio, close to the train station and an easy walk into the city: quiet, but convenient.

Who each one suits

Aman: couples, privacy seekers and anyone pairing Venice with the Dolomites.

Orient Express: design lovers and couples who want to be amazed by every room.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Aman Venice or Orient Express Venice?

Both are excellent. Choose Aman for privacy, the Grand Canal and Aman service; choose Orient Express Palazzo Donà Giovannelli for its extraordinary restoration and design.

Where is Orient Express in Venice?

In Cannaregio, on a quiet canal corner close to Santa Lucia train station.

Do either have a pool?

No. For a pool in Venice, look at Airelles Palladio on Giudecca.

Which floor should I ask for?

The piano nobile, the noble floor one level above the ground floor, where the ceilings are highest.
