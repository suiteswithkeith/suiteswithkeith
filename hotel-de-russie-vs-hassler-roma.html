Hotel de Russie or the Hassler?

Two classic addresses in the golden triangle, between Piazza del Popolo and the top of the Spanish Steps. One has a courtyard that feels like an escape; the other sits right above the Steps. Here is how I would choose.

The question behind the question

Do you want a courtyard to escape to, or the Steps at your door?

Both are in the busiest, most central part of Rome. Step outside the Hotel de Russie or the Hassler and you are in the crowds. What you come back to is the difference.

At the Hotel de Russie, it is the courtyard: beautiful, large, and really the star of the property. Add renovated rooms with the cutest lemon and orange ceilings and a spa with an indoor pool. I loved this hotel.

At the Hassler, it is tradition: a very traditional luxury hotel right at the top of the Spanish Steps, with Michelin-starred dining on the roof. If a courtyard and a pool sound like your trip, pick the de Russie. If classic grand-hotel style and a big dinner on the roof do, pick the Hassler.

The detail

What to know before you choose

The courtyards

The courtyard at the Hotel de Russie is where everyone gathers, and it feels like an escape from the city around it. There is a bar in it, with arched blue shelves of bottles and a mosaic-fronted counter.

The Hassler has a courtyard too, with a beautiful restaurant in it. It is a calmer place to eat than the streets outside.

Rooms

The Hassler is very traditional, and that is the appeal: canopy beds, damask walls, gilded frames and crystal lamps, for travelers who want Rome’s grand-hotel style.

The Hotel de Russie rooms have been renovated: soft greens, striped headboards and the cutest ceilings, each one lemon or orange themed.

Views

I saw a stunning suite at the Hotel de Russie with fantastic views, French doors opening onto a terrace over Piazza del Popolo. Ask me and I will tell you which one.

At the Hassler, you are right above the Spanish Steps, and some bathrooms have windows over the rooftops to St. Peter’s.

Spa and dining

The Hotel de Russie has a nice spa with an indoor pool, rare in the centro storico. The Hassler is building a large wellness and gym area that opens in 2027.

For dining, the Hassler is the one I would point to: a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof with the rooftops of Rome below you. If you want quiet on the street as well, the Eden, my top overall pick in Rome, is a few minutes away.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Hotel de Russie or the Hassler?

It depends on the trip. I loved the Hotel de Russie for its courtyard, renovated rooms and indoor pool. The Hassler is the one for very traditional luxury right above the Spanish Steps and Michelin-starred dining on the roof.

Which has a spa?

The Hotel de Russie, with an indoor pool. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027.

Which is better for dining?

The Hassler, with a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof and a beautiful courtyard restaurant.

Which is quieter?

Neither street is quiet; both open onto the crowds. The courtyard at the Hotel de Russie is the calmer place to retreat to. For a quiet street, I send people to the Eden, a few minutes from the Hassler.

Hotel de Russie or its sister, Hotel de la Ville?

The de Russie. Both are Rocco Forte hotels near the Spanish Steps, and its courtyard, renovated rooms and indoor pool made it the stronger stay over the Hotel de la Ville.
