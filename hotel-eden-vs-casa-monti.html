The question behind the question

Do you want a polished classic, or something hip, young and fun?

Hotel Eden is a classic done beautifully: anticipatory service, bright rooms that are modern and traditional at once, and a quiet street above the Spanish Steps. It suits almost everyone, and couples especially.

Casa Monti is the alternative to classic luxury. It is a boutique hotel from the team behind Le Fantaisie in Paris, full of super cool patterns, in a neighborhood where real Romans were having Sunday lunch at the trattorias when I visited.

If you want a grand hotel that gets everything right, pick the Eden. If a grand hotel sounds too traditional and you are younger, especially on a honeymoon, pick Casa Monti.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Design

Casa Monti is pattern on pattern: bird prints, harlequin diamonds, zigzag armchairs, and curtains that hide the TV. You can tell it is from the team behind Le Fantaisie in Paris.

Hotel Eden is classic in the best way. The rooms are beautifully decorated, modern and traditional at once, bright, with white marble bathrooms and incredible views over the rooftops.

Neighborhood and food

Around Casa Monti there are authentic Roman trattorias, and on the Sunday I visited, real Romans were having lunch at them. Inside, there is a beautiful rooftop restaurant and a lively restaurant downstairs.

Hotel Eden sits on a calm residential street above the Spanish Steps. The restaurant upstairs was fantastic, and the concierge was super helpful with dinner reservations around the city.

Romance, two ways

The spa at Casa Monti can be privatized, so you can book it just for the two of you. Ask me and I will arrange it before you arrive.

At Hotel Eden, the romance is the view. The restaurant upstairs looks across the domes, and it is the table I send couples to on their first evening in Rome.

Service

Service at Hotel Eden was anticipatory and warm, a step ahead the whole time. The fastest coffee I have ever had delivered was here.

The staff at Casa Monti were super kind and welcoming.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Hotel Eden or Casa Monti?

For most travelers, Hotel Eden, my top overall pick in Rome. Casa Monti is my pick for anyone who wants something other than classic luxury.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

For younger honeymooners who want something hip and fun, Casa Monti, with its private spa. For couples who want a polished classic, Hotel Eden.

Which has a spa?

Casa Monti has a spa that couples can book privately. I arrange it before you arrive.

Which neighborhood is better?

It depends on the trip. Casa Monti is in Monti, with real trattorias all around. Hotel Eden is on a quiet street above the Spanish Steps, close to everything.

Is Casa Monti a luxury hotel?

It is a really great boutique hotel in the center of Rome, and a great option for anyone who wants something other than the classic luxury hotels like the Eden. Think hip, young and fun at Casa Monti.
