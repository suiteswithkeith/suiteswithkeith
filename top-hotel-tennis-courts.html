Before you ask

Hotel tennis FAQ

Which hotels have the most beautiful tennis courts in the world?

My top two are both in Italy: Il San Pietro di Positano, a turf court blasted into the cliffside above the Amalfi Coast, and Hotel Cipriani, a private clay court on Giudecca framed by the Venetian lagoon.

What are the best tennis hotels in Italy?

Il San Pietro and Hotel Cipriani, plus Passalacqua and Villa d’Este on Lake Como and Splendido above Portofino’s harbor.

Where can I play tennis on Lake Como?

Villa d’Este has eight clay courts and is the best hotel on the lake for a tennis trip with friends. Passalacqua has a single clay court above the lake, and Grand Hotel Tremezzo has one too. My Lake Como ranking.

Which hotel has an indoor tennis court?

Bürgenstock Resort on Lake Lucerne, an alpine indoor court wrapped in timber below the peaks.

Which tennis resort has the most courts?

Mauna Kea Beach Hotel, Hawaii’s original tennis park, with a dozen courts along the Kohala Coast. Gstaad Palace has clay courts that host an annual stop on the pro tour.

Is there a good tennis hotel in Napa Valley?

Meadowood. Its tennis facility is underrated, with beautiful courts and instructors I’d recommend in a heartbeat. My Napa & Sonoma ranking.

Does it cost more to book these hotels through you?

No. Every hotel on this list is a Fora Reserve property, and booking through me is complimentary, at the same rate as booking direct, with Fora perks like space-available upgrades, daily breakfast and hotel credits on top.
