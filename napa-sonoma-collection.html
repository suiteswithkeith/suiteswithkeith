A note from Keith
Napa and Sonoma are personal to me — for many reasons, they’re probably why I started Suites With Keith to begin with. I grew up twenty-five minutes south, in Marin County, and for years my work brought me back to these valleys almost monthly. Over time, the people who run these hotels became personal friends.

Ten hotels across four bases — where I’d stay, eat, and taste. Every house here is a Fora or Virtuoso preferred partner.

How booking with me works

Every hotel in this collection is a property I book through my Fora / Virtuoso partnership. You pay the hotel’s own rate, booking through me costs nothing extra, and the reservation arrives with more attached to it.

Upgrades, prioritized

Preferred-partner guests sit at the top of the upgrade list, sometimes confirmed before arrival.

Breakfast, daily

Breakfast for two at nearly every house in this collection, every morning of the stay.

A credit on the folio

Property credit for spa, dining, or wine at most of these hotels — at a few it is among the strongest in the valley.

A person to call

You arrive as my guest, not a confirmation number, and if anything wobbles you call me, not a help line.
