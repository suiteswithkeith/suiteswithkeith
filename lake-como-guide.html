03 · Before you book

Things I'd tell a friend

  1. 01
    Don't stay in Bellagio. It's crowded. Visit for an afternoon.
  2. 02
    Reserve Passalacqua early. 24 rooms, so it books up first.
  3. 03
    Give it 4 nights. Enough to settle in.
  4. 04
    Hire a private driver. Trust me, Como roads are stressful.
  5. 05
    Go in May, June, September or early October. Most hotels close for winter.
