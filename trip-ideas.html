Santorini

The Caldera · Best May–June, Sept–Oct

Sunset catamaran cruise. The one thing I tell every couple to lock in early — the good boats sell out weeks ahead in high season.

Winery afternoon. Santorini's volcanic Assyrtiko is unlike anything else in Greece. Book a guided tasting with a driver.

Fira–Oia caldera hike. Free, iconic, and best started early — finish with lunch in Oia.

Milos

The Wild One · Boat Days Essential

Kleftiko boat excursion. Milos is an island you experience from the water — the sculpted white cliffs of Kleftiko are the reason you came.

Sarakiniko at golden hour. The moonscape beach. Go early or late; bring water shoes.

Mykonos

The Icon · Reserve Everything Early

Delos day trip. The ancient island next door — go with a licensed guide in the morning before the heat.

Private boat to Rhenia. The swimming day the beach clubs can't give you.

Beach club reservations. Scorpios and its peers book out — decide your days early.

Athens

The Gateway Worth 2–3 Nights

Private Acropolis tour. An expert guide at opening time changes the entire experience.

Athens Riviera day. Beach clubs and seaside lunches thirty minutes from the center.

The Quiet Sophisticates

Sailing day from Paros. South-coast sailing to Despotiko's empty beaches — the day my clients talk about most.

Sifnos food walk. The culinary island of the Cyclades — eat your way through Apollonia and Kastro.
