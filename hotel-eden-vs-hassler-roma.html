The question behind the question

Do you want the Steps at your door, or a few minutes away?

Hotel Eden and the Hassler are a few minutes apart above the Spanish Steps. The difference is what is outside the door.

The Hassler is right at the top of the Steps, about as central as Rome gets, and the Steps draw crowds from morning to night. It is a very traditional hotel, and that is its appeal. Some travelers want exactly that.

Hotel Eden sits on a quiet residential street just beyond them. You are still close to everything, the service is warmer, and it is usually priced slightly below the Hassler. That is why it is my top overall pick in Rome.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location and views

You cannot get much more central than the Hassler, right above the Spanish Steps. Some of its bathrooms have windows over the rooftops to St. Peter’s. The trade is the crowd at the front door.

Hotel Eden is a few minutes away on a calm street, with incredible views over the rooftops from the rooms and sunset over the dome of St. Peter’s. The walk home is a gentle rise from the centro, so wear the right shoes.

Dining on the roof

The dining is a highlight at the Hassler: a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof with the rooftops of Rome below you, and a beautiful courtyard restaurant that is a calmer place to eat than the streets outside.

The restaurant upstairs at Hotel Eden was fantastic, with the view across the domes I send couples to on their first evening. The concierge also sorted my dinner reservations around the city without any back and forth.

Rooms and style

The Hassler is classic luxury for travelers who want Rome’s grand-hotel style: canopy beds, damask walls, gilded frames and crystal lamps. If that is what Rome means to you, it delivers.

Hotel Eden is modern and traditional at once. The rooms are bright and beautifully decorated, with balconies over the rooftops and white marble bathrooms.

Service, wellness and price

Service at Hotel Eden was anticipatory and warm. I had the fastest coffee I have ever had delivered there. It is usually priced a little below the Hassler, for a stay I would rank above it.

The Hassler is building a large new wellness and gym area that opens in 2027. Until then, if a spa and gym matter, I would look at the Bulgari or the Corinthia.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Hotel Eden or the Hassler?

For me, Hotel Eden. Warmer service, a quieter street and usually a slightly lower rate. Pick the Hassler if you love very traditional luxury and want to be right at the top of the Spanish Steps.

Which is closer to the Spanish Steps?

The Hassler is right at the top of them. Hotel Eden is a few minutes away on a calm residential street.

Which is better for dining?

For a big dinner in the hotel, the Hassler’s Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof. For the view I send couples to on their first evening, the restaurant upstairs at Hotel Eden.

Does the Hassler have a spa?

Not yet. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027. Until then, the Bulgari and the Corinthia are the ones for a spa and gym.

Which costs less?

Usually Hotel Eden, slightly below the Hassler. I can quote them side by side for your dates.
