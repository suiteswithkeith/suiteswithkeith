Island notes

Athens

  • Pack one evening outfit for The Dolli; everything else is beach and walking.
  • Skip the Plaka souvenir streets. The food tour covers the neighborhoods worth your time.
  • Cards work everywhere on the Riviera. Take a little cash for the tour tips.

Sifnos

  • Beaches need wheels. The ATV is booked from the hotel; Stamna holds the keys.
  • Cash for the small tavernas and the Narlis Farm shop. The ATM is in Apollonia.
  • Fykiada is a walk in. Water shoes, and leave before the afternoon wind.

Milos

  • The western beaches are dirt track. The ATV is fine; a rental car would not be.
  • Sarakiniko is best before 7:00 AM. After that it is everyone’s photo.
  • The boat day carries lunch, towels and snorkels. Bring reef-safe sunscreen only.

Santorini

  • Oia is stairs. Andronis handles the luggage; you carry nothing.
  • Change money before you arrive; the caldera ATMs charge accordingly.
  • The catamaran cruise runs even in a stiff breeze. If it cancels, I already know, and the fallback is a private sunset table.
