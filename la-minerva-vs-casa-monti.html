The question behind the question

Do you want a palazzo by the Pantheon, or a boutique hotel in Monti?

La Minerva and Casa Monti are the two hotels I send younger travelers to in Rome, and both put you in a real neighborhood.

La Minerva is a palazzo by the Pantheon, run by the warmest team I met in Rome. Its shared spaces were the highlight of my stay, and it is where I put clients before La Dolce Vita.

Casa Monti is smaller and more playful, from the team behind Le Fantaisie in Paris, with trattorias all around and a spa couples can have to themselves. It suits younger couples best, especially on their honeymoon.

The detail

What to know before you choose

The neighborhood

Around Casa Monti, on Via Panisperna, there are authentic Roman trattorias, and when I visited on a Sunday, real Romans were having lunch at them.

La Minerva is on Piazza della Minerva by the Pantheon. The views are more authentic Rome than pristine Rome, and that is part of the charm.

Upstairs and downstairs

Both hotels have a restaurant on top and a buzz below. At La Minerva, the rooftop looks over the domes of the centro storico, and the bar and lobby downstairs have a real energy.

Casa Monti has a beautiful rooftop restaurant and a lively restaurant downstairs, with striped banquettes and wicker chairs.

For the two of you

The spa at Casa Monti can be privatized, so couples can book it just for the two of them. I arrange it before you arrive.

At La Minerva, Signature Suite bookings through me can include a 50-minute massage for two, on top of daily breakfast for two, a hotel credit and a VIP welcome.

The people, and the little things

At La Minerva, I shared a great laugh with the woman who checked me in, and I felt like I could be myself. The best slippers of my whole trip were here. I kept them.

The staff at Casa Monti were super kind and welcoming. And the details are fun: super cool patterns everywhere, and curtains that hide the TV.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, La Minerva or Casa Monti?

Both are great for younger travelers. La Minerva is a palazzo by the Pantheon with the warmest team I met in Rome. Casa Monti is a hip boutique hotel in Monti with a private spa for couples.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

Casa Monti suits younger honeymooners who want something hip, young and fun, and the private spa helps. La Minerva is great for social couples.

Which is better before La Dolce Vita?

La Minerva. La Dolce Vita journeys start in Rome, and I put clients there first.

Which has a spa?

Casa Monti has a spa couples can book privately. At La Minerva, Signature Suite bookings through me can include a 50-minute massage for two.

What if we want more polish and calm?

Then look at Hotel Eden, my top overall pick in Rome. If you are older or want more polish and calm than La Minerva or Casa Monti, it is where I would gravitate.
