Head-to-head · Altitude or the postcard

Grace Hotel or Katikies?

One sits so high on the Imerovigli caldera you’re sometimes literally above the fog line; the other holds the Oia real estate half the island’s postcards are made of. #07 and #10 in my Santorini ranking — here’s how I split them.

#07 and #10 in my Santorini ranking · both toured in person

Choose Grace

If altitude beats the postcard

Set way up on the Imerovigli caldera — on the right morning you’re literally above the fog line, a genuinely unique feeling. Bright, elegant white rooms, and dining from Michelin-starred chef Lefteris Lazarou, who personally visits tables.

Choose Katikies

If Oia is non-negotiable

The storied Oia terraces themselves — iconic white stairs and infinity pools that still deliver the postcard, with an expansive, genuinely impressive spa at Pelagos House behind them.

At Grace, I’d guide you to the outdoor plunge-pool rooms over the indoor hot tub suites, and I’ll make sure yours is one of the secluded ones off the walking path. Both hotels are worth weighing against my top five before committing — sometimes they win, and I’ll tell you when they do.

The question behind the question

Both live just outside my top five — for different reasons.

Grace is the one I wanted to love more than I did — but when pricing aligns and the room is right, the above-the-fog-line setting and that dining make it a genuinely special honeymoon pick for couples happy to trade Oia’s foot traffic for altitude and quiet.

Katikies is the opposite case: the location is beyond argument — it’s the interiors that are waiting on their next chapter. If the Oia postcard is the whole point of the trip, it delivers that like almost nothing else. Send me your dates and I’ll tell you which one wins them.
