The question behind the question

The classic, or the statement?

Il San Pietro is the classic, done properly: sea views from every room, the iconic tennis court, and a beach club on the sunset side. Borgo Santandrea is spectacular and very modern, and that makes it feel different from every other hotel on the coast.

The afternoon sun decides a lot of fall trips. At most hotels on the coast, Borgo Santandrea included, the water goes dark early, and your swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm. Not at Il San Pietro.

If you want sunsets, privacy and a hotel that fills your days, pick Il San Pietro. If you want something new and modern, with a proper beach, pick Borgo Santandrea.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms

At Il San Pietro, every room is impeccably kept and has a sea view. Some face the sunrise, others the sunset, so tell me which you want. The base rooms are fantastic; Signature and Prestige give you more space.

Borgo Santandrea is very modern, in the rooms and in the facilities. The private pool rooms are not that private, though: you can look down on your neighbors, and they can look down on you. Tell me what matters to you and I will request the right room.

Beach clubs and the afternoon sun

Il San Pietro’s beach club is on the sunset side, which is a huge selling point. In the fall, the best time to visit, swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm at most hotels on the coast. Not here.

Borgo Santandrea has the better beach itself: beautiful, massive and a proper beach. But it is one of the hotels where the sun leaves the water early. Come in May to June, or September.

Food and the days

Il San Pietro fills the days for you: a complimentary 2-hour yacht cruise, cooking and pizza classes, a daily cocktail masterclass, Pilates and yoga, the iconic tennis court and Michelin-starred dining.

At Borgo Santandrea, the beach is the day, and dinner at Alici is fantastic. Service is refined but warm.

Honeymoons

Both are honeymoon picks of mine. Il San Pietro is #06 in The Fifteen, my honeymoon shortlist, and the privacy is the point: no one can look down on you.

Borgo Santandrea is No. 84 on my World’s 50 Best (51–100), and my other honeymoon pick there. Choose it for the statement and the beach, and skip the private pool rooms if privacy is the priority.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Il San Pietro or Borgo Santandrea?

It depends on what you want. Il San Pietro is the classic, with sea views from every room, the tennis court and the sunset beach club. Borgo Santandrea is the modern, spectacular statement, with a proper beach. For a fall trip, the afternoon sun at Il San Pietro is hard to beat.

Which has the better beach?

Borgo Santandrea has the better beach: beautiful, massive and a proper beach. Il San Pietro’s beach club is on the rocks, but it is on the sunset side, so you keep the sun on the water late into the afternoon.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

Both are on my honeymoon lists. Il San Pietro is #06 in The Fifteen, and the privacy is the point. At Borgo Santandrea, skip the private pool rooms if privacy matters, because they are overlooked.

Should I book a private pool room at Borgo Santandrea?

Think twice. At Borgo Santandrea, the private pool rooms are not that private: you can look down on your neighbors, and they can look down on you. Not everyone will mind, but it is important to know.

Which is closer to Positano?

Il San Pietro, on the cliffs just outside town, with free boat and car shuttles in. Borgo Santandrea is above Conca dei Marini, just along the coast from Amalfi, on the quieter side of the coast.

Can I do both?

Yes. A few nights at each splits the trip between Positano and the Amalfi side of the coast, and I arrange the boat between them.
