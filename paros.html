Destination guide · The Cyclades

Paros

The island everyone quietly falls in love with.

The Greece Collection · Issue No. 01 · guide 04

Paros is the island I recommend more every year. Beautiful beaches, exceptional restaurants, stylish hotels and authentic villages — without feeling overly polished. If you’re looking for the Cyclades beyond the postcard version of Greece, start here.

Best for
Couples, repeat Greece visitors, food lovers
Ideal stay
3–5 nights — two islands per week; three is a travel day disguised as a vacation
Best months
May–June, September
Don’t miss
Naousa harbor after sunset · lunch at a Crios beach club · Kolymbithres’ granite coves · a sunset stroll through Lefkes
Getting around
Rent a car — the island is compact enough that everything feels within reach. Between islands, I arrange private boats and helicopters through Hoper.

Three perfect days

How I’d spend them

01Arrive. An afternoon at your hotel, then Naousa as the sun drops — the boutiques, the harbor, and a long dinner at Siparos while the waterfront comes alive.
02Discover. Lefkes in the morning, lunch at To Balconi tou Aki in Aliki, an afternoon swim at Lageri, and dinner back in Lefkes at Santa Pacou. Don’t miss sunset on the marble streets.
03Unwind. A slow morning, the day at Crios Beach Club, and a last dinner at Parostia at Cosme — one of my favorite restaurants on the island.

Paros’ greatest strength is its scale: compact enough that a lazy beach day never feels like missing out. Slow down and let it unfold.

Getting there

The ferry rules that save trips

All five rules →

Rule 1
Never book the last ferry of the day. If it’s cancelled, you’ve lost a night.
Rule 2
Fast ferries cancel in wind; slow ones usually don’t. In shoulder season, take the slow boat.
Rule 3
Don’t fly home the same day you sail. Give yourself a night in Athens.
Skip it all
A private boat between islands, or a helicopter direct — the thing I’m asked to arrange most. It turns a lost travel day into a morning.
