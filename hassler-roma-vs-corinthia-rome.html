The question behind the question

Do you want the Spanish Steps outside your door, or a quiet street?

The Hassler is about as central as Rome gets. That is the appeal, and it also means a busy area: you step out the door and you are in the crowds at the Steps.

The Corinthia is across the street from parliament, so it is incredibly safe, and quiet while still in the middle of everything. I think it is truly a game changer for Rome.

If you love traditional grand hotels and want the Steps as your front yard, pick the Hassler. If you want to come home to calm, a spa today and more room, pick the Corinthia.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location and quiet

The Hassler is right above the Spanish Steps, and the Steps draw crowds from morning to night.

The Corinthia is on Piazza del Parlamento: safe, quiet and walkable to everything. Like the Eden, it is central and still quiet.

Rooms and space

Space is the story at the Corinthia. The rooms are large and tastefully appointed, and some bedrooms open onto terraces. It is new, so categories and views vary; I’ll steer you to the right one.

The Hassler’s rooms are very traditional: canopy beds, damask walls and crystal lamps, for travelers who want Rome’s grand-hotel style.

Spa and wellness

The Corinthia’s spa and public spaces are beautiful, and big enough that the hotel never feels crowded.

The Hassler is building a large new wellness and gym area that opens in 2027. Until then, if a spa matters, I point people to the Corinthia or the Bulgari.

Dining and style

The dining is a highlight at the Hassler, with a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof and the rooftops of Rome laid out below you, and a beautiful restaurant in the courtyard.

The Corinthia is the new name: the renovation is a showstopper from the moment you walk in. Space alone puts it in the conversation with the city’s classics.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Hassler or the Corinthia?

It depends on whether you want to be in the action or come home to quiet. The Hassler for traditional luxury at the top of the Spanish Steps and Michelin-starred dining on the roof. The Corinthia for large rooms, a beautiful spa and a quiet, safe street in the middle of everything.

Which is quieter?

The Corinthia, across the street from parliament. The Hassler is at the Spanish Steps, which draw crowds from morning to night.

Which has a spa?

The Corinthia, and it is beautiful. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027.

Which has the bigger rooms?

The Corinthia. Its rooms are large, which is rare in central Rome. The Hassler’s rooms are classic and very traditional.

Which is better for a classic Rome stay?

The Hassler. It is a very traditional hotel right at the top of the Spanish Steps, and that is exactly its appeal.
