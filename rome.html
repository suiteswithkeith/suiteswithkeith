Plan my Rome stay

The first Rome decision isn’t which hotel. It’s whether you want to come home to calm.

Rome’s classic addresses cluster around the Spanish Steps and Piazza del Popolo, and step outside most of them and you are in the crowds. A few, like the Eden and the new Corinthia, put you in the middle of everything on a quiet street.

I stayed at the Eden and La Minerva and visited the rest in person in October 2026. Here is where I would send my own clients, and why.

Above the Spanish Steps · Stayed · My number one in Rome

Hotel Eden

Romantic classic (Dorchester Collection)

One of my top three stays in all of Italy. The service was anticipatory and warm, the rooms were bright, the restaurant upstairs was fantastic and the concierge was super helpful with dinner reservations. It sits on a quiet residential street just above the Spanish Steps, so you are close to everything and still come home to calm.

Right for
Couples, anniversaries, travelers who want quiet in the middle of Rome
Room I’d book
Ask about the top suites.They are spacious on their own and can be connected into larger configurations for families or two couples traveling together.

Read the full review

Keith’s notes

It costs slightly less than its rivals. Usually a little below the Hassler, the de Russie and the Bulgari, for a stay I’d rank above them.

Uphill from the Steps. A gentle rise from the centro, so wear the right shoes home from dinner.

The verdict

My top overall pick in Rome, and one of my top three stays in all of Italy.

Corinthia Rome

Grand new opening (Corinthia)

Wow. I was blown away by this hotel, and I think it is truly a game changer for Rome. It is across the street from parliament, so the area is incredibly safe, and quiet while still being in the middle of everything. The renovation is a showstopper, the spa and public spaces are beautiful, and the rooms are large and tastefully appointed.

Right for
Travelers who want space, a real spa and a central, quiet base

Keith’s notes

Space is the story. Space is hard to come by in central Rome, and that alone puts the Corinthia in the conversation with the city’s classics.

The verdict

A showstopper renovation, a beautiful spa and large rooms. Truly a game changer for Rome.

Bulgari Hotel Roma

Modern and lively (Bulgari Hotels & Resorts)

Spectacular. The base rooms are what other hotels would call deluxe rooms or even junior suites. It is very modern, with lots of marble and incredible mosaics in the bathrooms, and lively, almost like an EDITION but much more sophisticated, right next to the Mausoleum of Augustus.

Right for
Travelers who want space, energy and great dining without leaving the hotel

It’s lively, not hushed. If you want quiet and calm, the Eden is the one I’d pick.

The biggest gym I saw in Rome. Plus more places to eat and drink than I could count, including the rooftop bar.

Piazza del Popolo · Visited in person

Hotel de Russie

Garden retreat (Rocco Forte)

I loved this hotel. The beautiful, large courtyard is the star of the property, and it feels like an escape from the city around it. The renovated rooms have the cutest ceilings, each one lemon or orange themed, and there is a nice spa with an indoor pool, rare in the centro storico.

Right for
Couples, Rome first-timers, travelers who want a spa and a courtyard in the center
Room I’d book
Ask me about the suite with the views.I saw a stunning suite with fantastic views, and I’ll tell you which one.

Keith’s notes

I prefer it to its sister. The de Russie and the de la Ville are both Rocco Forte. For me the de Russie is the stronger stay.

The street out front is busy. Step outside and you’re in the crowds between Popolo and the Steps.

A beautiful courtyard, playful renovated rooms and a spa with an indoor pool. I loved it.

The St. Regis Rome

Belle Époque grande dame (St. Regis)

Rome’s original grand hotel, opened by César Ritz in 1894 and restored with chandeliers and frescoes. A saber goes through a champagne bottle every evening at aperitivo hour, which tells you what kind of hotel it is: theatrical on purpose.

Right for
Celebrations, grandeur lovers, Marriott loyalists

Know before you go

The fine print, from your advisor

01
Quiet or in the middle of it. At the Hassler, the de la Ville, the de Russie and even the Bulgari, you step out the door into busy, crowded streets. The Eden and the Corinthia are central and still quiet. Decide which you want before you pick a hotel.
02
Space is rare in central Rome. The Bulgari’s base rooms and the Corinthia’s large rooms are the exceptions. Elsewhere, tell me how much room you need and I’ll match the category to it.
03
Spas and pools are few. The de Russie has an indoor pool, the Bulgari the biggest gym I saw, the Corinthia a beautiful spa and Casa Monti a spa couples can book privately. The Hassler’s wellness area opens in 2027.
04
Transfers and drivers, arranged. I arrange private transfers from Fiumicino or Termini station, drivers for days outside the city, and onward travel by fast train or car to Florence, Naples or the Amalfi Coast.

Before you ask

Rome FAQ

What is the best luxury hotel in Rome?

For me, Hotel Eden. It was one of my top three stays in all of Italy: anticipatory, warm service, bright rooms, a fantastic restaurant upstairs, and a quiet residential street just above the Spanish Steps. It is usually priced slightly below the Hassler, the de Russie and the Bulgari.

Which Rome hotel is best for younger travelers?

Orient Express La Minerva by the Pantheon, for the warmest shared spaces and the friendliest team I met in Rome, or Casa Monti, a hip boutique hotel in Monti surrounded by real Roman trattorias.

Which Rome hotels have a spa or pool?

The Hotel de Russie has a spa with an indoor pool. The Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome, the new Corinthia has a beautiful spa, and Casa Monti has a spa couples can book privately. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027.

Where should I stay in Rome for quiet?

Hotel Eden, on a quiet residential street above the Spanish Steps, or the Corinthia, across from parliament. At the Hassler, the de la Ville, the de Russie and the Bulgari you step out into busy, crowded streets.

Hotel de Russie or Hotel de la Ville?

The de Russie. They are sister Rocco Forte hotels a few minutes apart, and the de Russie’s large courtyard, renovated rooms and spa with an indoor pool made it the stronger stay for me.
