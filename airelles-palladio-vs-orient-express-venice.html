The question behind the question

Do you want a resort, or a palace?

I toured both on the same day in October 2026, along with the Danieli and Aman Venice. Of Venice’s three new hotels, Airelles Palladio is my number one and Orient Express is my number two.

Airelles gives you more: pools, a real spa, gardens and four chefs, with St. Mark’s five minutes away when you want it. It is the better choice for families, and for me it is the most beautiful hotel in Italy.

Orient Express wins on the interiors. If the marble, the ceilings and the chandeliers are what you come to Venice for, or you are arriving by train, book it and ask for the piano nobile.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location: across the water, or a quiet corner

Airelles Palladio is on Giudecca, facing San Marco across the water. The hotel boat makes the crossing in about five minutes. My one fear was that it would feel isolated. It does not, at all, and you come home to real peace and quiet in a very touristy city.

Orient Express sits on a calm canal corner in Cannaregio, away from the San Marco crowds and an easy walk into the city. It is close to Santa Lucia, so it works well if you arrive by train from Florence, Milan or the Dolomites.

Design

At Orient Express, it is the mix: dark wood with some seventy kinds of marble, book-matched in the bathrooms, painted and coffered ceilings restored to their original glory, and Murano chandeliers. I have never seen so much marble in my life. Everywhere I looked, something new caught my eye.

At Airelles, the attention to detail in the finishes, the gardens and the rooms is truly serene, and the rooms are incredibly spacious. In the penthouse, one room has a slight slope where the original structure could not be changed. I loved it.

Pools, spa and dining

This is where Airelles pulls ahead. There is a 25-meter pool hidden in the gardens, an 8-meter pool just for children, and the Domus indoor pool in Spa Airelles by Guerlain, 1,700 square meters over three levels. Norbert Niederkofler, Matsuhisa, Jean-Georges and Cédric Grolet are all on the estate, so you can stay a week without repeating an evening.

Orient Express has no pool. It is a palazzo hotel. If a pool matters, Airelles is the answer in Venice.

Rooms, families and how long to stay

At Orient Express, ask for the piano nobile, the noble floor one level above the ground floor, where the ceilings are highest. Three nights is the sweet spot: long enough for the city, the islands and a few long lunches.

Airelles is the best family hotel in Venice: a kids club, the gardens, Villa Mason with three bedrooms and its own pool, and the four-bedroom Suite del Doge looking straight at St. Mark’s. I could easily do four nights.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Airelles Palladio or Orient Express?

For most travelers, Airelles Palladio. It has the pools, the three-story spa, the gardens and four chefs, five minutes by boat from St. Mark’s, and it is my number one hotel in Italy. Choose Orient Express if design is the reason you travel: the eight-year restoration is jaw-dropping.

Which is better for families?

Airelles Palladio, without question: outdoor, indoor and kids’ pools, a complimentary kids club, gardens to run around in and a private three-bedroom suite with its own pool. Orient Express has no pool.

Does Orient Express Venice have a pool?

No. Orient Express is a palazzo hotel. For a pool in Venice, Airelles Palladio has a 25-meter pool in the gardens, a kids’ pool and an indoor pool in its spa.

Which is easier from the train station?

Orient Express, close to Santa Lucia station in Cannaregio. Airelles Palladio is about 20 minutes from the station by water taxi.

Which room should I book at each?

At Orient Express, a room or suite on the piano nobile, where the ceilings are highest. At Airelles Palladio, the Suite del Doge for the big occasion, or Villa Mason, the three-bedroom suite with a private pool, for a family.

How many nights should I stay?

Three is my sweet spot for Venice, which suits Orient Express. At Airelles Palladio I could easily do four.
