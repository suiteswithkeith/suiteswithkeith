Head-to-head · The Carneros question

Carneros Resort or Stanly Ranch?

Napa’s southern gateway, two ways: the beloved classic of cottages and vineyard views, or the most architecturally striking hotel in wine country. They sit at #08 and #06 on my ranking — and the location argument cuts the opposite way you’d guess.

#08 and #06 in my Napa & Sonoma ranking · both stayed in person · both convenient to Napa and Sonoma

Choose Carneros

If wine country feel is the point

Sprawling, beautiful grounds where every cottage has private outdoor space — and a location I actually prefer to Stanly Ranch, because the vineyard-and-barn landscape feels much more like wine country.

Choose Stanly Ranch

If design and wellness are the trip

Possibly the most architecturally striking hotel on my list, with arguably the prettiest spa in the valley, a fire pit on every room’s terrace, and Bear — one of the more interesting dining rooms in Napa right now.

Fine print on both: consistency at Carneros varies — some stays flawless, others less so — which is exactly why booking through me means someone is watching your file. At Stanly Ranch, terrace privacy varies by room; I’ll steer you to the best-sited ones. Both sit at the south end, convenient to Napa and Sonoma alike.

The question behind the question

The postcard or the statement.

Carneros is the one with the unmistakable wine country feel — and with a renovation, I honestly think it moves several spots up my list. In the meantime, book it for what it does beautifully: space, privacy, and the two-bedroom residences that are the best product on the property.

Stanly Ranch is the statement piece — the architecture, the spa, the fire pits — though I don’t find myself wanting to go back as often as the hotels above it, because the marshes never quite deliver the postcard. Design-and-wellness trip, or the classic Carneros weekend: tell me which, and I’ll book the right one.
