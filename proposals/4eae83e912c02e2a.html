Ferretti 630

Elegance at sea

Keith's recommendation

A real step up in space and polish. Fully refitted in 2024, the 630 has a sweeping aft deck, a light-filled salon, and three cabins that are all ensuite. A dedicated crew of three looks after you, with lunch served on deck or in the salon, whichever the day calls for.

If I were booking for myself, this is the one: nearly the space and finish of the 68, every cabin ensuite, for noticeably less.
