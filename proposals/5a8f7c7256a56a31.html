Plose Mountain, above Brixen

Forestis

One of the most striking hotels in the Alps: minimalist towers in the forest at about 1,800 m, with views across to the Odle peaks.

A place to slow down, with an exceptional spa, beautiful food, and excellent hikes about 45 minutes away by car.

You keep your existing Tower Suite reservation. Keith adds his partner benefits and works with the team on the best building and floor.

Keith's note I'll push for the fourth or fifth floor or higher, in the tower closest to the lodge or the middle tower. That's where the view of the Odle is best. Half board covers breakfast and dinner in either restaurant.

Choose one · 2 nights

Two nights in the heart of the Dolomites

Forestis is about restoring. These three hotels put you right in the classic hiking terrain, each with a superb spa and table, and all about an hour from Forestis.

Ortisei, Val Gardena

Gardena Grödnerhof

  • Family-run Relais & Châteaux in a charming village
  • Michelin-starred Anna Stuben and a large, serene spa
  • Lifts to Seceda and the Alpe di Siusi minutes away

Best for food lovers who want village charm plus iconic hikes

Keith's note My pick for the best base in the Dolomites. I'd book a Superior Deluxe or above.

Alpe di Siusi

COMO Alpina Dolomites

  • Set right on Europe's largest high-alpine meadow; walk out the door onto trails
  • COMO Shambhala spa and an indoor/outdoor pool facing the peaks
  • Calm, contemporary design

Best for the most immersive, step-outside-and-hike setting

Keith's note I'll steer you away from the Mountain View rooms, where the chairlift runs right outside.

San Cassiano, Alta Badia

Aman Rosa Alpina

  • A storied mountain hotel, newly reimagined by Aman
  • Alta Badia is known for dramatic hikes and the best mountain-hut food in the Dolomites
  • Aman's signature spa and service

Best for polished, quiet luxury with a gourmet mountain scene

Keith's note The most luxurious stay in the region. Book a suite; the views are intimate rather than dramatic.

Keith's pick

Airelles Venezia (Palladio)

Keith's favorite hotel of more than 50 he has seen across Italy, with the best spa he experienced anywhere in the country

Three pools, so there's always a quiet spot to lounge

Set on Giudecca in a restored historic complex beside Palladio's church, with sweeping views of San Marco and expansive private gardens

A true retreat from the crowds

Keith's note My number one hotel in Italy. I'll ask for the piano nobile, where the ceilings are highest.
