Above Brixen · Sept 22 to 25

Forestis

One of the most striking hotels in the Alps: minimalist towers in the forest at about 1,800 m, with views across to the Odle peaks.

A place to slow down, with an exceptional spa, beautiful food, and excellent hikes about 45 minutes away by car.

Your Tower Suite is confirmed for three nights with breakfast and dinner. Keith's partner benefits add a $100 credit for the spa or dining, plus an upgrade and early check-in or late check-out when available.

Keith's note I'll push for the fourth or fifth floor or higher, in the tower closest to the lodge or the middle tower. That's where the view of the Odle is best. For one night, I'd book dinner at YERA, the fireside restaurant above the hotel.

Ortisei, Val Gardena · Sept 25 to 27

Gardena Grödnerhof

A family-run Relais & Châteaux in the heart of Ortisei, with the Michelin-starred Anna Stuben and a large, serene spa.

The lifts to Seceda and the Alpe di Siusi are minutes away, so the classic Dolomites hikes start almost at the door.

Your Superior Deluxe room is confirmed for two nights with breakfast. Keith's partner benefits add a $100 credit, a welcome of local sparkling wine with bites from Anna Stuben, and an upgrade and early or late check-out when available.

Keith's note My pick for the best base in the Dolomites. I'd save one evening for Anna Stuben, and I can reserve it for you.
