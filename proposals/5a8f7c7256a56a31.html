Fri, Sept 24 · Option one

The Witches' Benches

Your guide collects you at Forestis and drives you to Compaccio, on the Alpe di Siusi. From there the Bullaccia trail loops through meadows and pine forest, past the Bullaccia and Arnica huts, to a wooden weather cross looking down over Castelrotto and the Isarco valley.

Just above sit the Witches' Benches, rocks shaped like armchairs where, the story goes, the Sciliar witches gathered to dance under the moon. Some believe it was a Neolithic ritual site. Your guide tells the old legends as you walk.

The loop ends with a drink at a mountain inn and the view from the Angel's Rest platform, then one last steep descent back to Compaccio.

Mon, Sept 27 · 4 PM

Venice unveiled

Your guide meets you at the San Marco dock on the afternoon you arrive. St Mark's Square was built as the Republic's stage: between the Clock Tower and the Campanile, you stand at the centre of a state that lasted a thousand years.

Inside the Basilica, built as the Doge's private chapel, 8,000 square meters of gold mosaic glow even on a grey day. Next door is the Doge's Palace, where the councils met beneath vast canvases by Veronese and Tintoretto, painted to remind them who they served.

A full day out on the lagoon in your own water taxi, with a guide who knows the islands.
