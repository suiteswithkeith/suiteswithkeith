Time zones

Greece is 10 hours ahead of Los Angeles. Paris is 9 hours ahead. Frankfurt and Paris are 1 hour behind Greece.

Tipping guide

Appreciated but never expected in Greece. Comfortable ranges, always in euros, cash.

  • Restaurants: round up or leave 5 to 10 percent for a great meal
  • Hotel porters: 2 to 5 euros per bag · Housekeeping: about 5 euros per day
  • Drivers: 10 to 20 euros for a port or airport run
  • Boat skippers: about 10 percent of the charter for a great day
