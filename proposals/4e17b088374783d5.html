01 · Mykonos · June 26 to 29

Cali, a beach of its own on the quiet side

Cali is one of Greece's great independent hotels, on Mykonos's quieter east coast at Kalafatis. Service is refined, the infinity pool is one of the largest in Greece with real space between loungers, and the beach sits directly below the suites, so the girls can run between room and sand.

Town is 20 minutes away, with a scheduled shuttle to Mykonos Town and Ano Mera from 11AM to 1AM.

02 · Paros · June 29 to July 3

Parilio, the design one, with a beach club next door

As a design lover, Parilio was perhaps my favorite stay in the Cyclades. The light moving through it at twilight is magical, and the staff get to know you and your preferences by name.

Guests can use the beach club at sister hotel Cosme for free, five minutes away by the shared shuttle, so you get the beach without staying on it.

03 · Santorini · July 3 to 6

Andronis Arcadia, built for families on the caldera

Arcadia is a true resort rather than a boutique hotel, just outside Oia with sunset-facing suites and very private layouts. Every room has its own pool, some of the largest on the island.

The family side is real: the ARCADemy kids club and the Evexia Spa give the girls and the two of you somewhere to be at the same time.

04 · Athens Riviera · July 6 to 9

One&Only Aesthesis, a slow finish on the Riviera

A full seaside resort rather than a city hotel: bungalows and villas running down to the water in Glyfada, an easy run to the airport for the flight home.

The Acropolis becomes a morning out rather than the address, and the rest of the days are for the beach.
