Good to know
  • Climate fee. Greece charges a per-night climate resilience fee, paid directly to each hotel at checkout. At Cali it is €15 per night
  • Check-in and out. Cali: check-in 14:00, check-out 11:00. The other hotels are similar; early or late is always worth asking
  • Ferry boarding. Arrive at the port one hour before departure
Tipping guide

Appreciated but never expected in Greece. Comfortable ranges, always in euros, cash.

  • Restaurants: round up or leave 5 to 10 percent for a great meal
  • Hotel porters: 2 to 5 euros per bag · Housekeeping: about 5 euros per day
  • Drivers: 10 to 20 euros for a port or airport run
  • Boat skippers: about 10 percent of the charter for a great day
