Payment & cancellation terms
  • Transfers, cars and experiences: €300 of the deposit is non-refundable if you cancel. From 30 to 21 days before arrival 30% is charged, 20 to 14 days 60%, and 13 days or less 100%.
  • Ferry tickets are fully refundable up to 15 days before sailing, and the date can be changed within 2027.
  • Boat days: 30% on confirmation, the rest plus VAT and fuel 30 days before arrival, with the same 30%, 60%, 100% scale inside 30 days. If the captain or port authority cancels for weather, you get it back less a €250 to €300 handling fee. Fuel can move with the market; you hear about any change in advance.
  • Crew tips are not included; 10 to 15% is customary.
  • Everything is arranged by Original Senses, my partner in Athens, under their full terms. Any change made once you are in Greece is charged in full.
