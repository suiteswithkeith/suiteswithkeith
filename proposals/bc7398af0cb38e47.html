01 · Sifnos · June 16 to 21

The island where Greece slows down

Sifnos is my pick for the most underrated island in Greece. It's a food island first: the best tavernas in the Cyclades, pottery villages, hiking paths between white chapels, and beaches you can walk down to. Five nights here and you'll stop checking the time.

I'd stay at one of two small, beautifully designed hotels. NOS is my pick: quiet, close to the water, and the room I'd book has its own pool. Stamna is up in Apollonia, the main village, with an excellent restaurant and a view across the whole Cyclades.

Option A · my pick

NOS

One of my favorite stays in the Cyclades. NOS sits above Faros, a little fishing village with some of the best waterfront tavernas on the island, an easy walk down for dinner. It's quiet luxury on purpose: no scene, east-facing views, and sunrises you'll actually get up for. The Faros Superior is the room I'd book.

02 · Folegandros · June 21 to 26

Gundari, the coolest hotel in the Cyclades

Gundari is number two on my Greece Top 10, and the one I can't stop talking about. You leave the port, turn onto a dirt road and drive out toward the edge of the island, and then this dark stone building appears on the cliff, set into 80 acres of raw, wild land. I've never seen anything like it.

There's no airport on Folegandros, so the people who make it here really want to be here. When I stayed, nearly every other guest was on their honeymoon. Days are simple: your own infinity pool, a very good spa, the outdoor shower looking at the Aegean, and dinner in Chora, one of the most beautiful villages in Greece. Five nights here is a gift. An idea for between the islands

Private boats, not ferries

The ferries between Sifnos and Folegandros are infrequent and run on their own schedule, not yours. One idea: go by private boat instead. You leave when you're ready, your bags are handled, and a driver is waiting at the port on the other side.
