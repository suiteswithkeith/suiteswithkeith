01 · Milos · June 17 to 21

Erēma, a private estate above Plaka

Erēma is the newest hotel on Milos from the group behind Cosme and Parīlio on Paros, and when I stayed in June it felt more like a private estate than a resort. It's all suites, the design is quiet and confident, and every suite has its own pool with the Aegean straight ahead. Plaka, the prettiest village on the island, is just below you for dinner.

Milos is the island with the beaches, and the best of them you can only reach by water. Give one day to a private boat out to Kleftiko and the west coast: swimming in the sea caves, lunch on board, back in time for sunset from your pool. I'll set it up.

02 · Folegandros · June 21 to 24

Gundari, the coolest hotel in the Cyclades

Gundari is number two on my Greece Top 10, and the one I can't stop talking about. You leave the port, turn onto a dirt road and drive out toward the edge of the island, and then this dark stone building appears on the cliff, set into 80 acres of raw, wild land. I've never seen anything like it.

There's no airport on Folegandros, so the people who make it here really want to be here. When I stayed, nearly every other guest was on their honeymoon. Days are simple: your own infinity pool, a very good spa, the outdoor shower looking at the Aegean, and dinner in Chora, one of the most beautiful villages in Greece. I partner with Gundari directly, so I can look after you here more closely than almost anywhere.

03 · Santorini · June 24 to 27

Three nights in Oia, and four ways to do it

All four options are Canaves. I stayed at each of them in June, Canaves Oia Suites is my favorite hotel on the caldera, and I'm one of the brand's top agents, which matters when it comes to upgrades and which suite you're actually given.

The real choice is caldera or quiet. Oia Suites and Ena sit right on the cliff with the classic view. Epitome is a short walk outside the village, off the caldera, where you get more space, far fewer people, and the sun setting straight into the open sea instead of behind the cliff.

Option A · my top pick

Canaves Oia Suites

Cave suites carved into the cliff, polished but never stuffy. It sits higher on the caldera than most, so it's more private than you'd expect for Oia, and the recent renovation shows everywhere you look. For a first trip to Santorini, this is the one I'd book without thinking twice.

Superior Suite with plunge pool45 sqm · caldera views€4,500

Option B · the best value

Canaves Ena

The intimate sister hotel, with just 18 suites at the highest point of Oia, built into 17th-century wine caves. It's adults-focused, the swim-up pool bar is a lot of fun, and the village is a short walk away. You give up a little space compared with Oia Suites, but not the view.

Superior Suite with plunge pool55 sqm · caldera views€3,600

Option C · the most privacy

Canaves Epitome

My favorite hotel across nine islands this summer, and number one on my Greece Top 10. It's just outside Oia, behind volcanic stone walls and gardens, and the sunset here is the best in Greece. The Honeymoon Suite is on the top floor. Pick this if privacy matters more to you than the postcard view.

Between the islands
