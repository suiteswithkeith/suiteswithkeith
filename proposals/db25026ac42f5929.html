02 · Paros · Sep 25 to 29

Vione, quiet design and your own plunge pool

Vione is the kind of hotel that quietly impresses you. Clean architecture, understated interiors and a calm, adults-focused feel, with every detail considered.

It sits just outside Naoussa, so dinner in the old port is ten minutes away, and with the car you have the beaches on the east coast and the hill villages of Lefkes and Marpissa.

03 · Sifnos · Sep 29 to Oct 4

Verina Astra, a cliff over the Aegean

The most romantic stay on Sifnos: a handful of suites on the cliff at Poulati, with Kastro on foot and Bostani, the hotel's farm-to-table restaurant, for your dress-up dinner.

Sifnos is the island where Greece slows down. Days are about long lunches, pottery villages and swimming at quiet coves, and with the car you can reach all of them.
