Tipping guide

Appreciated but never expected in Italy or Greece — comfortable ranges, always in euros, cash.

  • Restaurants: service is usually included; round up or leave 5 to 10 percent for a great meal
  • Hotel porters: 2 to 5 euros per bag · Housekeeping: about 5 euros per day, left at checkout
  • Concierge: 10 to 20 euros when they arrange something special
  • Drivers and transfers: 10 to 20 euros for an airport run or point to point; more for a long day
  • Boat skippers: about 10 percent of the charter for a great day
  • Helicopter and jet crews: not expected; 20 to 50 euros per pilot if you would like to
  • VIP greeters and guides: 10 to 20 euros
