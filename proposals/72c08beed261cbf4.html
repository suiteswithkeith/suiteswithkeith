Step one · Pick your route

Six stops, or five with more Greece

The full route. Paris 3 nights, the Riviera 3, Lake Como 2, Venice 2, Rome 2 and Santorini 4. Everything on your list, with a lot of packing and unpacking.

My recommendation. Paris 3, the Riviera 3, Venice 2, Rome 2 and Greece 6. Same trip, one fewer hotel change, and two more nights on the island, which is where most families wish they'd had more time.

Lake Como is the stop I'd cut, not Venice. Here's why.

Why Lake Como, and not Venice

Three days on the Riviera already give you the villas, the boats and the swimming off the rocks. Lake Como is beautiful, but two days later it would feel like more of the same, and with only two nights most of it goes to getting there and back. Venice is unlike anywhere else on earth, and it's the stop a 17-year-old is most likely to remember.

It's also the easier trip

From the Riviera you can fly straight from Nice to Venice in about an hour and a quarter, then take the fast train to Rome. Getting to Lake Como means a four to five hour drive or a train through Milan. And the full route costs about the same in hotels as my version, so dropping Como buys you two extra nights in Greece for the same money.

Getting between stops

Trains where they're easy, flights where they save a day

I'll book every leg for you, with drivers waiting at each station, airport and dock so nobody is dragging four suitcases across Venice or up a Santorini hill.

Paris to the Riviera

The fast train from Gare de Lyon is about 5.5 hours to Antibes, and a lovely ride. The flight to Nice is about an hour and a half and saves most of a day. With only three nights in Paris, I lean toward flying.

The Riviera to Venice, or to Lake Como

To Venice, a short direct flight from Nice, about an hour and a quarter, then a water taxi to your hotel. To Lake Como, a private driver along the coast and up through Italy, about four and a half hours. That's the leg that makes the full route harder.

Through Italy

Italy's fast trains are the way to go: Lake Como to Venice is about three and a half hours with a change in Milan, and Venice to Rome is about four hours, city center to city center. I'll book first class seats together.

Rome to the islands

Fly Rome to Athens, about two hours, then Athens to Santorini, about 45 minutes. As we discussed, that flight beats the helicopter by a mile: the helicopter runs about €6,000 for the four of you, and the flight costs a small fraction of that. For Mykonos it's a similar short flight, and Ios is a short ferry ride from Santorini. At the end, you fly back to Athens and home to Vancouver.
