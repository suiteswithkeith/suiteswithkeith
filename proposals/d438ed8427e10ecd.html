Getting Between the Islands

Ferries, flights & airport transfers

Greek island transfers are their own kind of planning — ferry schedules shift with the wind, and the right connection turns a travel day into part of the trip. Here’s exactly how you'll move from the airport through four islands and home again — two Delta legs across the Atlantic, a private car in from the airport, three Seajets high-speed catamarans between the islands, and the early Aegean hop home — each one booked and timed around the day it belongs to.
