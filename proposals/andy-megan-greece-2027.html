Cosme, the best beach stay on Paros

Cosme is my pick for a beach stay on Paros. The base rooms are bigger than many hotels' junior suites, the private beach club sits right in front, and the circular spa and gym are some of the most interesting buildings on the island.

Naoussa is a short walk, so no car all week. Easy and laid-back after the Athens pace.

03 · Sifnos · May 15 to 18

Nos, the quiet island in the middle

Nos is one of my favorite stays in the Cyclades: minimalist Cycladic architecture above the fishing village of Faros, facing east for sunrise, with your own plunge pool.

Sifnos is the island for two foodies who like a trail: clay-pot cooking, the Kastro to Chrysopigi coastal walk, and tavernas that are worth the trip on their own.

My pick

One more boat day, on Santorini

With the helicopter from Sifnos, one more day on the water is worth it. You'll already see the best of Paros and Antiparos on the boat to Sifnos, so I'd save it for the caldera. Either a full day of swimming at Red Beach, White Beach and the hot springs, finishing with the sunset off Oia, or a half day: a morning sail when the water is calmest, or an evening one timed to the sunset.

Santorini

Wine tasting and a long lunch

An afternoon at a couple of the island's best wineries, tasting the volcanic Assyrtiko the island is known for, with a long lunch looking out over the caldera. A driver takes you there and back.
