Twenty-seven nights in Greece: a road trip through the Peloponnese, then three islands by ferry. Everything below runs in order, with confirmation numbers under each item.

Kathryn: your passport reads Katherine, so that is the name to give at check-ins and ferry ports.

Tipping guide

Appreciated but never expected in Greece. Comfortable ranges, always in euros, cash.

  • Restaurants: round up or leave 5 to 10 percent for a great meal
  • Hotel porters: 2 to 5 euros per bag · Housekeeping: about 5 euros per day, left at checkout
  • Concierge: 10 to 20 euros when they arrange something special
  • Drivers and transfers: 10 to 20 euros for a port or airport run
  • Boat skippers: about 10 percent of the charter for a great day
