01 · Paros · May 10 to 14

Cosme, the best beach stay on Paros

Cosme is my pick for a beach stay on Paros. The base rooms are bigger than many hotels' junior suites, the private beach club sits right in front of the hotel, and the circular spa and gym are some of the most interesting buildings on the island.

Between the beach, the spa and the restaurants you could happily stay put, and Naoussa is a short walk for dinner.

02 · Sifnos · May 14 to 18

Nos, the island where Greece slows down

Nos is one of my favorite stays in the Cyclades. Minimalist Cycladic architecture above the fishing village of Faros, sweeping Aegean views and a pace that matches Sifnos exactly.

Sifnos is the cooking island: pottery villages along a ridge, the best tavernas in the Cyclades for an island its size, and in May it is green and walkable.

03 · Santorini · May 18 to 22

Canaves Oia Suites, the classic caldera finale

This was the best hotel I saw for honeymooners on Santorini. A full renovation brought beautiful finishes everywhere, and it sits higher on the caldera than most, so it is noticeably more private than the rest of Oia.

After eight quiet nights you have earned the postcard: the sunset from your own terrace, and Oia's lanes a short walk away.
