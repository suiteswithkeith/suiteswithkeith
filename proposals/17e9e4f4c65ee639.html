01 · Athens · June 8 to 9

One night in Athens, three ways to do it

You land the morning of June 8, which is enough for the Acropolis, a long lunch and an early night before the ferry. I've quoted three hotels for the night, all refundable, so pick the one that suits you.

A77 Suites is twelve suites in a restored 19th-century house in Plaka, a short walk to the Acropolis. Xenodocheio Milos is the hotel from Estiatorio Milos, with a MICHELIN Key and great seafood downstairs. Conrad Athens is the full-service option, with a big pool terrace and spa.

02 · Paros · June 9 to 13

Vione, quiet design and your own pool

Vione is the kind of hotel that quietly impresses you. Newer, quieter and more private than the other Paros options, with clean architecture, understated interiors and the best sunset of the bunch.

Naoussa's old port is ten minutes away for dinner, and the boat day leaves for Antiparos and Despotiko from just down the coast.

03 · Santorini · June 13 to 16

Canaves Ena, eighteen suites on the caldera

Ena is where the Canaves story started, in suites that trace back to 17th-century wine caves. With just 18 suites it feels intimate, and it sits higher on the caldera than most, so it is noticeably more private.

It is at the entrance to Oia's lane rather than in the middle of it, so it stays quiet, and the swim-up pool bar is a fun extra on a honeymoon.
