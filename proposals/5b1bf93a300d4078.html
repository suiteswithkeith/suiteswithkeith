01 · Santorini · Sep 15 to 19

Canaves Oia Suites, the classic caldera stay

This was the best hotel I saw for first-timers on Santorini. A full renovation brought beautiful finishes everywhere, and it sits higher on the caldera than most, so it is noticeably more private than the rest of Oia.

Every suite has its own water and covered outdoor space, which matters in September sun. Oia's lanes and the sunset are a short walk away.

02 · Paros · Sep 19 to 25

Cosme, the best beach stay on Paros

Cosme is my pick for a beach stay on Paros. The base rooms are bigger than many hotels' junior suites, the private beach club sits right in front of the hotel, and the circular spa and gym are some of the most interesting buildings on the island.

Naoussa is a short walk for dinner, and with the car you have the whole island, from Lefkes in the hills to the beaches on the south coast.
