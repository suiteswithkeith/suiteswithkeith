Napa & Sonoma at Christmas

December 19 to 26, 2026 · Four travelers · Seven nights

A first trip to wine country for you, your husband, your son and his fiancée. Northern California is my home region, and Christmas week there is quiet, green and cozy, with fire pits on every terrace. Below are two ways to do the week, each with a little space for both couples. Most options come in under your $18,000 budget, and I've marked a couple of stretch options in case they're worth it to you.

The plan

One resort all week, or the coast first

True two-bedroom suites in wine country are rare and get expensive fast at Christmas, so for most of these hotels I've paired a one-bedroom suite with a room next door, or two rooms that connect. You and your husband get the suite with the living room, the kids get their own room, and everyone still has a door to close.

Plan A keeps you in one place for all seven nights. Plan B starts with two nights on the Sonoma Coast, with ocean views, fireplaces and a slower pace, then moves inland for five nights so you wake up in wine country on Christmas morning. Doing the coast first also skips the pricier weekend nights at the resorts, which is why Plan B costs less overall.

Plan B · Part one · Sonoma Coast · Dec 19 to 21

Two nights by the ocean

About 90 minutes north of San Francisco, and under an hour from Healdsburg. Both lodges are small and quiet, and both work well as a calm start before the wine country part of the week. Prices are for two rooms, one per couple.

Option B1 · easy and cozy

The Lodge at Bodega Bay

A classic coastal lodge looking out over the bay and the Pacific. Rooms have fireplaces and balconies, there's a pool and hot tub facing the water, and the restaurant focuses on local seafood. It's also the closer of the two, so less time in the car.

Option B2 · wild and quiet

The Sea Ranch Lodge

A tiny, newly redone lodge with just 17 rooms, on the bluffs of The Sea Ranch, famous for its 1960s architecture. It's a longer, very scenic drive up Highway 1. Come for walking trails along the cliffs, the chapel and a well-regarded dining room. It's quieter and more remote than Bodega Bay.
