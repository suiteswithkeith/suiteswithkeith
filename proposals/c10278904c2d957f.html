Napa & Sonoma at Christmas

December 19 to 26, 2026 · Four travelers · Seven nights

A first trip to wine country for you, your husband, your son and his fiancée. Northern California is my home region, and Christmas week there is quiet, green and cozy, with fire pits on every terrace. Here are my favorite resorts for your family, each set up so both couples have their own space.

Step one · How you spend the week

All week in wine country, or start by the water

The full week (Montage Healdsburg, Four Seasons Napa Valley, Stanly Ranch or Solage). Seven nights at one resort. Unpack once and settle in.

Coast first (Bodega Bay Lodge or Sea Ranch Lodge). Two nights at Bodega Bay on the Sonoma Coast, with ocean views and fireplaces, then five nights at the resort, so you wake up in wine country on Christmas morning.

Golden Gate first (Cavallo Point). Two nights at Cavallo Point, right under the Golden Gate Bridge, with Sausalito at your doorstep and an easy ferry into San Francisco. Then up into Napa or Sonoma for five nights.

Both two-night starts skip the resorts' pricier weekend nights, which is why they cost less than the full week.

Where everything is

The coast and the vineyards

Bodega Bay is under an hour from Montage in Healdsburg, and a little over an hour from Calistoga and Stanly Ranch. Sea Ranch is farther up the coast and more remote. Cavallo Point sits at the foot of the Golden Gate Bridge, about 50 minutes from Stanly Ranch and about an hour and 15 minutes from Healdsburg or Calistoga.

My pick

Montage Healdsburg

My home away from home. I've stayed here more than 20 times, more than any other hotel in wine country. The vineyard rooms, with their fire pits looking over the vines, are the reason I keep going back, and the restaurant has the best view of any dining room in the region. A free shuttle runs into downtown Healdsburg, so nobody has to drive home from dinner.

If you go coast first · Dec 19 to 21

Two nights by the ocean

About 90 minutes north of San Francisco. Both lodges are small and quiet, a calm start before the wine country part of the week. Prices are for two rooms, one per couple.

Both lodges have nicer room categories too, like Bodega Bay's Ocean Club suites. Happy to upgrade either couple if you'd like.

My pick for the coast

Bodega Bay Lodge

A classic coastal lodge looking out over the bay and the Pacific, with fireplaces and balconies in the rooms, a pool and hot tub facing the water, and a restaurant focused on local seafood. It's the closer of the two to wine country.

Two bay-view rooms with fireplace · 2 nights$2,325

The Sea Ranch Lodge

A tiny, newly redone lodge with just 17 rooms on the bluffs of The Sea Ranch, famous for its 1960s architecture. Come for cliffside walking trails, the chapel and a well-regarded dining room. It's quieter and more remote, and a longer, very scenic drive.

Two king rooms · 2 nights$3,306

If you start at the Golden Gate · Dec 19 to 21

Two nights under the bridge

Cavallo Point sits right under the Golden Gate Bridge, in the restored buildings of old Fort Baker. It's a beautiful area, with Sausalito at your doorstep, an easy ferry into the city, and redwoods out your back door. A lovely place to start for two nights, then up into the Napa or Sonoma valley.
