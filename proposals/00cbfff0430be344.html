Four nights on Milos

September 27 to October 1, 2027 · Two travelers · Four nights

Milos is the island my clients ask about most, and late September is my favorite time to go: the sea is still warm, the summer crowds are gone, and hotel rates drop. Here are the two places I'd look at for a stay that feels special without the top-of-the-island price tag, plus one splurge if you'd like to compare.

Why these hotels

All the Milos you came for, at a gentler price

The top hotels on Milos run €1,000 a night and up. Onero and White Pebble come in around €600 to €750 a night for late September, with breakfast every morning, sea views from every room and the island right on your doorstep.

The real choice is how you want your evenings to feel. Onero is a new resort on a quiet hillside with private pools and a short drive to everything. White Pebble is a small hotel above the fishing village of Pollonia, where you can walk to dinner every night. And if you'd like to see what the splurge looks like, I've included Erēma too.

Prices are totals for all four nights, with breakfast included, and all of them are fully refundable until the dates noted.

Option A · my pick for value

Onero

The hotel Milos has been waiting for. It's brand new, built into the hillside at Parasporos, and every one of its 31 suites faces the sea. Rooms are on the smaller side, the terraces do the living, and the ground-floor suites have their own pools. You're ten minutes from the port, fifteen from Plaka for sunset and eighteen from Sarakiniko, so I'd rent a car here.

Option B · walk to dinner

White Pebble Suites

A polished little hotel above Pollonia, one of the most charming villages on Milos, and I loved it when I stayed in June. Some of the island's best restaurants are a short stroll away, and the waterfront is the place to be in the evening. It's a village hotel, so it isn't as private as a resort, but the location does half the work.
