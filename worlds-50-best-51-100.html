My picks

Ten I’d send you to tomorrow

NO. 56LondonBookable + perks

Rosewood London

A Belle Époque bank reborn off High Holborn — the courtyard entrance alone is worth the stay.

Right forA grand London base — Covent Garden and the West End on foot.
Skip ifYou want small and boutique; this is stately, not intimate.
WhenYear-round — December, with the lights up, is the sleeper.
NO. 58Positano, ItalyBookable + perks

Il San Pietro di Positano

Family-run cliffside legend — every room a private terrace, and a lift carved through rock to its own beach.

Right forHoneymoons and big anniversaries — Amalfi at full volume.
Skip ifYou’re bringing young kids; it’s a formal, grown-up house.
WhenLate May–June or September; July–August is the crush.

NO. 62Montalcino, TuscanyBookable + perks

Castiglion del Bosco

An 800-year-old Val d’Orcia estate — Brunello vineyards, a private golf club and suites in the old borgo.

Right forSlow wine weeks — Brunello tastings, drives through Val d’Orcia.
Skip ifYou want to walk to dinner in town; the estate is gloriously remote.
WhenMay–June, or September–October for harvest.
Read the full brief →
NO. 66RomeBookable + perks

Orient Express La Minerva

The storied Palazzo Fonseca reawakened — Roman grandeur with a rooftop looking straight at the Pantheon.

Right forA first Rome trip done at the top level — the Pantheon is out the front door.
Skip ifYou’re after quiet seclusion; you’re in the thick of the centro.
WhenApril–June or September–October.
NO. 67Cabo San Lucas, MexicoBookable + perks

Montage Los Cabos

On swimmable Santa Maria Bay — rare for Cabo — with barefoot luxury done properly.

Right forFamilies and groups — Cabo’s rare swimmable beach.
Skip ifYou want walkable nightlife; downtown is a drive.
WhenNovember–May; summer runs hot.
Read the full brief →
NO. 68Brixen, ItalyBookable + perks

Forestis Dolomites

A former sanatorium at 1,800 metres — pure Dolomite air, spring water and floor-to-ceiling peaks.

Right forA phones-down spa reset — silence, saunas, mountain air.
Skip ifYou need a scene; evenings here are a whisper.
WhenDecember–March for snow, June–September for trails.
NO. 74La Manzanilla, MexicoBookable + perks

Four Seasons Tamarindo

3,000 acres of protected Pacific coast — architecture that frames the wild, not the other way around.

Right forGoing properly off-grid — empty coast, golf, night skies.
Skip ifYou want restaurants and town life nearby; there are none, by design.
WhenNovember–April.
Read the full brief →
NO. 81Hanoi, VietnamBookable + perks

Capella Hanoi

Bill Bensley’s opera-house fever dream — 47 art-stuffed rooms a curtain-call from the Opera House.

Right forDesign lovers and city explorers — the Old Quarter is close by.
Skip ifMinimalist calm is your thing; this is maximalism, joyfully.
WhenOctober–April; summer is a steam room.
NO. 84Amalfi, ItalyBookable + perks

Borgo Santandrea

1960s Italian glamour above Conca dei Marini — hand-made tiles, a private beach club, 30 rooms.

Right forThe Amalfi Coast without Positano’s crowds — plus a real beach club.
Skip ifYou want to be inside Positano’s buzz; this side is quieter.
WhenMay–June or September.
NO. 95Lisciano Niccone, UmbriaBookable + perks

Reschio

An Etruscan castle restored over thirty years — estate gin in the minibar, a spa in the old wine cellar.

Right forRomance and long dinners — riding at dawn, a spa in the cellar.
Skip ifYou need a beach or a scene; this is deep countryside.
WhenMay–June or September–October.
