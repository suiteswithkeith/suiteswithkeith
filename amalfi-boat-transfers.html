There is no airport on the Amalfi Coast, and every road in is a cliffside switchback.

After an overnight flight, an hour and a half of hairpin turns is nobody’s idea of an arrival. A private boat replaces it. Your driver meets you at arrivals with your name on a sign, takes you to the harbor, and a captain runs you down the coast to your hotel. You arrive the way the coast is meant to be seen: from the water, with the villages stacked up the cliffs in front of you.

These are arranged individually. Boat, captain, route and timing depend on your flight, your hotel and the sea that day. One thing to know up front: I am not a boat-ticket desk. The transfer comes as part of the hotel work, for trips whose hotels I am booking, or as an add-on to a hotel booking you have already made and hand over to me to manage.

No. 01

Why go private

The arrival, upgraded

Best for · honeymoons, anyone who gets carsick, families with luggage

The drive from Naples is beautiful and a lot of curves, and it comes right after a long flight. By boat, the first hour of your trip is the best one: open water, the coast coming into view, and nothing to do but sit back.

No winding road. The boat skips the switchbacks entirely. If you know you cannot do the road, this is how I build your arrival from the start.

Met at arrivals. Your driver is waiting with your name on a sign, the bags are handled, and the car takes you straight to the boat.

Straight to your hotel. Where the hotel can take a boat, you step off at its own beach club or landing. Where it cannot, we come in at the nearest harbor and a car is waiting.

This is the part of an Amalfi trip I spend the most time on for clients, and the part you should never have to think about.

No. 02

The routes that work

Pick the port by the hotel

Rule of thumb · Naples for Positano, Salerno for Amalfi

Where you are staying decides where the boat leaves from. Get that right and the transfer is short, calm and direct.

Naples → Positano. For Il San Pietro, Villa Treville and Le Sirenuse. Out of the Bay of Naples, around the tip of the peninsula and along the cliffs to Positano.

Salerno → Amalfi side. For Santa Caterina, Miramalfi and Borgo Santandrea. The shortest, calmest way in.

Fly into Salerno. Salerno airport now has flights, which puts you much closer to the coast than Naples. Pair it with the boat from Salerno and you are at the hotel almost as soon as you land.

Capri ↔ the coast. Add a few nights on Capri before or after, and the boat is the only sensible way between the two.

Hotel to hotel. Splitting the stay between Positano and the Amalfi side? The boat turns check-out day into a ride along the whole coast.

When a boat is not right. The closest helipad is in Scala, up near Ravello, about 20 to 25 minutes by car from most of these hotels. I have the car waiting when you land.

Tell me the flight you land on and the hotel you end at. I will build everything in between.

No. 03

Good to know before you book

The honest fine print

Read this · before you commit to a date

A private boat is a small boat, and the sea gets a vote. None of this is a reason not to do it. It is just what separates a smooth arrival from a memorable one for the wrong reasons.

The sea decides on the day. If it is too rough to run, the boat does not go. I always have a car on standby, so a rough sea changes how you arrive, never whether you arrive, and never costs you a hotel night.

Pricing is per boat, not per person. It depends on the route, the boat and the season, so I quote it to your dates. Split between a family or two couples, it is a very different number than it sounds.

Luggage has limits. Most boats handle a couple of large cases per couple comfortably. If you are travelling heavy, tell me up front and I will size the boat, or send the bags ahead by car.

Both ends are handled. The driver at arrivals, the porters, the boat and the hotel all know the timing. Nobody is waiting on a dock wondering where you are.

Book it early in season. Roughly May through September, the good captains and the good boats are spoken for weeks out.

Handled properly, it is the best first hour of the trip. Let’s handle it properly.
