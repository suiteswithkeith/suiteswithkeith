Passalacqua or the EDITION?

A 24-room villa that only its guests can enter, or a 148-room modern hotel with a lively scene. Passalacqua is the best honeymoon hotel on Lake Como. The EDITION is a neat, modern hotel that’s right for a lot of travelers. Here is how I would choose.

The question behind the question

Is this the trip that calls for the pinnacle?

Passalacqua is unlike anywhere else in the world: a 1787 villa that’s meant to feel like your own home, with just 24 rooms and no outside guests. It’s the best honeymoon hotel on Lake Como and the one I reserve for trips that genuinely warrant it. I loved it, but it isn’t my personal style; with two restaurants and a quiet village, I’d want a livelier base after a couple of nights.

The EDITION is the opposite. It’s a 148-room modern hotel that’s lively, social and see-and-be-seen, closer in mood to West Hollywood or Mykonos than to a classic Como villa. The spa and gym are outstanding, and the two-bedroom suite is good value for a family.

If it’s a honeymoon or a milestone, book Passalacqua; it sits above the EDITION in my ranking. If the trip doesn’t warrant the pinnacle, the EDITION is one of the smart ways into the lake, as long as its scene is your scene.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Privacy or a scene

At Passalacqua, the restaurants and spa are closed to outside visitors, which is a big part of why it feels so private. Staff remember your name. If you’re a client staying nearby and want to see it, I can sometimes arrange a lunch or a tour.

The EDITION is built for energy: lively, social and see-and-be-seen, with the brand’s signature Le Labo scent and a younger, mostly American crowd. It suits LGBTQ+ travelers especially well. If that isn’t your scene, you’ll be happier somewhere quieter.

The rooms to book

At Passalacqua, the Junior Suite Al Lago is the one; it opens right toward the water. Rooms in the Palazzo and the lakeside rooms are generously sized, while rooms elsewhere can be small, and most rooms see the lake in the distance. With so few rooms, it’s the single hardest confirmation on the lake, so hold dates early.

At the EDITION, book a lake-view room on the third floor or above. Lower floors sit above the loud casual restaurant, and non-lake-view rooms face the spa across the street or an interior courtyard. A non-lake-view booking won’t be upgraded to a lake view without a paid supplement. For families, the two-bedroom suite is around $5,500 a night, and good value.

Spa, gym and grounds

Passalacqua’s terraced gardens fall from the villa to the water. The spa is Moroccan-inspired, with vaulted ceilings, old stone columns and peacock wicker chairs, and an underground tunnel leads down to the lower gardens. The sunken clay court is #04 on my tennis list.

The EDITION wins on wellness. The spa has three saunas, a steam room and an indoor heated pool, and the gym is one of the best I’ve seen in a hotel, with free weights up to 40 kilos and a squat rack. Its one outdoor pool floats on the lake, which I’m not a fan of; a second is planned for next season.

Dining, and getting around

Passalacqua has only two restaurants, and Moltrasio is a quiet village without much going on, so plan a few dinners across the lake by boat. It’s about an hour from Milan by private car. One small note: clients who stayed recently found the pool service slow.

The EDITION has three or four outlets, all on the expensive side, and there’s no town at the door. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is a short walk away for meals. The hotel has no boats of its own, so I book boats for my clients, and the arrival I recommend is a private boat from Como.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Passalacqua or the Lake Como EDITION?

For a honeymoon or a milestone, Passalacqua. It’s the best honeymoon hotel on the lake and ranks above the EDITION in my list. The EDITION is right for younger travelers, LGBTQ+ travelers, serious gym-goers and families in the two-bedroom suite.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

Passalacqua. It’s #05 in The Fifteen, my honeymoon shortlist. I’d pair it with Il Sereno, ten minutes away by boat: here’s the pairing.

Can I visit Passalacqua without staying there?

Generally, no. Passalacqua’s restaurants and spa are for hotel guests only. If you’re a client of mine staying nearby, I can sometimes arrange a lunch or a tour.

Which is better for families?

The EDITION, thanks to its two-bedroom suite, around $5,500 a night and good value for a family sharing one suite. Passalacqua is best for honeymoons, milestones and two couples traveling together.

Which room should I book at each?

At Passalacqua, the Junior Suite Al Lago, or a room in the Palazzo or a lakeside room. At the EDITION, a lake-view room on the third floor or above.

How do I get to each one?

For Passalacqua, a private car from Milan, about an hour. For the EDITION, a private car from Milan to the water in Como, then a private boat; the road adds about 45 minutes beyond Como. I arrange both.
