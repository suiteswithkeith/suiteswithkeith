Head-to-head · Venice

Aman Venice or Airelles Palladio?

Two of the best hotels in Venice, built for different trips. Aman is a 24-room palazzo on the Grand Canal; Airelles Palladio is a garden estate with pools on Giudecca, five minutes across the water. Here is how I would choose.

Toured both · October 2026

Choose Airelles Palladio

My pick of the two

The pools, the three-story spa, four restaurants and four bars, gardens on a scale Venice does not have anywhere else, and the best family suites in the city. It is the most beautiful hotel I have seen in Italy.

Choose Aman Venice

If you want to be Aman, on the Grand Canal

Twenty-four rooms in a restored palazzo on the Grand Canal, with Aman’s quiet, personal service and real privacy. It is one of the most beautiful Amans I have seen, and the natural pairing with Aman Rosa Alpina.

The question behind the question

Why I would book Airelles Palladio

I toured both on the same day in October 2026, along with the Four Seasons Danieli and Orient Express. Both are fantastic, and they serve different purposes.

If you are comparing what a hotel gives you, Airelles Palladio wins: Aman has no pool, a smaller spa and fewer places to eat and drink. Airelles is also the better choice for families, and for me it is simply the more beautiful of the two. It is my number one hotel in Italy.

Aman wins at being Aman. It is smaller, more private and more personal, with a location on the Grand Canal in the middle of the city, and it connects beautifully to Aman Rosa Alpina in the Dolomites. For couples, either one is a wonderful choice.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location: in the city, or facing it

Aman sits on the Grand Canal in San Polo, a short walk from the Rialto. You step out into Venice.

Airelles is on Giudecca, across the water from the Zattere and five minutes by boat from St. Mark’s. Some travelers will want the center; many will prefer coming home to peace and quiet in a very touristy city. I would argue that is the selling point.

Pools, spa and dining

This is where Airelles pulls ahead. It has a long outdoor pool in the gardens, an indoor pool in a three-story Guerlain spa, four restaurants and four bars.

Aman has a small spa with three treatment suites, a fitness center, Arva for Italian dining and a bar overlooking its Canal Garden. There is no pool.

Families

Airelles, without question. There is a kids club, room to roam, a three-bedroom suite with its own private pool, and the four-bedroom Suite del Doge for the whole family.

Aman’s two three-bedroom apartments can house a family, but the hotel is built for calm, and there is no pool.

The service

Both are exceptional. At Aman I met the general manager, Licinio Garavaglia, who is genuinely a fantastic person, and I know he will take great care of my clients.

Airelles was lively in October without ever being loud. Private and secure, and still social and fun. Sophisticated, never stuffy.

The top suites

Airelles has the Suite del Doge, 450 square meters with four bedrooms in Villa Frollo, which can be privatized to 13 bedrooms.

Aman has Coccina’s Apartment on the fourth floor, reached by a private elevator, at 290 square meters with three bedrooms, frescoes and painted ceilings, and the three-bedroom Grand Canal Apartment at 226 square meters.

Pairing with the Dolomites

Aman offers two nights at Aman Venice and three nights at Aman Rosa Alpina with transfers included. It is an easy way to see Venice and the mountains in one trip.

For a faster connection, I can arrange a helicopter between the two. The nearest helipad is about eight minutes from Aman Venice.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Aman Venice or Airelles Palladio?

For most travelers, Airelles Palladio. It has the pools, the larger spa, more restaurants and bars, and it is the most beautiful hotel I have seen in Italy. Choose Aman Venice if you want a small, very private palazzo on the Grand Canal with classic Aman service, or if you are pairing Venice with Aman Rosa Alpina in the Dolomites.

Which is better for families?

Airelles Palladio, clearly. It has outdoor and indoor pools, a kids club, gardens to run around in, a three-bedroom suite with its own private pool and the four-bedroom Suite del Doge. Aman Venice has no pool and is built for quiet.

Which is better for couples?

Both are excellent. Pick Aman if you want to be in the middle of the city and value privacy above everything. Pick Airelles if you want a resort with a pool, a three-story spa and a lively bar scene to come back to.

Does Aman Venice have a pool?

No. Aman Venice has a small spa with three treatment suites and a fitness center, but no pool. Airelles Palladio has an outdoor pool in the gardens and an indoor pool in its spa.

Is Airelles Palladio too far from the center of Venice?

No. It is on Giudecca, and the hotel boat reaches St. Mark’s in about five minutes. I worried it would feel isolated, and it does not. Many travelers will prefer having a calm side of the water to come home to.

Can you combine Aman Venice with Aman Rosa Alpina?

Yes. Aman offers a combination stay of two nights at Aman Venice and three nights at Aman Rosa Alpina in the Dolomites, with transfers included. I can also arrange a helicopter between the two; the nearest helipad is about eight minutes from Aman Venice.

What are the largest suites at each hotel?

At Airelles Palladio, the Suite del Doge: 450 square meters (about 4,800 square feet) with four bedrooms, in Villa Frollo, which can be privatized to 13 bedrooms. At Aman Venice, Coccina’s Apartment: 290 square meters (3,122 square feet) with three bedrooms and a private elevator, and the Grand Canal Apartment: 226 square meters (2,432 square feet) with three bedrooms.
