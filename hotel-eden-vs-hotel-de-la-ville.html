The question behind the question

Do you want a rooftop for sunset drinks, or a quiet street to come home to?

Hotel Eden and the Hotel de la Ville are a short walk apart above the Spanish Steps, and they feel very different once you step outside.

The Hotel de la Ville is right at the top of the Steps, which are crowded from morning to night. Its highlight is Cielo, the rooftop bar and restaurant across the top two floors. Honestly, I do not have a ton to say beyond that, and between the two Rocco Forte hotels I prefer the de Russie.

Hotel Eden is on a calm residential street, with anticipatory service, bright rooms and its own restaurant upstairs. It is my top overall pick in Rome, and the one I would book here.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Look and feel

The Hotel de la Ville is an 18th-century palazzo with colorful, very Roman interiors by Tommaso Ziffer: yellow walls hung with engravings, plaster reliefs and black and white floors.

Hotel Eden is modern and traditional at once, from the gilded lobby lounge to rooms that are bright and beautifully decorated.

Two rooftops

Cielo at the Hotel de la Ville spreads across the top two floors, with views all over the city. It is the hotel’s highlight, and the place for sunset drinks.

The restaurant upstairs at Hotel Eden had fantastic service, and the view across the domes is the one I send couples to on their first evening in Rome.

Rooms

The Hotel de la Ville has 104 rooms and suites, some with terraces and city views, and marble bathrooms with freestanding tubs.

At Hotel Eden, the rooms have incredible views over the rooftops, and the top suites are spacious and can be connected for families or two couples traveling together.

Quiet, and the better Rocco Forte

The difference you feel every day is the street. The Hotel de la Ville opens onto the crowds at the top of the Steps; Hotel Eden is a short walk away on a calm residential street.

If you like the idea of a Rocco Forte hotel near the Steps, look at the Hotel de Russie too. Its large courtyard, renovated rooms and spa with an indoor pool made it the stronger stay for me.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Hotel Eden or Hotel de la Ville?

Hotel Eden, my top overall pick in Rome. The Hotel de la Ville is nice, with a great rooftop, but I preferred its sister, the de Russie.

Which is closer to the Spanish Steps?

The Hotel de la Ville, right at the top of them on Via Sistina. Hotel Eden is a short walk away on a calm residential street.

Where should I go for sunset drinks?

Cielo, the rooftop bar at the Hotel de la Ville, with views all over the city. At Hotel Eden, dinner upstairs with the view across the domes is my first-evening pick for couples.

Which is quieter?

Hotel Eden. The top of the Spanish Steps, where the de la Ville sits, is crowded from morning to night.

Hotel de la Ville or Hotel de Russie?

The de Russie. They are sister Rocco Forte hotels a few minutes apart, and its courtyard, renovated rooms and indoor pool made it the stronger stay over the de la Ville.
