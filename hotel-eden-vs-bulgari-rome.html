The question behind the question

Do you want to come home to quiet, or to a scene?

Hotel Eden is calm. Warm, anticipatory service, bright rooms and a quiet residential street above the Spanish Steps. It is my top overall pick in Rome.

The Bulgari is energy. It is very modern and lively, almost like an EDITION but much more sophisticated, with base rooms so large you often do not need to upgrade. Step out the door and you are in busy streets, right next to the Mausoleum of Augustus.

If you want quiet and calm, the Eden is the one I would pick. If you want space, a scene and great dining without leaving the hotel, pick the Bulgari.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Room size

The base rooms at the Bulgari are very large. At a lot of hotels they would be called deluxe rooms or even junior suites, so the entry rate already buys real space. In central Rome, that matters.

The rooms at Hotel Eden are bright, modern and traditional at once, with incredible views over the rooftops. For more space, the top suites are spacious on their own and can be connected for families or two couples.

Eating and drinking

Honestly, I am not even sure how many places there are to eat and drink at the Bulgari: a beautiful rooftop bar and pizza restaurant, another bar on the floor below, and downstairs a classic Roman trattoria with affordable dishes like €18 pastas. I loved that.

Hotel Eden has one restaurant I would build an evening around: the one upstairs, with fantastic service and the view across the domes. The concierge is super helpful with reservations everywhere else.

Mood and service

Service at Hotel Eden was anticipatory, warm and personal, and I had the fastest coffee I have ever had delivered there. The whole place is calm.

The Bulgari is lively and sophisticated, with lots of marble and incredible mosaics in the bathrooms. If that energy is what you want from Rome, it is spectacular.

Gym, spa and price

The Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome, massive and with serious equipment, plus a spa. If training on the road matters, that settles it.

Hotel Eden is usually priced slightly below the Bulgari, for a stay I would rank above it. I can quote them side by side for your dates.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Hotel Eden or the Bulgari?

For me, Hotel Eden, my top overall pick in Rome. Pick the Bulgari if you want space, energy and great dining without leaving the hotel.

Which has bigger rooms?

The Bulgari. Its base rooms are what other hotels would sell as deluxe rooms or junior suites. At Hotel Eden, ask about the top suites if space matters.

Which is quieter?

Hotel Eden, on a quiet residential street above the Spanish Steps. The Bulgari is on the lively side, inside and out.

Which has a gym?

The Bulgari has the biggest gym I saw in Rome, plus a spa.

Which costs less?

Usually Hotel Eden, slightly below the Bulgari. Because the Bulgari’s base rooms are so generous, you often do not need to upgrade there.
