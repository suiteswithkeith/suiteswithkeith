The question behind the question

Do you want Rome’s grand-hotel tradition, or the opposite?

The Hassler is a very traditional hotel, and that’s the appeal. You get the top of the Spanish Steps and a Michelin-starred dinner on the roof.

Casa Monti is the hotel I send people to when the Hassler feels too traditional. It is hip, young and fun, and on a Sunday the trattorias around it are full of Romans having lunch.

If grand-hotel style is part of the dream, pick the Hassler. If you are a younger couple who wants something fun and a real neighborhood, pick Casa Monti.

The detail

What to know before you choose

The neighborhood

The Hassler is right above the Spanish Steps, about as central as Rome gets. It also means a busy area: you step out the door and you are in the crowds at the Steps.

Casa Monti is in Monti, and the neighborhood is the real thing. There are authentic Roman trattorias all around, and when I visited on a Sunday, real Romans were having lunch at them.

Rooms and design

The Hassler’s rooms are very traditional, with canopy beds and damask walls, and some bathrooms look over the rooftops to St. Peter’s.

Casa Monti is from the same company as Le Fantaisie in Paris, and it shows: super cool patterns, pattern on pattern, and curtains that hide the TV.

Dining

The dining is a highlight at the Hassler: a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof with the rooftops of Rome laid out below you, and a beautiful restaurant in the courtyard.

Casa Monti has a beautiful rooftop restaurant and a lively restaurant downstairs, and the trattorias outside the door.

Spa

At Casa Monti, couples can book the spa just for the two of them. I arrange it before you arrive.

The Hassler’s large new wellness and gym area opens in 2027. Until then, if a spa matters, I point people to the Bulgari or the Corinthia.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, the Hassler or Casa Monti?

They suit opposite travelers. The Hassler is for classic, traditional luxury at the top of the Spanish Steps. Casa Monti is for younger couples who want something hip, young and fun.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

For younger honeymooners, Casa Monti, and book the private spa. The Hassler suits travelers who love classic luxury; for a classic Rome stay as a couple, my top pick is the Eden.

Which has a spa?

Casa Monti, which couples can book privately. The Hassler’s new wellness and gym area opens in 2027.

Which has the better food?

The Hassler has a Michelin-starred restaurant on the roof. Casa Monti has two restaurants of its own and real Roman trattorias all around.

I want something in between. What else?

For quiet, warm, classic luxury above the Steps, the Eden, my top pick in Rome. For a palazzo by the Pantheon with real-Rome energy, La Minerva.
