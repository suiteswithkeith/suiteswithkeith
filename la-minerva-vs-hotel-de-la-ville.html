The question behind the question

Do you want the Pantheon neighborhood, or the Spanish Steps on your doorstep?

La Minerva is about people. The team is the warmest I met in Rome, and the restaurant upstairs, the bar downstairs and the lobby are shared spaces you actually want to sit in.

The Hotel de la Ville is a nice Rocco Forte hotel right at the top of the Spanish Steps. Its highlight is Cielo, the rooftop bar and restaurant across the top two floors. Honestly, I do not have a ton to say about it beyond that.

If you want a real neighborhood and a team that lets you be yourself, pick La Minerva. If you want the Spanish Steps on your doorstep and a rooftop for sunset drinks, the de la Ville does that, though I would also look at its sister, the de Russie.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Two rooftops

The rooftop restaurant at La Minerva is where I would spend the evening, looking over the domes and rooftops of the centro storico.

At the Hotel de la Ville, Cielo spreads across the top two floors, with views across Rome’s skyline. It is the hotel’s highlight.

Interiors

The Hotel de la Ville is an 18th-century palazzo with colorful, very Roman interiors by Tommaso Ziffer: yellow walls, engravings, plaster reliefs and black and white floors. There are 104 rooms and suites, some with terraces and city views.

La Minerva is an Orient Express palazzo by the Pantheon, and its personality is in the people, the lobby and the bar downstairs.

Spa and the extras

The Hotel de la Ville has a Sicilian-inspired spa, plus the Julep cocktail bar and Mosaico, the main restaurant, on the courtyard.

At La Minerva, Signature Suite bookings through me can include a 50-minute massage for two, on top of daily breakfast, a hotel credit and a VIP welcome. Small thing: the best slippers of my whole trip. I kept them.

The neighborhood

La Minerva puts you by the Pantheon, in a real part of the city, and I appreciated that compared with a lot of its competitors. It is also where I put clients before La Dolce Vita.

The top of the Spanish Steps, where the Hotel de la Ville sits, is crowded from morning to night. For quiet, the Eden is a short walk away on a calm residential street.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, La Minerva or Hotel de la Ville?

It depends on what you want. La Minerva for warmth and a real neighborhood by the Pantheon. The Hotel de la Ville for the Spanish Steps on your doorstep and a great rooftop, though between the Rocco Forte hotels I prefer the de Russie.

Which has the better rooftop?

They are different evenings. Cielo at the Hotel de la Ville is a bar and restaurant across two floors, made for sunset drinks. The rooftop restaurant at La Minerva looks over the domes of the centro storico, and it is where I would spend the evening.

Which is better for younger travelers?

La Minerva, for the warmest shared spaces and the friendliest team I met in Rome.

Which has a spa?

The Hotel de la Ville has a Sicilian-inspired spa. At La Minerva, Signature Suite bookings through me can include a massage for two.

Hotel de la Ville or Hotel de Russie?

The de Russie. The two are sister Rocco Forte hotels a few minutes apart, and its courtyard, renovated rooms and indoor pool made it the stronger stay over the de la Ville.
