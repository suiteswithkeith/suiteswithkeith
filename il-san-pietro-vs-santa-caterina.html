Il San Pietro or Santa Caterina?

Two classics of the Amalfi Coast. Il San Pietro sits on the cliffs just outside Positano; Santa Caterina is a family hotel in Amalfi, open since 1904. Here is how I would choose.

If you want Positano and the sunset

A sea view from every room, a beach club that keeps the sun when the rest of the coast is in shade, the iconic tennis court and a long list of inclusions.

The question behind the question

The classic in Positano, or a family home in Amalfi?

Il San Pietro is the classic, done properly. For me it is one of the two best options in Positano: very private, a sea view from every room, and free shuttles into town when you want the scene.

Santa Caterina is the kind of hotel where you feel like a guest in someone’s home. I stayed there, and it is one of the few hotels this past month that left me with a feeling I can’t quite describe.

You do not have to choose. Many of my clients do both: Santa Caterina for slow days in Amalfi, then a few nights at Il San Pietro in Positano. I can split the stay.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Rooms

At Il San Pietro, every room is impeccably kept and has a sea view. Some face the sunrise, others the sunset, so tell me which you want. The base rooms are fantastic; Signature and Prestige give you more space.

At Santa Caterina, every category I book faces the sea head-on: the Special Suites, with a private pool over the sea, the Full Sea View Garden Suites, the front sea view Junior Suites, and the Front Sea View Doubles on an upper floor. I stayed in room 45, in a little building just to the left of the main hotel. So private and serene, truly a dream.

Beach clubs and the afternoon sun

Il San Pietro’s beach club is on the sunset side, which is a huge selling point. In the fall, the best time to visit, swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm at most hotels on the coast. Not here.

Santa Caterina is one of the hotels where the water gets dark fairly early. Getting there is half the fun: walk down through gardens that feel like a fairy tale at least once, and take the elevator another time for the coolest view you have ever seen on the way to the beach.

Service and the days

Santa Caterina is still run by the founding family, and so many of the team have been there 15 to 20 years or more. You dial 260 for the front desk, there is no WhatsApp, and reception keeps the calendar in real books. I spent a full day the way my clients would: coffee in bed, the gym by the sea, a swim, room service, another swim. I never felt bored.

Il San Pietro fills the days for you: a complimentary 2-hour yacht cruise, cooking and pizza classes, a daily cocktail masterclass, Pilates and yoga, the iconic tennis court and Michelin-starred dining.

Honeymoons and families

For a honeymoon, Il San Pietro is #06 in The Fifteen, my honeymoon shortlist, and the privacy is the point.

For a multigenerational trip, Santa Caterina. Families come back for decades, and Casa Marchesa, about 300 meters away, has a three-bedroom villa that keeps everyone together, and a pool just for its guests.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Il San Pietro or Santa Caterina?

It depends on the trip. Pick Il San Pietro for Positano, the sunset beach club and the tennis court. Pick Santa Caterina to slow down in Amalfi with a family that has run the hotel since 1904. Many of my clients do both.

Which has more afternoon sun at the beach club?

Il San Pietro. Its beach club is on the sunset side. At Santa Caterina, as at most hotels on the coast, the water gets dark fairly early, and in the fall swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm.

Which room should I book at each?

At Il San Pietro, tell me sunrise or sunset; the base rooms are fantastic, and Signature or Prestige give you more space. At Santa Caterina, a front sea view: a Special Suite with its own pool for a milestone, or room 45, where I stayed.

Which is better for a honeymoon?

Il San Pietro is on my honeymoon shortlist, The Fifteen. For a milestone trip at Santa Caterina, I book the one-bedroom Special Suite with its own private pool over the sea.

Which is better for a multigenerational trip?

Santa Caterina. Families come back here for generations, and the three-bedroom villa at Casa Marchesa keeps everyone together. Tell me who is traveling and I will plan the rooms around everyone.

Can I do both?

Yes, and I recommend it. Santa Caterina for slow days in Amalfi, then Il San Pietro for a few nights in Positano. Both work beautifully by boat from Naples or Salerno, and I arrange all of it.
