Airelles Palladio or the Danieli?

Both look straight at San Marco from opposite sides of the water. The Danieli puts you on the Riva next to the Doge’s Palace; Airelles Palladio is a garden estate with pools on Giudecca, five minutes across by boat.

The question behind the question

Why I would book Airelles Palladio

I toured both on the same day in October 2026. I liked the Danieli and think it is a great option, but it did not wow me the way Airelles did.

Airelles gives you more: pools, a real spa, gardens and four restaurants, with St. Mark’s five minutes away when you want it. It is the better choice for families and for anyone who wants calm in a very busy city.

Book the Danieli if being at San Marco is the point. The lobby is a showstopper, the rooftop restaurant is spectacular, and a bigger hotel means better odds of an upgrade.

The detail

What to know before you choose

The view, from both sides

The two hotels face each other across the basin. From the Danieli’s roof you look over the Salute and the lagoon; from Airelles you look back at San Marco and the Doge’s Palace.

Either way you get the postcard. The difference is whether the crowds are under your window or across the water.

The lobby versus the gardens

The Danieli’s Gothic atrium is the great see-and-be-seen room in Venice, and worth a drink even if you stay elsewhere.

At Airelles the showpiece is outside: nearly a hectare of walled gardens, lawns and the long outdoor pool.

Rooms and service

The Danieli’s rooms are understated and classic. With a high room count, service is harder to personalize, but upgrades are more likely.

Airelles has 45 keys, so the team knows you, and the rooms are spacious and individually styled.

Families

Airelles, clearly: pools, a kids club, gardens and a private three-bedroom suite with its own pool.

The Danieli has no pool, and the Riva outside is crowded.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Airelles Palladio or the Danieli?

For most travelers, Airelles Palladio. It has pools, a three-story spa, gardens and four restaurants, five minutes by boat from St. Mark’s. Choose the Danieli if you want to be at San Marco, with its famous lobby and rooftop restaurant.

Does the Danieli have a pool?

No. Airelles Palladio has an outdoor pool in its gardens and an indoor pool in its spa.

Is the Danieli part of Four Seasons?

Yes. It reopened as the Danieli, A Four Seasons Hotel, in July 2026.

Is it worth visiting the Danieli if I stay elsewhere?

Yes. Have a drink in the Gothic lobby or book dinner at Terrazza Danieli on the roof for one of the great views in Venice.
