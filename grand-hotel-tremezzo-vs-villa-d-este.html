Head-to-head · Lake Como

Grand Hotel Tremezzo or Villa d’Este?

Lake Como’s two great classic hotels. One is an Art Nouveau landmark in the middle of the lake, looking straight across at Bellagio. The other is a Renaissance estate in Cernobbio and the lake’s original grand hotel. The choice is mostly about where on the lake you want to be.

Toured both · October 2026

Choose Grand Hotel Tremezzo

If you want the middle of the lake

An Art Nouveau hotel from 1910 in Tremezzo, facing Bellagio across the water. It’s livelier, works well for families, and puts the villas, Bellagio and Varenna a short boat ride away. The same family owns Passalacqua.

Choose Villa d’Este

If you want the institution

A 16th-century estate in Cernobbio with a 25-acre park, a pool floating on the lake and century-old clay courts. It’s formal in the best way, and the closest of the grand hotels to Como and Milan.

The question behind the question

The heart of the lake, or its most famous address?

Grand Hotel Tremezzo is the one I’d pick for a first trip to the lake. It sits right in the middle, where Lake Como is at its most beautiful, with Bellagio across the water and the ferries and private boats at the door. It’s lively and colorful, and it’s easy to bring children.

Villa d’Este is the one for travelers who want the history. Everyone from royalty to film stars has swum in that floating pool, and the estate itself, with its park, grottoes and courts, is at a scale no new hotel can rebuild. It’s closer to Milan, and it’s formal, which is part of the appeal.

If you’re torn, ask yourself whether you’ll spend your days exploring the lake or enjoying the hotel. Explorers should be in Tremezzo. People who want the hotel to be the destination should be at Villa d’Este.

The detail

What to know before you choose

Location is the real decision

Lake Como is long, and where you sleep shapes the trip. Tremezzo is in the central section, where the lake’s three branches meet, so Bellagio, Varenna and Villa del Balbianello are all short hops by boat.

Cernobbio is at the southern tip, ten minutes from the town of Como. You’re closer to Milan and to Como’s restaurants and shops, but the famous mid-lake villages are a longer boat ride away.

Arriving at Grand Hotel Tremezzo

The road up the west shore from Como is narrow and winding, and it takes 45 minutes to an hour. I usually split the journey: a car from Milan to the water in Como, then about 30 minutes by private boat to the hotel’s front door. It’s a much better first hour on the lake.

Which is better with children?

Grand Hotel Tremezzo, for most families. It’s livelier, the pools are a big draw, and it’s set up for kids in a way the more formal Villa d’Este isn’t. Villa d’Este suits multigenerational trips where the grandparents set the tone.

Which is more formal?

Villa d’Este, clearly. It’s old-world in every sense, and that’s the point. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is polished but relaxed, with more color and energy around the pools.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Which is better, Grand Hotel Tremezzo or Villa d'Este?

It depends on your trip. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is better for exploring the lake and for families, because it’s mid-lake facing Bellagio. Villa d’Este is better if you want history, formality and a hotel that is the destination, and it’s closer to Milan.

Do both hotels have floating pools?

Yes. Villa d’Este’s pool floating on the lake is the original and the most famous. Grand Hotel Tremezzo also has a pool floating on the lake, alongside its other pools.

Which is closer to Milan?

Villa d’Este, in Cernobbio, is about an hour from Milan. Grand Hotel Tremezzo is further up the lake, about an hour and a half by car, or a car to Como plus about 30 minutes by private boat.

Is Grand Hotel Tremezzo good for families?

Yes. It’s one of the most family-friendly of the lake’s classic hotels, with several pools and a relaxed, lively atmosphere.

Who owns Grand Hotel Tremezzo?

The De Santis family, who also opened Passalacqua in Moltrasio.
