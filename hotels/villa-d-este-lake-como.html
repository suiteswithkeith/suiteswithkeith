Villa d'Este

On my tennis ranking, Villa d'Este earns its place with century-old courts along the water, part of one of Lake Como's grandest estates. On my featured page I put it more simply: clay courts in a 25-acre renaissance park above the lake — old-world tennis at its finest.

The court is a doorway into what Villa d'Este actually is: the lake's original grand hotel, a renaissance estate whose gardens, mosaics, and floating pool have defined Como luxury for generations. Passalacqua may be the celebrated newcomer, but this is the institution it gets measured against.

It's a Fora Reserve property in my partner book, and my pick on the lake for travelers who want history with their luxury — the sense that everyone from royalty to film stars swam in this same pool.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Old-world means old-world. The formality and classic interiors are the point. If your taste runs modern, that's Il Sereno across the lake.

Cernobbio is a smart base. The town side of the lake — easy access to Como city and the villa circuit by boat.

Compare the lake's icons before you book. Villa d'Este, Passalacqua, the Mandarin, Il Sereno, and the EDITION each fit a different couple — my Lake Como proposal shows how I frame the choice.
