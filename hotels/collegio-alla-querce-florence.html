The review

A brighter, more modern take on Florence — quiet and green in the hills above the city, with sweeping views from almost every room.

Collegio alla Querce is a brighter, more modern take on Florence. It sits in the hills about a 30-minute walk from the center — quiet and green, with an impressive art collection and sweeping views from almost every room.

The appeal is light, space and the view: the city laid out below you, and a scenic walk when you want to be in it. The mood is a little more romantic and it draws a slightly younger crowd, while still being wonderful for families.

I’ve slept here, not just toured it, so I can tell you which rooms, views and layouts are worth asking for. It’s my pick for return visitors, couples, and families who want a countryside feel without giving up Florence.
