The first look

Rosewood comes to Greece, and it chose Crete.

Rosewood Blue Palace is Rosewood’s first hotel in Greece: a full reimagining of the Blue Palace on the hillside between the fishing village of Plaka and the port of Elounda, looking straight across the water to Spinalonga. It’s expected to open in spring 2027.

Most of my Greece business is in the Cyclades, and the question I get more every season is “What about Crete?” Crete has the beaches, the food and the history, but until now it hasn’t had a hotel at the level my clients book on Mykonos or Santorini. I think this is the one that changes that.

I’ve been talking with the Rosewood Blue Palace sales team since the fall, and they’ve kept me in the loop on the opening, the room mix and the booking timeline. I’ll be on property in May 2027, soon after it opens, and I’ll update this page with my own take, room by room. Until then, this is everything I can tell you, and how to get ahead of everyone else for 2027.

What to expect

The resort, at a glance

01
Private pools at more than half the keys. 85 of the 154 rooms and suites have their own pool. In Greece that’s the detail that decides the booking, so it’s the first thing I’ll be matching you to.
02
Generous rooms from the start. Entry rooms are about 46 m² (500 sq ft) and suites start around 52.6 m² (570 sq ft), bigger than the entry rooms at most Cyclades boutique hotels.
03
Greek designers, a Cretan sense of place. Architecture by the Athens firm K-Studio and restaurant and bar interiors by Greek-born, London-based Afroditi Krassa. Rosewood describes it as built with local methods and materials: cool stone, hand-woven textiles, natural textures.
04
An Asaya spa and real wellness. Rosewood’s Asaya well-being concept, with a fitness center, movement studio, yoga pavilion and hydrotherapy areas.
05
A private beach, three sea-view pools, and Spinalonga. Three outdoor pools face the Gulf of Elounda, there’s a private beach, and Rosewood says guests will get access to the islet of Spinalonga across the water.
06
Food rooted in the island. Several restaurants and bars, plus experiences built around Cretan cooking: small long-table feasts in family courtyards and village chapels, mountain herbs and lyra music.
Getting there

Easier than you think

By air
About 50 minutes by car from Heraklion airport, which is a 35-minute flight from Athens. The new Kastelli airport will cut that to about an hour once it opens.
By ferry
About 2 hours from Santorini and about 3 hours 40 minutes from Paros, so Crete pairs easily with an island trip. See my Greek island pairings.
Transfers
I’ll arrange a private car from the airport or port so you’re not waiting on a taxi line.
When to go
Late spring and early autumn are the sweet spot on Crete: warm sea, long days, fewer crowds. Peak summer dates will be the first to go in an opening year.

Before you ask

Rosewood Blue Palace FAQ

When does Rosewood Blue Palace open?

Rosewood Blue Palace is expected to open in spring 2027. It will be Rosewood’s first hotel in Greece.

When can I book Rosewood Blue Palace?

Rosewood is launching the hotel’s website and booking engine in November 2026. Join my first-access list and I’ll send you 2027 rates and availability as soon as they’re released, so you can hold the best rooms for peak dates before they go.

Where is Rosewood Blue Palace?

On the hillside between the fishing village of Plaka and the port of Elounda in northeast Crete, on the Gulf of Elounda and looking across to the islet of Spinalonga.

How many rooms does Rosewood Blue Palace have?

154 rooms and suites, 85 of them with private pools. Entry rooms are about 46 m² (500 sq ft) and suites start at about 52.6 m² (570 sq ft).

How do you get to Rosewood Blue Palace?

It’s about 50 minutes by car from Heraklion airport, which is a 35-minute flight from Athens. By ferry, Crete is about 2 hours from Santorini and about 3 hours 40 minutes from Paros. I arrange private transfers from the airport or port.

Is this the old Blue Palace in Elounda?

Yes. It’s a full reimagining of the Blue Palace, with architecture by the Greek firm K-Studio and restaurant and bar interiors by Afroditi Krassa, designed around Rosewood’s A Sense of Place philosophy.

Does Rosewood Blue Palace have a beach?

Yes. The resort has a private beach and three outdoor pools facing the sea, and Rosewood says guests will also get access to the islet of Spinalonga.

How much will Rosewood Blue Palace cost?
