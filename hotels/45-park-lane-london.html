The review

Moody, sexy and small, with Dorchester-quality beds and service and stunning marble bathrooms.

Moody, sexy and small: just 45 rooms and suites, including one penthouse, with Dorchester-quality beds and service. The suites are all velvet sofas, soft textured walls and brass, and the marble bathrooms are stunning, with a separate WC, double vanity, tub and shower, heated floors and Toto toilets.

It’s the kind of hotel where the small things are already handled before you think to ask. There was oat milk and regular milk already in the fridge, so I could have my coffee first thing in the morning without calling down. A small touch, but an incredible one.

And because it’s so small, the personal attention is incredible, almost like Portrait in Florence and Milan. This is a hotel for someone who likes to come back and know the hotel will remember every little thing about them. That’s a great part of this property.

Every room faces Hyde Park. I was there in late November, when the trees are bare, but in spring, summer and fall the view across the park is beautiful.

Know before you book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

01
Booking a Junior Suite? The guaranteed upgrade covers rooms up to the Junior Suite category. Book a Junior Suite and the upgrade is subject to availability at check-in instead; I’ll still ask for it.
02
Park view, guaranteed. Every room faces Hyde Park, and booked through me as a Fora advisor that’s guaranteed. In spring, summer and fall, the view over the park is beautiful.
03
Start with a Park View Studio. The entry category is bigger than a standard room. Two can interconnect (a king and a twin), sleeping up to six with rollaways and no suite required.
04
Book a suite for the butler. Butler service starts at the one-bedroom suites, like the Curzon Suite.
05
Book Sushi Kanazawa early. Thirteen seats, and it’s open to the public. Tell me your dates and I’ll ask for seats with the room.
06
45 Park Lane or The Dorchester? It’s a lot like Grand Hotel Tremezzo or Passalacqua on Lake Como: they’re simply for different travelers. If you want the vibrant scene and to feel like you’re in the middle of London, choose The Dorchester. If you value the discretion, privacy and intimacy of a smaller hotel, choose 45 Park Lane. And you can always walk across the street for tea, or share the spa.
