The review

Mykonos’ newest big-name resort, on a hillside above Kalo Livadi Bay with its own private beach, and a team I send clients to with total confidence.

Four Seasons Hotel Mykonos opened for its first summer in 2026, and it’s already one of the hotels I recommend most on the island. It sits on a hillside above Kalo Livadi Bay on the south coast, built like a Mykonian village across about 15 acres, with its own private beach below and two infinity pools facing the Aegean.

I have a close relationship with the team here and have sent them clients, so I know how they look after people. The service is what you’d expect from Four Seasons, which is still rarer on Mykonos than it should be.

The rooms are the quiet win. Even the entry-level Deluxe Sea-View Rooms are around 500 square feet, large enough for a family of three, which is very unusual on an island where standard rooms tend to be small. For families and groups, there are two villas, a two-bedroom and the three-bedroom Syros Villa, each with its own pool. More on the villas →

What stood out

The details that made the list

01
Spacious Deluxe Sea-View Rooms. The entry-level category is around 500 square feet with sea views, and big enough for a family of three without jumping to a suite.
02
A private beach on Kalo Livadi Bay. Beach days without the beach-club scramble, with the south coast’s energy close by when you want it.
03
Two infinity pools and a kids club. Both pools face the Aegean, and the Four Seasons kids club gives the kids their own program while you enjoy the beach.
04
A spa set among gardens. Four single treatment rooms and three couples’ suites, including a private VIP suite and an open-air pavilion, plus a sauna, a vitality pool, a spa garden, a fitness studio and a yoga pavilion.
05
Villas with private pools. The Two-Bedroom Sea-View Villa (up to 4) and the Syros Three-Bedroom Villa (up to 6), for families and groups who want a private house with Four Seasons service behind it.

The Fine Print, From Someone Who Books It

01
It’s about 20 minutes from Mykonos Town. That’s the trade for a private beach and a quieter setting. I arrange drivers for dinners in Chora.
02
Families, start here. Few Mykonos hotels fit a family this well, from the room sizes and the kids club to the villas. Tell me the kids’ ages and I’ll match you to the right room or villa.
03
Comparing the south coast? Fouquet’s is the more energetic choice near Paraga and Scorpios, and Kalesma sits above Ornos Bay, close to everything.
04
Transfers and private boats, arranged. I arrange private transfers from the airport or port, private boat days along the south coast or to Delos, and private boat transfers to Paros or Naxos instead of the ferry.
