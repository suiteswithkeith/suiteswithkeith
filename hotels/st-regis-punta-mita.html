The St. Regis Punta Mita

Quieter and more residential in feel, with butler service throughout — a refined base on the same beautiful peninsula.

Butler Service Throughout (St. Regis)  ·  Punta Mita Riviera Nayarit Peninsula

Inquire to book

Punta Mita is the gated peninsula north of Puerto Vallarta where Mexico's Pacific coast goes quiet and polished, and the St. Regis is its most residential address — low-slung casitas through tropical gardens, three pools stepping toward the sand, and the brand's butler service running through the whole stay.

The mood here is unhurried on purpose. Where the coast's flashier resorts perform, the St. Regis settles you in: breakfast overlooking the break, an afternoon between pool and spa, tequila cart at sunset. The Jack Nicklaus golf across the peninsula and the surf breaks off the point are both minutes away.

I book it for couples and families who want Punta Mita's calm without the adults-only format — and for St. Regis and Marriott loyalists, this is one of the best places in the Americas to spend that status.

What Stood Out

The Details That Made The List

Butler service, resort-wide. The St. Regis signature translated to the beach — unpacked, pressed, and the day arranged before breakfast.

The residential calm. Casitas in gardens rather than towers on a strip — the quietest luxury on this stretch of the Pacific.

The peninsula itself. Gated, green and genuinely beautiful — with golf, surf and Sayulita's color a short drive away.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Adults-only isn't the format. Families are welcome and well served; couples who want a fully adult resort should look at Naviva next door.

The beach is Pacific. Beautiful, swimmable in the coves, but this is ocean, not Caribbean bathwater — the pools carry the calm-water duty.
