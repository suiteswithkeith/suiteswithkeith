Las Ventanas al Paraíso

A Rosewood resort famous for over-the-top service theater — for celebrations that deserve a little spectacle.

ShowFamous Over-The-Top Service Theater

Las Ventanas al Paraíso occupies a specific seat on my Los Cabos shortlist: it's the celebration hotel. A Rosewood resort famous for over-the-top service theater, it's my pick for occasions that deserve a little spectacle — proposals, milestone anniversaries, the trips that need a crescendo.

Service theater is a real category, and Las Ventanas practically invented Cabo's version of it. Where Montage is understated and Chileno Bay is residential, this property leans all the way in — the staging, the surprises, the sense that the resort itself is celebrating with you.

As a Rosewood preferred partner, I book it with the amenity package attached — and, more importantly, with the property's events machinery pointed at whatever you're celebrating.
