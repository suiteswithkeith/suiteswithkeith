The Connaught

Quieter and clubbier than its sibling Claridge's — home to the Connaught Bar, Hélène Darroze's dining room, and the most discreet service in the city.

ConnaughtBar — World's-Best Pedigree
3Michelin Stars At Hélène Darroze
Inquire To Book

If Claridge's is London's stage, The Connaught is its private club. Tucked onto Carlos Place in the quietest corner of Mayfair, it trades spectacle for discretion — the hotel where the service anticipates rather than performs.

The Connaught Bar has spent years trading the 'World's Best Bar' title with a handful of rivals, and its tableside martini trolley is a London ritual. Hélène Darroze's dining room holds three Michelin stars, the Aman Spa is one of only two in Europe, and the famous mahogany staircase has been polished by a century of the world's most private guests.

I book it for repeat London visitors, for anyone who values being left alone beautifully, and for couples who'd rather have the best bar in the world downstairs than a scene in the lobby.

What Stood Out

The Details That Made The List

The Connaught Bar. The martini trolley, mixed to your profile at the table — a world's-best bar that actually deserves the title.

Three stars in-house. Hélène Darroze's dining room means one of London's great meals is an elevator ride away.

Genuine discretion. The staff's superpower is knowing when not to be seen — the most private feel of the great London hotels.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Understated is the brief. If you want grandeur and buzz, Claridge's delivers it three streets away — the Connaught whispers on purpose.

The bar fills by early evening. House guests get priority, which is exactly why staying here matters — but still time your night around it.

Mount Street is your high street. The best small stretch of shopping and galleries in London starts at the front door.

The connoisseur's London hotel — quieter fame, deeper comfort, and the best bar on earth one floor down.
