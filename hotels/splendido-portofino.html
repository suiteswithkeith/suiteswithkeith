The Splendido earned its spot on my tennis ranking with a court terraced above Portofino's harbor, the Ligurian coastline stretching out beyond the net. Every hotel on that list has a view; very few have Portofino itself as the view.

The hotel is a former monastery folded into the hillside above one of Italy's most photographed harbors — Belmond's Riviera showpiece, and a Fora Reserve property in my partner book. Terraced gardens, the famous pool, and the walk (or shuttle) down to the piazzetta define the days here.

As a Belmond preferred partner, I book it on its own or as the coastal leg of a Belmond Italy run — Venice's Cipriani, Ravello's Caruso, and the Splendido make an itinerary I never get tired of building.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Splendido versus Splendido Mare. The hillside hotel and its little sister on the harborfront are different products — I'll place you based on how you want to live for the week.

Portofino is tiny and seasonal. The village is best May through September, and the hotel's calendar follows — shoulder weeks are the value play.

Boats are the point here. A chartered day along the Ligurian coast — Camogli, San Fruttuoso, the Cinque Terre — is the experience I add to nearly every Splendido booking.

Portofino's harbor beyond the net and Belmond service behind you — the Italian Riviera at its most cinematic.
