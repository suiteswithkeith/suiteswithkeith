The review

Very historic and beautifully kept, with a stunning modern spa and the best place on the lake to actually swim — in the middle of very busy Bellagio.

I stayed at Villa Serbelloni in October 2026, and it’s an interesting hotel. I’m still making up my mind about it. It’s very, very historic, and the rooms are beautifully maintained, but the decor is a bit too old for my taste.

What I loved: the spa, which is stunning and surprisingly modern for a hotel this historic; the little speakeasy cocktail bar; and the pool, beach and floating pier you can jump into the lake from. In summer, it would be the best hotel on the lake for actually swimming. Service is formal, but warm, and I never felt unwelcome.

The bigger question is Bellagio. It’s the lake’s most famous village, and it’s incredibly crowded. I was there in October and couldn’t imagine July and August. If you love old Italian hotels and art and Bellagio is the point of your trip, it’s a beautiful choice. Otherwise, I usually steer clients to Villa d’Este.

Know before you book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

01
Bellagio is crowded. Packed with day-trippers, even in October; July and August would be a nightmare. You can visit Bellagio easily by boat from almost anywhere on the lake, so think hard about whether you want to sleep there.
02
The decor is very traditional. Frescoes, chandeliers, damask and antiques, all beautifully kept. If you love that, you’ll love it here. If you prefer something fresher, look elsewhere.
03
A big hotel with the village at the door. More than 90 rooms and three places to eat on property, plus all of Bellagio’s restaurants outside the front door.
04
Arrive by boat. A car to Como, then a boat. The lake road from the town of Como takes 45 minutes to an hour and it’s narrow and winding. I arrange a private car from Milan to the water in Como, then about 30 minutes by private boat to the hotel’s dock. It’s the best way to arrive, especially on a honeymoon.
