The review

The warmest shared spaces in Rome, in a hotel in a real Roman neighborhood, where I felt like I could be myself.

For me, La Minerva’s shared spaces were the highlight. The restaurant upstairs, the bar downstairs, the lobby, and above all the service and warmth of the people who work there. I felt like I could be myself here, and I actually shared a great laugh with the woman who checked me in.

The views around the hotel are more authentic Rome than beautiful, pristine Rome. I think that’s part of its charm: it’s a hotel in a real part of the city, by the Pantheon, and I really appreciated that compared with a lot of its competitors.

If you’re younger, it’s a great hotel. If you’re older or want more polish and calm, I’d gravitate toward the Hotel Eden.
