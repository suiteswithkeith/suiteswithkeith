Mauna Kea Beach Hotel

Hawaii's original tennis park — a dozen courts set along the Kohala Coast.

#11In My Hotel Tennis Ranking
12Courts Along The Kohala Coast
Inquire To Book

Mauna Kea Beach Hotel closes out my ranking of the world's most beautiful hotel tennis courts at #11 — and it's the volume pick on a list full of single showpiece courts. This is Hawaii's original tennis park: a dozen courts set along the Kohala Coast, at a Fora Reserve property with real history behind it.

Where most entries on my list are one perfect court you share with a handful of guests, Mauna Kea is a program — the place for families and groups who want tennis to be part of every day, not a single photogenic hit.

It also pairs naturally with the two Four Seasons properties on my honeymoon list, Hualalai and Lanai, for clients building a longer Hawaii itinerary across islands.
