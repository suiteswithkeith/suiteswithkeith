The review

Away from the crowds, five minutes from everything.

Airelles Palladio is set away from the crowds of Venice on Giudecca, and it is an easy five-minute hop across to everything the city has to offer. It is incredibly private and secure, and at the same time lively, social and fun. Sophisticated, but never stuffy.

It is not on the Grand Canal, and I would argue that is a selling point. You get real peace and quiet in a very touristy city. My one fear was that it would feel isolated. It does not, at all. In October it was lively, but never loud.

The arrival is spectacular, and the attention to detail in the finishes, the layout of the gardens and the rooms is truly serene. The rooms are incredibly spacious.

What stood out

The details that made it my number one

01
The arrival. You come in by water to a private landing, and the reveal of the gardens is one of the great hotel arrivals in Europe.
02
A three-story spa. Spa Airelles by Guerlain is remarkable: three floors, with an indoor pool.
03
The outdoor pool and gardens. A stunning pool set in walled gardens, with lawns and pergolas. Space that physically does not exist across the water in San Marco.
04
Four restaurants and four bars. Plus a beautiful library. You could stay a week without repeating an evening.
05
History you can feel. In the penthouse, one room has a slight slope where the original structure could not be changed. Some might call that unfinished. I loved it; it shows how historic the building really is.
Rooms & suites

Which room to ask for

01
The Suite del Doge. The penthouse in Villa Frollo: 450 square meters, about 4,800 square feet, with four bedrooms. Villa Frollo can be privatized to 13 bedrooms. See the Suite del Doge →
02
The three-bedroom suite with a private pool. Not a standalone villa; it connects to one of the larger historic buildings, but it is entirely private, with its own pool. The one I would book for a family, at approximately $15,000 to $25,000 per night.
03
Ask for the piano nobile. In any palazzo, I always want a room on the noble floor, one level above the ground floor, where the ceilings are much higher.
Know before you book

The fine print, from your advisor

01
You are on Giudecca, deliberately. The hotel boat makes the crossing in about five minutes. If you want to step out your door into the streets of Venice, look at Aman instead.
02
Stay four nights. Three is my sweet spot for Venice; at Airelles I could easily do four.
03
Book early for July and Biennale years. Redentore weekend in July puts the fireworks right over the Giudecca Canal, and Biennale years fill the city.
04
Transfers, arranged. I arrange the private water taxi from Marco Polo airport to the hotel dock, and private boats to Murano, Burano and Torcello.
Before you ask

The questions I get

Where is Airelles Palladio in Venice?

On Giudecca island, facing San Marco across the Giudecca Canal. The hotel boat reaches St. Mark’s in about five minutes.

Does Airelles Palladio have a pool?

Yes. An outdoor pool in the gardens and an indoor pool in the three-story Guerlain spa.

Is Airelles Palladio good for families?

It is the best family hotel in Venice: pools, a kids club, gardens and a private three-bedroom suite with its own pool.

How many nights should I stay?

I could easily do four nights at Airelles. Three is my sweet spot for Venice elsewhere.
