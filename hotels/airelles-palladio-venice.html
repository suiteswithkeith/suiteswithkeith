The review

Away from the crowds, five minutes from everything.

Airelles Palladio is set away from the crowds of Venice on Giudecca, and it is an easy five-minute hop across to everything the city has to offer. It is incredibly private and secure, and at the same time lively, social and fun. Sophisticated, but never stuffy.

It is not on the Grand Canal, and I would argue that is a selling point. You get real peace and quiet in a very touristy city. My one fear was that it would feel isolated. It does not, at all. In October it was lively, but never loud.

The arrival is spectacular, and the attention to detail in the finishes, the layout of the gardens and the rooms is truly serene. The rooms are incredibly spacious.

The estate

How the estate fits together

Airelles is not one building. It is a small walled estate at the eastern end of Giudecca: three historic buildings, a three-level spa and about a hectare of gardens, all behind the waterfront facing San Marco.

01
Palladio. 23 rooms and suites with views over Venice, beside Palladio’s Le Zitelle church. The lobby, abc kitchens and the Palladio Bar are here.
02
Villa Frollo. 10 rooms, crowned by the four-bedroom Suite del Doge, which looks straight at St. Mark’s. Norbert Niederkofler’s restaurant and the Botanica Frollo Bar are here too.
03
Conventino. 12 rooms at the quiet back of the estate, closest to the pools and the gardens.
04
Spa Airelles by Guerlain. 1,700 square meters over three levels: treatment rooms, a hammam, a sauna, the Yashi Dome and the Domus indoor pool with its mezzanine, plus a fitness area and yoga studio.
05
The 25-meter pool. Hidden in the gardens, with the Pool Bar alongside for pizza and salads all day.
06
The kids’ pool and Kids Club. An 8-meter pool just for children, and a Kids Club with games and creative workshops.
07
Villa Mason. The three-bedroom suite with its own pool and private garden, part of Conventino.
08
The gardens. About a hectare of walled lawns and pergola walks between the buildings.
09
The hotel boats. Private shuttles leave from the hotel’s own landing on the Fondamenta for St. Mark’s.
Dining

Four chefs, five bars

That is a lot of firepower for 45 rooms, and it is a big part of why the estate never feels isolated. You can eat somewhere different every night without crossing the water.

01
Norbert Niederkofler. The three-Michelin-star chef from the Dolomites brings his “Cook the Lagoon” menu to Villa Frollo: Venetian specialties built around the lagoon and its producers.
02
Matsuhisa. The first in Venice. Traditional Japanese cooking in the Nobu style.
03
abc kitchens by Jean-Georges. His first address in Italy, in the Palladio building, with a Venetian and Mediterranean menu.
04
Cédric Grolet. The pastry chef’s first venture in Italy. His trompe-l’oeil desserts and viennoiserie run through every restaurant in the hotel.
05
The bars. The Palladio Bar for cocktails and wine. Elton’s Club after dinner, with live music. The Botanica Frollo Bar for rare spirits. Zitelle Bacaro on the Fondamenta for cicchetti and coffee facing St. Mark’s. And the Pool Bar.
What’s included

The inclusions are part of the magic

One of my favorite things about Airelles is how much is simply included in your stay. You stop signing for things, and the whole experience feels generous from the moment you arrive.

01
A fully included minibar. And they do not skimp: full bottles of wine, bottled cocktails, sweet and savory snacks, and a glass jar of candy, restocked daily and again at turndown. Nespresso and a selection of teas are in the room too.
02
Nightly gifts at turndown. A little surprise waits for you every evening when you come back to the room.
03
Daily breakfast. A full gourmet breakfast, overlooking the gardens or inside the historic rooms.
04
Poolside refreshments. Hot drinks and a selection of non-alcoholic drinks served around the pools throughout the day.
05
Laundry and luggage valet. Standard laundry for up to ten pieces per room, per day, and the staff will pack and unpack your suitcases for you.
06
The hotel boat. Frequent, on-demand shuttles across to St. Mark’s in about three to five minutes.
07
The spa, pools, gardens and kids club. Daily access to Spa Airelles by Guerlain, the indoor and outdoor pools and the gardens, plus a complimentary kids club for the little ones.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Where is Airelles Palladio in Venice?

On Giudecca island, facing San Marco across the Giudecca Canal. The hotel boat reaches St. Mark’s in about five minutes.

How do I get to Airelles Palladio from the airport?

About 40 minutes by water taxi from Marco Polo Airport, straight to the hotel’s own landing. Book through me and stays of two nights or more include the one-way water taxi.

Does Airelles Palladio have a pool?

Yes, three: a 25-meter outdoor pool in the gardens, an 8-meter kids’ pool, and the indoor Domus pool in the three-level Guerlain spa.

Is Airelles Palladio good for families?

It is the best family hotel in Venice: pools, a kids club, gardens and a private three-bedroom suite with its own pool.

What is included at Airelles Palladio?

More than almost anywhere I know: the full minibar (wine, bottled cocktails, snacks and candy, restocked daily), nightly turndown gifts, daily breakfast, poolside drinks, laundry for up to ten pieces a day, luggage packing and unpacking, the hotel boat to San Marco, and access to the spa, pools and kids club.

Can I pair Airelles Palladio with the Dolomites?

Yes, and it’s one of my favorite Italy combinations. Airelles pairs naturally with Forestis, Gardena Grödnerhof, COMO Alpina or Aman Rosa Alpina. I arrange the private car, about two and a half to three hours, or a helicopter.

How many nights should I stay?

I could easily do four nights at Airelles. Three is my sweet spot for Venice elsewhere.
