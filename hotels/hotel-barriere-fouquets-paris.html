The review

The Champs-Élysées address with a private-club feel — younger in energy, home to one of the city's best spas, and the best-priced palace on my list.

On my featured page, Fouquet's is the Paris palace with a different personality: the Champs-Élysées address with a private-club feel — younger in energy, and home to one of the city's best spas. In my Paris proposal it carries another distinction: exceptional added value — the best-priced palace of the five I compare.

Glamorous, contemporary, and superbly located just off the Champs-Élysées, Fouquet's brings real French flair — and, through my partner program, some of the best added value of any palace in the city. The base Superior runs a genuinely generous 37 square meters with a Paris view.

My take from the proposal, verbatim: even the base room punches well above its price here, and the rate is a genuine value versus standard pricing. Take a close look at the perks — they're excellent. Happy to quote higher categories, too.
