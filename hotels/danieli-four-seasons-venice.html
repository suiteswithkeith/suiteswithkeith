The review

The lobby and the rooftop are the stars.

For me, the stars of the Danieli are the lobby and the rooftop restaurant. The Gothic atrium really is a showstopper, and it is a great place to see and be seen. Terrazza Danieli on the roof looks over the Salute, the Doge’s Palace and the lagoon.

The rooms are understated and very classic in design. The location can be a plus or a minus: you are steps from St. Mark’s, and it is busy right in front of your door.

It is a bigger hotel, which makes personalized service harder, but it also means a higher likelihood of an upgrade. I liked the Danieli and think it is a great option. It just did not quite wow me the way Aman and Airelles did.

Know before you book

The fine print, from your advisor

It is busy outside. The Riva is the busiest waterfront in Venice. For calm, look at Airelles Palladio or Aman.
02
Book a lagoon view. The rooms are classic; the view is what makes them.
03
Service at scale. Less personal than a small palazzo, which is where my relationships with the team help.

No pool, and the spa is still coming. The spa is due to open in late 2026.
