The review

A perfectly restored Chianti borgo outside Siena — the most well-rounded of the Tuscan borghi, and one of my favorite stays so far.

Borgo San Felice is a medieval hamlet in the Chianti hills just outside Siena, perfectly restored and run as one hotel. Stone houses, a little chapel and a piazza, all surrounded by the estate’s vineyards — quaint in the best sense of the word.

Of all the borghi I’ve stayed at and visited in Tuscany, it’s the most well-rounded. The service is fantastic, the food is fantastic, and the village has been restored so carefully that it never feels like a set. More than anything, it just feels like home.

It also has everything you’d want for a longer stay: tennis, a gym and a spa on property, with Siena close enough for an afternoon in town and dinner back at the borgo.

I’ve slept here, not just toured it, and it’s one of my favorite stays so far. It’s a truly perfect gem, part of Fora Reserve, and my pick for anyone who wants Tuscany done well from start to finish.
