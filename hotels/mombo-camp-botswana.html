Mombo Camp

Botswana's most storied camp, on an island in the Delta's richest game country — water and land safari in one, at the top of the category.

OkavangoChief's Island, Moremi
No. 1Botswana's Most Storied Camp
Inquire To Book

In the safari world, Mombo is spoken of the way sommeliers speak of a legendary vintage. Wilderness's flagship sits on Chief's Island in the Moremi Reserve, the heart of the Okavango Delta, surrounded by what is widely considered the richest concentration of game in Botswana.

The camp raises tented luxury to its ceiling — vast suites on raised walkways, plunge pools looking over the floodplain, and a staff that runs one of the smoothest operations in Africa. Because the Delta floods seasonally, the safari here is double-sided: classic game drives and water-based exploring, in one stay.

Botswana's model is low-volume, high-value tourism — fewer vehicles, emptier wilderness, higher rates — and Mombo is that philosophy at its peak. This is the connoisseur's safari, and the one wildlife film crews keep coming back to.

The Details That Made The List

True wilderness, few vehicles. Botswana's low-volume model means sightings without the crowd of vehicles you'll meet elsewhere.
