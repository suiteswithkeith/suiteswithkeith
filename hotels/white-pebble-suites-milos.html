The review

Seaside elegance above Pollonia — contemporary Cycladic design in one of Milos' most charming villages.

White Pebble Suites pairs contemporary Cycladic design with one of Milos' best locations. Perched above the waterfront village of Pollonia, it offers understated luxury, personalized service, and easy walking access to the island's best restaurants, cafés, and seaside promenade.

It's a polished boutique experience in one of Milos' most charming villages. If you're looking for contemporary luxury with easy access to Pollonia's restaurants and waterfront, it's an excellent base for exploring the island.

Pollonia itself becomes part of the stay: walk into the village every evening, settle into the slower rhythm of the waterfront, and use the harbor as your launch point for the rest of the island.
