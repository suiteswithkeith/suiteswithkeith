The review

The legendary Eden-Roc pool, blasted into the cliffs above the Mediterranean — the only hotel on both my pools and tennis lists in France.

The Eden-Roc is the only French hotel that appears on two of my worldwide rankings. The famous pool — blasted into the cliffs above the Mediterranean — closes out my Top Hotel Pools list, and the clay courts tucked into the pines, just steps from the sea below, earned a spot on my hotel tennis ranking as well.

That double placement tells you what this property is: not a one-trick icon, but a complete Riviera estate where the legend actually holds up. The saltwater pool carved from the rock has been photographed for nearly a century, and it still stops people mid-sentence when they walk down to it.

It's a Fora Reserve property in my book of partners, which is exactly the level of relationship you want behind a booking like this — because at the Eden-Roc, how you book matters almost as much as when.
