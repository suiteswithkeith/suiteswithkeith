The review

The best breakfast in Greece, from the hotel group with the best service on Mykonos.

In my Greece Collection edit, Bill & Coo took two categories: Best Breakfast went to Bill & Coo Suites, and Best Service on Mykonos went to the Bill & Coo family as a whole. That's not an accident — this is the group that consistently out-executes the island.

Bill & Coo Suites is the property to consider if being closer to Mykonos Town or traveling with family is a priority. Its sister property, Bill & Coo Coast, is my choice for a true beachfront stay — unlike most of Mykonos' luxury hotels perched high above the sea, the Coast brings you directly to the shoreline.

Between the two, you're choosing a location, not a level of quality: exceptional dining and relaxed, genuinely warm service run through both.
