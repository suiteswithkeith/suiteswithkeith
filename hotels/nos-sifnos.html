NOS

NOS is one of Sifnos' most thoughtfully designed boutique hotels. Set above the fishing village of Faros, it pairs minimalist Cycladic architecture, sweeping Aegean views, and an atmosphere that feels effortlessly calm.

NOS isn't trying to impress with excess. Everything feels intentional, from the architecture to the pace of the experience. If you're looking for a hotel that feels deeply connected to Sifnos rather than separate from it, this is one of my favorite stays in the Cyclades.

Sifnos is my pick for the most underrated island in Greece — the island where Greece slows down — and NOS matches that rhythm exactly.

The Details That Made The List

Wake up for sunrise. The east-facing views make early mornings one of the most memorable parts of staying here.

The Faros Superior is the room I'd book. Sun-washed interiors, generous indoor-outdoor living, and the effortless simplicity that defines NOS.

An afternoon of doing nothing. NOS is one of the few hotels where lingering by your suite feels just as rewarding as exploring the island.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Someone Who's Been

This is quiet luxury, deliberately. Adults, design lovers, and wellness travelers will love it; anyone chasing scene and nightlife should look at Mykonos instead.

Walk into Faros for dinner. The fishing village feels refreshingly untouched, with some of Sifnos' best waterfront tavernas.

Sifnos asks you to linger. Some islands ask you to see more — this one doesn't. Build your itinerary accordingly, and my Sifnos guide shows how.
