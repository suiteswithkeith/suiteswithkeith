The review

A single court cut into the rainforest, ringed by white sand and turquoise water — remote luxury at its most literal.

Bawah Reserve holds #9 on my ranking of the world's most beautiful hotel tennis courts, and it might be the most improbable entry on the list: a single court cut into the rainforest of a private archipelago in Indonesia's Anambas Islands, ringed by white sand and turquoise water.

It's a Fora Reserve property, and it belongs to a category I love booking — places where the journey itself filters the crowd. Like Gundari on Folegandros, the guests who make it to Bawah really want to be there, and the atmosphere shows it.

This is the pick for couples who've done the Maldives and want something wilder — the same private-island seclusion with an eco-lodge soul instead of glossy overwater polish.
