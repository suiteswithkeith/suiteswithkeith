Minos Palace

Adults only, on its own private peninsula — an island within an island, with the sea on three sides of you.

360°Sea Views From A Private Peninsula
From ~$400Per Night In September (Approx. — Ask Me For Exact Dates)
Inquire To Book

Minos Palace occupies a piece of geography no new hotel will ever get again: its own small peninsula on Mirabello Bay, between Agios Nikolaos town and Elounda, with water on three sides and a private sandy beach tucked into the coves. The hotel calls it an island within an island, and for once the marketing is just a description.

It's the adults-only sister of Minos Beach Art Hotel — both run by the founding Mamidakis family's bluegr group — and all 148 rooms and suites face the sea. The top categories add private pools, including the Infinity Lagoon suites whose pools flow into a shared lagoon.

The dining was overhauled in 2024 — Amar on the beachfront, MOM for Cretan longevity cooking — and the new Nao Longevity Hub gives the wellness program a genuine health-science angle, drawing on the island's famously long-lived diet. It's also the best value of my three Crete picks, usually a meaningful step below the cliff-edge newcomers in price.

What Stands Out

The Details That Made The List

The peninsula. Water on three sides means a swim before breakfast is a ten-step commute, and every room faces the sea because there's nowhere else to face.

Adults only, properly. No children on property — the quiet here is structural, not seasonal.

The 2024 dining reset. Amar on the beachfront and MOM's Cretan longevity menus, plus the NAMA pool bar and MOSA beach bar — a real step up from resort-standard.

The Nao Longevity Hub. Science-led longevity programming built on Cretan tradition, alongside the Ananea Wellness spa — a different angle from every other wellness resort in Greece.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print

It's a big house. 148 rooms is resort-scale — if you want a 20-room boutique, look at Acro Suites up the coast.

The bones are 1970s. Renovated throughout, but this is an established property on legacy architecture, not a new-build — that's also why the location is unrepeatable.

Book a sea-facing upgrade with intent. All rooms have sea views, but the sunset side and the private-pool suites are the categories worth the difference.

Pair it. Ten minutes from Agios Nikolaos and just down the coast from Elounda — it works beautifully as the quiet half of a two-stop Crete route.

Quiet, surrounded by water, and the best value on my Crete shortlist — the definition of an easy recommendation.
