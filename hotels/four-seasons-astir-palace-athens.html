Four Seasons Astir Palace

Where Athens meets the sea — a private peninsula that feels like two vacations in one.

30 minFrom The Acropolis
From $1,300Per Night, Booked Through Me
Inquire To Book

Four Seasons Astir Palace proves Athens isn't just a city destination. Set along a private peninsula overlooking the Saronic Gulf, it combines world-class dining, multiple beaches, and resort-style luxury just thirty minutes from the Acropolis.

It's the perfect balance of city access and a true seaside escape — spend your mornings by the sea, your afternoons exploring Athens, and return each evening to one of Europe's finest urban resorts.

It's the hotel I build day two of my Athens itinerary around: a day along the Riviera, a long seaside lunch, a stroll through Astir Marina, and dinner overlooking the Saronic Gulf. On my sample honeymoon itinerary, this is where my clients land first.

What Stood Out

The Details That Made The List

The private coves deserve more than a quick swim. Astir's beach club is one of the Riviera's biggest advantages — spend a full afternoon there.

Pelagos is worth planning your evening around. One of the best Michelin-starred meals in Greece, right on property. Manko is the other table I send clients to on the peninsula.

The Sea View Bungalow Suite is the room I'd book. Privacy, generous outdoor space, and direct access to the Riviera lifestyle.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Someone Who's Been

It's a resort first, a city base second. If your trip is museums and rooftops, stay at The Dolli instead — or better, split the stay and do both.

Explore Vouliagmeni. Walk to the lake or enjoy dinner in town before returning to the calm of the peninsula.

Few hotels balance city and beach this effortlessly. That makes it ideal for families and longer stays where one member of the party wants beach days and another wants the Acropolis.

Astir Palace feels like two vacations in one — few hotels balance city and beach this effortlessly.
