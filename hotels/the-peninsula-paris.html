The review

Impeccably rebuilt near the Arc de Triomphe — rooftop dining with Eiffel views and the sharpest service technology of any palace.

My featured-page line on the Peninsula: impeccably rebuilt near the Arc de Triomphe — rooftop dining with Eiffel views and the sharpest service technology of any palace. Forbes Five-Star, and in my Paris proposal, a perennial favorite.

The proposal spells out why it keeps winning for my clients: a meticulously restored palace known for flawless service, a rooftop restaurant with Eiffel Tower views, and some of the most generous perks in the city. The rooms' in-room technology is genuinely the best executed of any Paris palace.

And the perks are the closer. The preferred-partner program here is one of the strongest around — completely flexible check-in and check-out and a 4th night free. Flexible arrival and departure alone can effectively add a day to a Paris trip. The Junior Suite is a generous, comfortable entry point.
