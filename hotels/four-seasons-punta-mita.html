Four Seasons Resort Punta Mita is the family standby on my Mexico shortlist — two beaches, two golf courses, and the kind of consistency that makes multigenerational trips easy. On a coast full of statement resorts, this is the one engineered for everyone at once.

The Punta Mita peninsula delivers the Riviera Nayarit formula — lush, quiet polish instead of Cabo's drama — and the Four Seasons here has had decades to perfect the family version of it: surf mornings on gentle breaks, golf for the players, and beaches calm enough for every age.

It's also one of the properties where my Four Seasons preferred partnership matters most in practice: multigenerational trips involve many rooms, many requests, and many moving parts, and having the brand relationship behind the file changes how those get handled.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

The standby, not the statement. Design-first couples will be more excited by Mandarina up the coast — this property's genius is dependability.

Same peninsula, quieter neighbor. The St. Regis Punta Mita is the more residential, butler-serviced base nearby — ask me which fits your group.

Whale season applies here too. December through April, humpbacks calve in these waters — a private boat beats the crowds every time.

The resort that makes multigenerational trips easy — in my practice, that's among the highest compliments there is.
