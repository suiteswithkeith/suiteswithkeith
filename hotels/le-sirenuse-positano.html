The review

Really cool, and right in the middle of it.

Le Sirenuse is a really cool, lively property in the middle of Positano. That is either a selling point or a sore point, depending on who you are and what you are looking for.

Service is very formal, and you come to Le Sirenuse to see and be seen. The artwork around the property is stunning, and the lookout over the church dome is truly stunning. And the breakfast is incredible. Out of this world.

I would only stay in a true sea-view room or suite here. The rest either have courtyard views, or the pool view is right on top of the pool, and it is loud.

There is a new beach club, with complimentary boats there at 10:30 and 12:30. The hotel also runs a sunset cruise, but it needs to be booked in advance. I learned this the hard way.

If you want privacy, go for Il San Pietro or Villa Treville. If you want to be able to walk into town, which is busy, pick Le Sirenuse. It is very easy to get into town from the other two, so being outside it is not really a minus. It is more of a plus: you get more privacy and quiet.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Is Le Sirenuse in the center of Positano?

Yes. It is right in the middle of town, so you can walk everywhere. That is a selling point or a sore point depending on what you are looking for: Positano is busy, and Le Sirenuse is lively.

Which room should I book at Le Sirenuse?

Only a true sea-view room or suite. The other rooms either look onto the courtyard, or have pool views right on top of the pool, which gets loud.

Does Le Sirenuse have a beach club?

Yes, a new one. Complimentary boats take you there at 10:30 and 12:30. The hotel also runs a sunset cruise, but it needs to be booked in advance. I learned that the hard way, so I book it for you.

Le Sirenuse, Il San Pietro or Villa Treville?

If you want privacy and quiet, go for Il San Pietro or Villa Treville. If you want to walk into town, pick Le Sirenuse. Getting into Positano from the other two is very easy, so their distance from town is not really a minus. It is more of a plus: you get the privacy and quiet.

What is the service like at Le Sirenuse?

Very formal. You come to Le Sirenuse to see and be seen.

How do I get to Le Sirenuse?

I arrange the private transfer from Naples or Salerno. The nearest helipad is in Scala, close to Ravello, if you want to arrive by helicopter. Reach out and I will handle it.
