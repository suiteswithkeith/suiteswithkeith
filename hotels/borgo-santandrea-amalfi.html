The review

Spectacular, almost awe-inspiring.

Borgo Santandrea sits above Conca dei Marini, just along the coast from Amalfi. It is very modern, in the rooms and in the facilities, and that makes it feel different from every other hotel on the Amalfi Coast. I liked that.

The beach is the star: beautiful, massive and a proper beach. Dining at Alici is fantastic, and the service is refined but warm.

Two things to know. The sun leaves the water early, so in the fall your swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm. And the private pool rooms are not that private. Come in May or June, or in September, and let me pick the room.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Where is Borgo Santandrea?

Above Conca dei Marini, just along the coast from Amalfi. A boat transfer from Naples or Salerno is practical and enjoyable, and the nearest helipad is in Scala, close to Ravello.

Does Borgo Santandrea have a beach?

Yes, and it is the best part: beautiful, massive and a proper beach. The sun leaves the water early, though, so in the fall your swim time ends around 4:30 or 5pm.

Should I book a room with a private pool?

Think twice. The private pool rooms are not that private: you can look down on your neighbors, and they can look down on you. Not everyone will mind, but it is important to know.

When is the best time to go?

May to June, or September.

Santa Caterina or Borgo Santandrea?

Borgo Santandrea is spectacular and brand new in feel. Santa Caterina is the opposite: it has been here for over a century and it knows exactly what it is doing. If you want warmth and history over a statement, choose Santa Caterina.

Is Borgo Santandrea good for a honeymoon?

Yes. It is one of my two honeymoon picks on my World’s 50 Best (51–100) list, alongside Il San Pietro.
