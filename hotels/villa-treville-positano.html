The review

So unique, and so private.

Villa Treville has probably the most private rooms in all of Positano. It feels like a private estate on the cliffs, not a hotel, and that is exactly the point.

Every room is very different, not just a little different. So talk to me about your style preferences and I will get you into the right room. The artwork around the villas is so cool and eclectic, but it really works.

Service was fantastic and warm, and the team is just outstanding. Their suite with the outdoor tub and shower is a standout, along with one of the coolest showers I have ever seen: a rain shower falling from an old brick dome.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Is Villa Treville in the center of Positano?

No. It sits on the cliffs just outside the town, which is a big part of why it feels so private. The hotel runs free boat shuttles into Positano on its own boats.

Which room should I book at Villa Treville?

It depends on your style, because every room is very different, not just a little different. Tell me what you like and I will match you to the right one. The suite with the outdoor tub and shower is a standout.

Does Villa Treville have a beach?

Yes, a beach club on the rocks at the water's edge, with loungers right over the sea.

Does Villa Treville have a spa and gym?

Yes. The spa is in an iron-and-glass garden pavilion that opens to the sea, and the gym looks straight out over the water.

Villa Treville or Il San Pietro?

For me they are the two best options in Positano. Il San Pietro is the classic; Villa Treville is more private, more eclectic, and maybe slightly better value.

Is Villa Treville good for a honeymoon?

Yes. The privacy, the suites and the boats make it one of the most romantic places to stay on the Amalfi Coast.
