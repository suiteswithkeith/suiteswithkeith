The review

A resort and a city in one stay.

The Cipriani has been the answer to “where do we stay in Venice” since 1958, and its advantage is geography. It sits on the tip of Giudecca, across the water from San Marco, with lagoon views on three sides. A complimentary boat has you at the Piazza in about five minutes, and when you come back, the crowds stay on the other side.

That space buys things no hotel in the center can offer. A heated saltwater pool, the largest in Venice. The Casanova Gardens. A clay tennis court framed by hedges and the lagoon, which is why the Cipriani sits at #02 in my hotel tennis ranking. A kitchen garden that supplies the restaurants, a small private marina and a children’s club. It is the Venice hotel for couples and discreet travelers who want to see the city by day and retreat each evening, and the easiest one to recommend for families who want a pool.

The style has always been classic late-1950s Venice: Murano glass, terrazzo floors, a soft, romantic palette, nothing over-modernized. It is also changing. Belmond brought in the architect Peter Marino for a multi-phase renovation. Phase one kept the famous lobby largely as it was, added a light-filled, double-height reception and redesigned 13 rooms. Those include two new signature suites, Serenissima and Laguna, laid out like Venetian apartments with a dining room, library and salon, plus 11 suites and junior suites with balconies over the Casanova Gardens. The spa had long been the one part of the hotel that lagged the rest, and it has been replaced by the first Dior Spa in Venice. Oro, the small Michelin-starred restaurant under the culinary direction of Massimo Bottura, was reimagined too and now serves lunch for the first time.

It is a Belmond flagship and a Fora Reserve property, and I am a preferred Belmond partner, which is how the Bellini Club upgrades and amenities follow you onto the island. I have not stayed at the Cipriani myself yet, so this page sticks to what I know as a Belmond partner. My own notes and photos are coming.

Before you ask

The questions I get

Where is Hotel Cipriani in Venice?

At Giudecca 10, on the tip of Giudecca Island, directly across the water from St Mark’s Square. It is not on the main island, which is the point: gardens, space and quiet that hotels around San Marco cannot offer.

How do you get from Hotel Cipriani to St Mark's Square?

The hotel runs a complimentary shuttle boat to St Mark’s Square, about five minutes across the water. A private launch can also be arranged.

Does Hotel Cipriani have a pool?

Yes. An Olympic-sized, heated outdoor saltwater pool, which Belmond describes as the largest pool in Venice. It is one of the main reasons summer travelers choose the Cipriani, and the atmosphere is calm rather than lively.

Is Hotel Cipriani open year-round?

No. The Cipriani is seasonal and closes for the winter. Belmond currently lists the reopening as 1 June 2027. Summer weeks sell early, so plan well ahead.

How many rooms does Hotel Cipriani have?

Belmond lists 55 rooms, 20 suites and 4 signature suites: Laguna, Serenissima, Palladio and Dogaressa.

What changed in the Peter Marino renovation?

The first phase added a new double-height reception and redesigned 13 rooms, including the Serenissima and Laguna signature suites and 11 suites and junior suites overlooking the Casanova Gardens. The original lobby was largely kept. The first Dior Spa in Venice followed, and Oro now serves lunch.

Is Hotel Cipriani good for families?

Yes, with one caveat. It has the biggest pool in Venice, gardens, a tennis court, a children’s club and suites that sleep up to four. The pool scene is quiet and leans adult, so it suits families with calm kids or older children best.

What perks do I get booking Hotel Cipriani through an advisor?
