The review

A nice Rocco Forte hotel at the top of the Spanish Steps, with a rooftop bar and restaurant, a courtyard and a spa, though I preferred its sister, the de Russie.

The Hotel de la Ville was nice. Honestly, I don’t have a ton to say about it, and I preferred its sister property, the Hotel de Russie.

It’s a Rocco Forte hotel on Via Sistina, right at the top of the Spanish Steps, in an 18th-century palazzo with colorful, very Roman interiors by Tommaso Ziffer. There are 104 rooms and suites, some with terraces and city views.

The highlights are Cielo, the rooftop bar and restaurant across the top two floors with views all over the city, Mosaico on the courtyard, the Julep cocktail bar, and a Sicilian-inspired spa.
