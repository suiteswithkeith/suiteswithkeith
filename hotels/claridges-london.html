Claridge's

The art deco icon and London's definitive grande dame — impeccable service theater, the famous foyer tea, and suites that feel like private Mayfair flats.

1856 Mayfair's Grande Dame  ·  Foyer The Definitive Afternoon Tea

Inquire to book

Every city has a hotel that defines it, and London's is Claridge's. A fixture of Mayfair since 1856 and the art deco masterpiece of the 1920s rebuild, it has hosted every royal family in Europe and never once seemed impressed with itself for it.

What keeps it at the top isn't nostalgia — it's the service, which runs with a warmth and precision that other hotels study. Afternoon tea in the Foyer under the Chihuly chandelier is the version every other tea is measured against, and the rooms manage the trick of feeling like the private flat of a very stylish friend.

For a first serious London trip, a celebration, or anyone who wants the full English hotel experience executed perfectly, this is where I start the conversation.
