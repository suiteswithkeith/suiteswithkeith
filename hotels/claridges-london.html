The review

Mayfair’s grande dame: classic Art Deco below, contemporary suites above, and a spa dug six floors down.

Claridge’s is the grande dame of Mayfair: open since 1856, with roots as a coffee house in 1812, and the building by C.W. Stephens, the architect of Harrods. It has 264 rooms across nine floors.

The hotel is really two hotels in style. Floors one to three are the classic, renovated Art Deco Claridge’s. Floors seven to nine were added during the recent renovation, with sections of the facade craned into place, and they’re contemporary. Decide which you want before you book.

Then there’s the art (a Damien Hirst butterfly piece on the first floor and a Picasso on the fourth), the bars, and the Christmas tree, designed this year with Anya Hindmarch after Burberry last year.

What stood out

The details that made the list

01
Eleven Signature Suites. Each by a different designer: the mirrored Penthouse by yacht designer Rémi Tessier, the Residence by André Fu (who also designed the spa), the Octagon Suite by Pierre-Yves Rochon and the Muse Suite by Bryan O’Sullivan.
02
Dante. The signature restaurant for breakfast, lunch and dinner, with live music and a head bartender from Bar Termini known for his martinis. It began as a pop-up and is now permanent.
03
The bars. A champagne bar by David Collins, who also designed the Connaught Bar; the Fumoir, more intimate and private; and the very pink Painter’s Room.
04
A spa dug by hand. Claridge’s had no spa until about three years ago. They dug six floors beneath the hotel, by hand, so guests wouldn’t hear it. Treatments open with a Japanese-inspired foot ritual, and Josh Wood does hair.
05
Christmas on Brook Street. The facade and the tree are an event every year.
Know before you book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

01
Choose your floor. Floors one to three for classic Art Deco; floors seven to nine for contemporary. I’ll request the right one.
02
The spa is expanding. Construction starts in January to add treatment rooms and a floor with a large pool, hot tub, sauna, steam and a snow shower. Today there’s a small pool, kept at 32°C.
03
Claridge’s or The Connaught? Both are Maybourne and Mayfair. The Connaught is quieter and clubbier; Claridge’s has more buzz.
