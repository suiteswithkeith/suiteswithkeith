The review

The 18th-century landmark on Place de la Concorde, reborn under Rosewood — grand without stuffiness, with one of the city's most beautiful pools.

The Crillon opens the Paris section of my featured page for a reason: the 18th-century landmark on Place de la Concorde, reborn under Rosewood — grand without stuffiness, with one of the city's most beautiful pools. Forbes Five-Star, and in my proposal's words, Paris history reimagined: glamorous, art-filled, and impeccably run, a short stroll from the Tuileries and the Champs-Élysées.

"Grand without stuffiness" is the phrase to hold onto. Rosewood's rebirth kept the landmark bones — the facade has watched over the Concorde since before the Revolution — and layered in an art-filled, contemporary warmth the old palaces rarely manage. The mosaic-lined pool is the city's most beautiful swim.

I'm a preferred partner of Rosewood, and the program here is decisive: a 4th night free plus a confirmed one-category upgrade at booking. Because of that, my proposal advice is to size up from the base room to make the most of the offer.
