Montage Los Cabos

Montage Los Cabos is my go-to first recommendation in Cabo, and the reason starts with the water: it sits on Santa Maria Bay, one of the few genuinely swimmable beaches in Los Cabos — a detail that surprises almost everyone planning their first trip to this coast.

The style is understated design and standout service, which is exactly the combination I want leading a Cabo shortlist. It's also on the short list of properties I've personally worked with and would send a group to tomorrow: Cabo's biggest, most complete resort, with multiple restaurants, a full spa, and enough space to host a large incentive group without anyone feeling on top of each other.

That range is the point. The same property anchors a couple's first Cabo trip, a family week, and a full corporate incentive program — and my partner relationship with Montage travels with every one of those bookings.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Corporate groups, start with me. Room blocks, buyout logistics, and incentive programming here are exactly what my corporate travel practice does.

Whale season is December–April. Humpbacks calve in these waters — I time trips and book the private boat.

My first recommendation in Cabo for couples, families, and companies alike — very few resorts can say that sentence.
