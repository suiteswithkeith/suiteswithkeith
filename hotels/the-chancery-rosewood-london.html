The review

Grand, beautifully designed and international in feel, on Grosvenor Square.

The Chancery is grand, and the design is genuinely beautiful, with striking artwork throughout and the polish you expect from Rosewood.

It’s also the most international-feeling hotel on my London list. It’s sleek and contemporary rather than distinctly British, the kind of hotel that would feel just as at home in New York. For some travelers, that’s exactly the point.

The service lives up to the Rosewood standard, and I fully expect guests have incredible stays here. I’d match it to return visitors who already know London and want something contemporary, and send first-timers to its sister, Rosewood London, for the classic British experience.
