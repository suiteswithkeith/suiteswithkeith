The review

A whole medieval borgo in the Valdarno hills — the most authentic stay I’ve found in Tuscany.

Il Borro is a medieval village on a wooded ridge in the Valdarno hills, restored stone by stone by the Ferragamo family and run as one estate. The lanes, the terracotta roofs and the little church are the real thing — it’s the most authentic Tuscan stay I’ve walked through.

It reminded me of Aman Sveti Stefan in its layout: an entire historic village turned into the hotel, so you’re sleeping inside the borgo rather than looking at it from a resort across the road. That’s rare, and it’s what makes Il Borro so unique.

There are really two ways to stay. If you’re on your honeymoon, stay in the original borgo — that’s the magic. If you’d rather have a more modern room, the estate’s newer accommodations are a two-minute drive away, with the village still right there for dinner and an evening walk.

I toured it today, and it goes straight onto my Tuscany shortlist. Prices are competitive for the level, and it’s a Relais & Châteaux property and part of Fora Reserve, so the perks apply when you book through me.

The honeymoon stay. Rooms inside the original borgo are the ones to book for a honeymoon — atmospheric, private and steps from dinner.

Stunning pools. The pools are gorgeous — including an infinity pool that looks out over the woods to the borgo and the hills. (If the pool is the deciding factor, Borgo Pignano has the best of the three borghi.)

Competitive pricing. For a stay this distinctive, rates are competitive with the other top Tuscan estates — and Fora Reserve perks apply on top.
