The review

Quintessentially British, on Hyde Park Corner, with a personal butler in every room and service that sets it apart.

The Lanesborough is quintessentially British: a Grade I listed Regency building on Hyde Park Corner, renovated in 2017, with 93 rooms, half of them suites. Each of the four floors is designed a little differently, all in the Regency style.

What sets it apart is the service. There are around 360 staff for 93 rooms, and every room category, not just the suites, comes with a personal butler. It’s part of the Oetker Collection, the family-founded group behind Le Bristol in Paris and Hotel du Cap-Eden-Roc, and you feel that same natural, family warmth here.

Across London the rooms are broadly excellent now, so service is what separates the top hotels. Your suite is a great memory; a flawless check-in and a butler who remembers how you take your tea are what make you come back. That’s where The Lanesborough shines.

What stood out

The details that made the list

01
A butler in every room. Every category, from Deluxe up, comes with a personal butler.
02
The Royal Suite. Up to seven bedrooms and around 400 square meters across an entire side of the building. The recently renovated Presidential Suite configures from one to five bedrooms. Both are made for multi-generational trips, and Thanksgiving week books out.
03
The new Pearl rooms. On the top floor, with balconies. It took four years of permissions to turn the windows into doors in a Grade I listed building.
04
Café LPM. The first café from La Petite Maison, launched here as a one-year pop-up, opening onto the terrace in summer.
05
Afternoon tea, and Bridgerton. The tea is a signature, and the hotel is the official Bridgerton partner, a natural fit for a Regency house.
06
The spa and club. A subterranean spa much larger than you’d expect, with sauna, steam and a bio sauna, plus around seven classes a day (reformer Pilates, mat, yoga). Hotel guests become members of the club for their stay, and the class schedule arrives a week before you do.
Know before you book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

01
Choosing your room. Categories run Deluxe, Executive and Premier, each with a larger “B” version, plus the Pearl rooms with balconies. The hotel’s website has 3D walkthroughs of each, and I’ll tell you which to pick.
02
Eat British. Four outlets: The Grill, the club restaurant, the Library Bar and the Withdrawing Room, with British classics like Beef Wellington and Dover sole. The newly renovated cigar lounge isn’t always open to the public.
03
Lanesborough or Claridge’s? Both are quintessentially British. The real difference is location: Hyde Park Corner and Knightsbridge here, Mayfair at Claridge’s.
04
Book holidays early. The large suites go first for Thanksgiving and Christmas.
