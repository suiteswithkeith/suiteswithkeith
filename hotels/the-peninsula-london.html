The review

Overlooking the park, with fun, serious dining and some of the largest penthouses in London.

The location is the first selling point: overlooking the park at Hyde Park Corner.

Then the dining, which is some of the most fun in London, from the venue built around old rally cars to a beautiful Michelin-starred restaurant.

And the penthouses are some of the largest I’ve ever seen, maybe in all of London, with private entrances. It’s very private and great for ultra-VIPs. The design is unmistakably Peninsula, polished and precise.
