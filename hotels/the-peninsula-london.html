The Peninsula London

The newest of London's palaces, opened in 2023 on Hyde Park Corner with some of the largest entry rooms in the city, a rooftop restaurant and a full spa with a pool.

2023 Opening, Hyde Park Corner  ·  Full Spa With Indoor Pool

Inquire to book

The Peninsula London opened in 2023 at Hyde Park Corner, looking across to Wellington Arch, and it's the first ground-up London palace in a generation. Everything that's hard to retrofit into an old building, from room size to soundproofing to the spa, was built in from day one.

The rooms are the headline: entry categories that are bigger than many London suites, with the Peninsula's bedside tablet controlling everything. Brooklands on the rooftop has the best terrace view in Belgravia, and the spa downstairs has a proper pool, which is rarer in central London than it should be.

I book it for guests who want modern comfort without giving up palace service, families who need space, and anyone who has stayed with Peninsula in Paris or Asia and knows the standard.

What Stood Out

The Details That Made The List

The biggest entry rooms in town. You don't have to book a suite to get room to spread out, which changes the math on a London stay.

A real spa. Indoor pool, treatment rooms and a gym that guests actually use. Most London palaces can't match it.

Peninsula service DNA. The same polish as The Peninsula Paris, with flexible check-in and check-out when the hotel can manage it.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

It's new, and feels it. Gorgeous, but no century of history in the walls. Guests who want patina should look at Claridge's or The Lanesborough next door.

A big hotel. It's a busy, social house rather than an intimate one. Courtyard-facing rooms are the quietest.

Book Brooklands early. The rooftop is one of the hottest tables in Belgravia. I reserve it when I book the room.

The modern London palace. When space, a pool and flawless systems matter more than history, this is the pick.
