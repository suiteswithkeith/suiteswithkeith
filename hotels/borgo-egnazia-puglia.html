Borgo Egnazia

Space, food, and privacy — the Puglia entry on my shortlist of honeymoon hotels I'd book anywhere in the world.

#07In The Fifteen — My Honeymoon Shortlist
3Words: Space, Food, And Privacy
Inquire To Book

The Fifteen isn't a ranking — it's my shortlist of honeymoon hotels I'd book anywhere in the world, where every property earns its place for a different kind of couple. Borgo Egnazia is #07, and the three words I gave it there tell you exactly why: space, food, and privacy.

Built as a whitewashed village in the Puglian countryside near the Adriatic, it's the entry on my list for couples who find the classic honeymoon suite claustrophobic. Here you can take a room, a suite, or an entire casetta with its own courtyard — and the property's scale means you're never stacked on top of other guests.

And the food: Puglia is Italy's most exciting eating region right now, and Borgo Egnazia is its flagship table — the reason food-driven couples on my list keep landing here over the more famous coasts.
