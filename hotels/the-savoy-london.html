The review

London’s first purpose-built luxury hotel, freshly refurbished, with river views from bed and the American Bar downstairs.

The Savoy opened in 1889 as London’s first purpose-built luxury hotel, and it’s one of only two Fairmont hotels, with The Plaza in New York, that keeps its own name. For its 135th anniversary it has been completely refurbished, and every room has been sold as new since July 2026.

There are 263 rooms and 64 suites, in two styles: Edwardian, which is every room facing the river, and Art Deco. Half the hotel looks over the Thames, and sightseeing from bed, with the London Eye and Waterloo Bridge out the window, is the reason to book a river view.

It’s also one of the most family-friendly grand hotels in London, with amenities for children and a dedicated kids’ afternoon tea, and it has a bar on the World’s 50 Best list downstairs.

What stood out

The details that made the list

01
The American Bar. A legendary, World’s 50 Best bar with live music from 6:30pm. Princess Diana’s table and Frank Sinatra’s table, with a river view and a sightline to the bar, are still in use. Reserve ahead.
02
The new Grand River View Suite. About 900 square feet, sleeping up to five, with a living room and a guest bathroom. There are only three, from about £4,500 a night.
03
River View Suites with a butler. Butler service is complimentary from the One-Bedroom River View Suite up, and there are 16 of them. The Signature Suites can become two bedrooms, and the Royal Suite is 2,600 square feet.
04
Four restaurants, four bars. The Savoy Grill, open since 1905, for beef Wellington; 1890 for Michelin-starred fine dining, with a dinner-dance on Saturdays; the Gallery for afternoon tea; and the new Bopo wine bar, with more than 100 wines by the glass. Three are run by Gordon Ramsay.
05
Breakfast, your way. À la carte breakfast across the Gallery, the breakfast lounge and the lobby, at no extra charge.
06
A heated daylight pool. On the third floor, open year-round.
