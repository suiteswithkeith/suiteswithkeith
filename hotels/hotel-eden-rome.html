The review

One of my top three stays in all of Italy, with anticipatory service, bright rooms with real views, and a quiet street when the rest of Rome is crowded.

The Eden was one of my top three stays in all of Italy. The service was so anticipatory and warm, the rooms were bright, the restaurant upstairs was fantastic, and the concierge was super helpful with dinner reservations. It was just an all-around fantastic stay, and I loved it there.

The location is a big part of it. At the Hassler, the Hotel de la Ville, the de Russie and even the Bulgari, you step out the door and it’s busy and crowded. The Eden sits on a quiet residential street just above the Spanish Steps, so you’re close to everything and you still come home to calm.

And it’s usually priced slightly below those competitors while delivering what I think is a better product. For me that’s a huge selling point, and it’s why the Eden is now my top overall pick in Rome.
