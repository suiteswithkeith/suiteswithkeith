Naviva, A Four Seasons Resort

Fifteen tented villas hidden in the Punta Mita jungle — adults-only, wellness-driven, and as close to a private nature reserve as Mexico gets.

15Tented Villas, Adults Only
1:1A Personal Guide Shapes Each Stay
Inquire To Book

Naviva is Four Seasons' most intimate experiment anywhere in the world: fifteen luxury tents folded into the jungle on the Punta Mita peninsula, adults-only, with a stay that's shaped around you rather than a resort schedule.

If Mexico's Pacific coast splits into Cabo's drama and Punta Mita's quiet polish, Naviva goes one step further inward — this is the retreat side of the peninsula, built for couples who want the nature, the spa, and the privacy without a big-resort footprint.

I place clients here for honeymoons, milestone anniversaries, and unplug-completely trips — and for second visits to Punta Mita, when the big resorts have already been done.

The Details That Made The List

Fifteen tents, that's it. The smallest Four Seasons anywhere — you'll know the whole team by day two.

Built around you. Each stay is personally guided and shaped to what you want — wellness, adventure, or nothing at all.

The jungle is the amenity. Plunge pools in the canopy, open-air living, and the Pacific through the trees.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Adults only. If the trip includes kids, the St. Regis side of the peninsula is the play — ask me.

It's a retreat, not a scene. No big pool deck, no beach club buzz — that's the point. Pair it with Mandarina if you want both moods in one trip.

Tents are tents — luxurious ones. Open to the jungle's sounds and air. Most couples love it; light sleepers should talk to me about villa placement.

The most personal Four Seasons in the world — and my Punta Mita pick for couples who want to disappear for a week.
