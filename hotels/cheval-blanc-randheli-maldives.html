The review

The most polished service in the atolls — and the only hotel on two of my lists at once.

Cheval Blanc Randheli is the only property that appears on two of my lists at once: #11 on The Fifteen — the honeymoon hotels I'd book anywhere in the world — and #5 on my ranking of the world's most beautiful hotel tennis courts. That overlap tells you what kind of place this is.

On The Fifteen, I describe it simply as the most polished service in the atolls. The Maldives has no shortage of beautiful islands; what separates Randheli is execution — the LVMH-trained precision that turns a beautiful week into a flawless one.

And then there's the court: a cerulean surface cut into the jungle, ringed by palms on a private Maldivian island. Morning tennis, afternoon lagoon — it's a Fora Reserve property, and the rhythm it offers is unlike anywhere else I book.
