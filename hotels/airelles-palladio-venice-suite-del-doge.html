The review

The most spectacular hotel I toured in Venice, and its most spectacular suite.

I toured four of Venice’s top hotels in one day in October 2026: the Four Seasons Danieli, Aman Venice, Orient Express Palazzo Donà Giovannelli and Airelles Palladio. Airelles was the most spectacular of the four. For me it is the number one hotel in Italy right now, and one of the most beautiful properties I have ever seen.

The Suite del Doge is the crown of it. It fills the heart of Villa Frollo, one of the historic buildings on the Airelles estate on Giudecca: 450 square meters, about 4,800 square feet, with four bedrooms, a living room, a kitchen, frescoed walls, soaring ceilings and antiques chosen piece by piece. The salon opens onto a stone balcony with Santa Maria della Salute framed dead center across the water.

What makes it work is the setting. You are five minutes by boat from St. Mark’s, but you come home to gardens, a private arrival and genuine quiet in one of the busiest cities in the world. Private and secure, and yet the hotel itself is lively, social and fun. Sophisticated, never stuffy.

Inside the suite

What you get in 4,800 square feet

Around the suite

Why Airelles Palladio is my number one in Italy

01
The arrival. You come in by water to a private landing on Giudecca, and the reveal of the gardens is one of the great hotel arrivals in Europe.
02
A three-story spa. Spa Airelles by Guerlain is remarkable: three floors, with an indoor pool under timber galleries and brick walls.
03
The outdoor pool and gardens. A long stone-edged pool set in nearly a hectare of walled gardens, with lavender lawns and pergolas. Space that physically does not exist across the water in San Marco.
04
Four restaurants, four bars. Plus a library I could have stayed in all afternoon. In October it was lively but never loud.
05
The worry that never came true. I expected Giudecca to feel isolated. It does not. The hotel boat makes the crossing in about five minutes, and having a calm side of the water to come back to is the luxury.
