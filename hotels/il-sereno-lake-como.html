Il Sereno

Patricia Urquiola's modern lakefront statement — floor-to-ceiling glass, a floating pool, and water-taxi arrivals at your suite.

On my featured Italy page, Il Sereno is the modern counterweight to Como's grande dames: Patricia Urquiola's modern lakefront statement — floor-to-ceiling glass, a floating pool, and water-taxi arrivals at your suite. In my Lake Como proposal, its category is simply "Modern Luxury."

It's the most modern luxury stay on the lake: sleek architecture, glass opening straight to the water, and interiors built for design lovers. Every suite faces the lake head-on from the Torno shoreline, and arriving by water taxi at your own hotel is the kind of detail that defines the whole stay.

In my proposal I show two ways in: the Garden Lake View Suite — the larger room, around 860 square feet, which sees the lake just not head-on — and the Grand Suite Lago, which trades a little size for a private terrace and a direct, front-and-center lake view. If the view is everything, go Grand Suite; if space and value matter more, the Garden suite is hard to beat.

What Stood Out

The Details That Made The List

The architecture. Urquiola's glass-and-stone statement — the design-lover's answer to a lake of historic villas.

The floating pool. Suspended at the water's edge — swimming level with the lake itself.

Water-taxi arrivals. The dock is the front door — Bellagio, Como, and the villa circuit all by boat.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Choose your suite by view versus space. Grand Suite Lago for the head-on view and private terrace; Garden Lake View for the square footage — my Lake Como proposal breaks down both.

Modern is the point. If your Como dream is frescoes and history, that's Villa d'Este or Passalacqua — this is the lake for design lovers.

Watch for suite offers. Il Sereno runs genuine promotions on its larger suites more often than its neighbors — I time bookings to catch them.

The most modern luxury stay on the lake — for design lovers, Il Sereno isn't the alternative to classic Como; it's the reason to go.
