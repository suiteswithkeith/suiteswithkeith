The review

The grand, nature-rich flagship in Baa Atoll — a marine research centre on-site, and Hanifaru Bay's mantas a short boat ride away.

Landaa Giraavaru is the grand finale of the Four Seasons Collection in my sample Maldives proposal — the nature-rich flagship in Baa Atoll, home to a marine research centre and a short boat ride from Hanifaru Bay, one of the world's great manta and whale-shark sites.

The arrival is part of the show: a complimentary scenic seaplane from sister property Kuda Huraa, coordinated seamlessly by the Four Seasons team — roughly $1,000 of value for two, and the reason I call this pairing the most complete, worry-free Four Seasons experience.

For couples who want their honeymoon to include the ocean's biggest wildlife moments — mantas by the hundred in season — this is the island I point to first.
