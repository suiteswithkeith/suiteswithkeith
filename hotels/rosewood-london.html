The review

Classic, old-luxury London with big rooms, a World’s 50 Best bar and a lovely high tea. My pick for a first trip.

Rosewood felt like classic, old-luxury London: a grand Edwardian building with more than 300 rooms, and rooms and suites that are large for the city.

It’s a great base for families and business travelers, and the easiest recommendation I can make for a first trip to London. The one-bedroom suite is massive.

You don’t need to leave to eat and drink well, either: there’s a beautiful bistro-style restaurant, a bar on the World’s 50 Best list, and a lovely high tea.
