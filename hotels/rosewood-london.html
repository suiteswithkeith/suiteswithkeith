Rosewood London

A 1914 Edwardian landmark in Holborn, entered through a grand carriage courtyard, with Scarfes Bar and a residential-feeling calm between Covent Garden and the City.

1914 Edwardian Landmark  ·  Scarfes Bar & Holborn Dining Room

Inquire to book

Rosewood London occupies the former Pearl Assurance headquarters on High Holborn, a 1914 Belle Epoque building you enter through a cobbled carriage courtyard. The moment you pass the gates, the traffic disappears.

Inside, it's Rosewood's residential style at its best: generous rooms, a grand marble staircase, and two of the most-loved rooms in London for drinks and dinner. Scarfes Bar, hung with Gerald Scarfe's caricatures, is the best fireside martini in town, and the Holborn Dining Room is famous for its pies.

I book it for guests who want a grand hotel with a lived-in, unstuffy feel, and an address that walks to Covent Garden, the British Museum, Soho's theatres and the City.

Know Before You Book

The Fine Print, From Your Advisor

Holborn isn't Mayfair. It's a working, central neighborhood rather than a postcard one. Guests who want Bond Street at the door should look at Claridge's.

Room categories matter. Courtyard-facing rooms have the view; I'll request them for a first visit.

Reserve Scarfes. It fills up on weekend evenings. I'll book a table for your first night.

London's most welcoming grand hotel. Edwardian scale, Rosewood warmth, and the best bar in the city.
