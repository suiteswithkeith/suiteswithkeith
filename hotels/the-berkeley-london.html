The review

The contemporary Maybourne, with more buzz than its sisters and The Emory’s wellness next door.

The Berkeley has more buzz than its sister hotels, and it plays the contemporary one next to Claridge’s and The Connaught. It’s bigger than it looks from the entrance, with 190 rooms and suites in a C-shaped building.

Every textile was recently refreshed, and the room designs vary across the hotel. The first-floor balcony and terrace rooms are the ones I’d book: consistent in design, with warm wood and felt walls, somewhere between a Japanese listening bar and a 1960s or ’70s café.

Guests can also use the four floors of wellness at The Emory next door.
