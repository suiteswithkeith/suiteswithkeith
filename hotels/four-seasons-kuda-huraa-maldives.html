Kuda Huraa opens the Four Seasons Collection — the classic-luxury itinerary in my sample Maldives proposal, and my easiest recommendation for first-time visitors. In the proposal I call it the polished, family-run classic close to Malé, and that closeness matters: a 25-minute speedboat and you're on the island.

The real magic of this itinerary is what comes next. Because Kuda Huraa and Landaa Giraavaru sit under one brand, the inter-resort seaplane between them is complimentary — roughly $1,000 of value for two — and the entire ten-night journey is handled by a single team from arrival to departure.

If you'd rather not think about a single logistic, this is it — my exact take from the proposal. For a first Maldives honeymoon, the seamlessness is worth the premium.
